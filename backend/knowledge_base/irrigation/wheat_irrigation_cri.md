# Wheat Irrigation Stages & CRI Timing (गेहूं में सिंचाई की क्रांतिक अवस्थाएं)

## The 6 Critical Irrigation Stages of Wheat
Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations during its lifecycle depending on soil type and winter rainfall. The stages in order of priority are:
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI / ताज मूल या शिखर जड़ बनने की अवस्था)**: 20 to 25 days after sowing (DAS). **MOST CRITICAL STAGE**.
2. **Tillering Stage (कल्ले फूटने की अवस्था)**: 40 to 45 DAS.
3. **Late Jointing Stage (गांठें बनने की अवस्था)**: 60 to 65 DAS.
4. **Flowering / Heading Stage (फूल आने की अवस्था)**: 80 to 85 DAS.
5. **Milking Stage (दुग्ध अवस्था)**: 100 to 105 DAS.
6. **Dough Stage (दाना पकने / सख्त होने की अवस्था)**: 115 to 120 DAS.

## When Should the First Irrigation (CRI) Be Done? (पहली सिंचाई कब करें?)
- **Exact Timing**: The first irrigation must be applied strictly between **21 to 25 days after sowing** (अगेती बुवाई में 21 दिन पर और सामान्य बुवाई में 21 से 25 दिन पर).
- **Scientific Significance**: At this stage, crown roots (permanent root system) initiate just below the soil surface. 
  - If moisture is deficient at CRI, crown root formation is severely inhibited, secondary tillers fail to emerge, and final grain yield drops by **25% to 35%**, an irrecoverable loss.
- **Irrigation Depth**: The first irrigation should be light (5 to 6 cm depth). Heavy flood irrigation at CRI causes standing water, suffocates young roots, and causes temporary yellowing of leaves.
- **Top Dressing with First Irrigation**: Broadcast the first split of Urea (approx. 45-50 kg/acre) right after irrigation when the soil reaches field capacity (wattar / वत्तर स्थिति).

## Water Scheduling with Limited Water Supply
- If only **1 irrigation** is available: Apply strictly at **CRI stage** (21-25 DAS).
- If only **2 irrigations** are available: Apply at **CRI** (21 DAS) and **Flowering stage** (80-85 DAS).
- If only **3 irrigations** are available: Apply at **CRI** (21 DAS), **Jointing** (60 DAS), and **Milking stage** (100 DAS).
