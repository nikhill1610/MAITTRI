# Micro-Irrigation: Drip & Sprinkler Systems and PMKSY Subsidy Guide (सूक्ष्म सिंचाई: ड्रिप, स्प्रिंकलर एवं पीएमकेएसवाई अनुदान)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Traditional flood irrigation (*खुली क्यारी विधि*) squanders between 55% and 65% of pumped groundwater through conveyance percolation, deep leaching, and surface evaporation. In water-scarce regions of Uttar Pradesh—especially Bundelkhand, South-Western semi-arid zones, and undulating terrains—transitioning to **Micro-Irrigation (Drip and Sprinkler Systems)** achieves **water use efficiency of 90% to 95% in Drip and 75% to 85% in Sprinklers**. Coupled with **Fertigation (*ड्रिप द्वारा खाद देना*)**, farmers save 30% to 40% on fertilizer costs while boosting commercial crop yields by 20% to 40%. Under the Central and State Government's **PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)** scheme, small and marginal farmers receive **up to 55% capital subsidy** on certified micro-irrigation installations.

## Comparison: Flood vs. Sprinkler vs. Drip Irrigation
| Performance Metric | Traditional Flood / Furrow | Sprinkler Irrigation System | Drip Irrigation System |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Water Use Efficiency (WUE)**| **35% – 45%** | **75% – 85%** | **90% – 95%** |
| **Fertilizer Savings** | 0% (High leaching losses) | 10% – 15% savings | **30% – 40% savings via Fertigation** |
| **Weed Infestation** | High (Entire plot surface wetted) | Moderate | **Extremely Low** (Only root zone wetted) |
| **Best Suited Crops** | Paddy, Low-value fodder | Wheat, Mustard, Gram, Pulses | **Potato, Tomato, Vegetables, Sugarcane** |
| **Energy / Pumping Reduction**| 0% | 20% – 30% diesel/power savings | **40% – 50% power savings** |
| **Terrain Adaptability** | Strictly flat, leveled land | **Undulating, sloping, sandy soils**| Leveled or graded rows |

## 1. Drip Irrigation & Fertigation Mechanics

### Core System Components
1. **Control Head**: Pump unit, Non-Return Valve, Backflow Preventer, and Pressure Gauge.
2. **Filtration System (The Heart of Drip)**:
   - *Hydrocyclone / Sand Media Filter*: Removes heavy sand particles from tube-well water.
   - *Disc / Screen Filter (120 mesh / 130 microns)*: Catches fine organic silt and algae before water enters laterals.
3. **Fertilizer Injector (Venturi Assembly)**:
   - Uses pressure differential vacuum to suction dissolved liquid fertilizer directly into the main irrigation line.
4. **Distribution Laterals & Emitters (Drippers)**:
   - 12 mm or 16 mm UV-stabilized LLDPE lateral pipes laid along crop rows.
   - In-line drippers spaced at 20 cm, 30 cm, 40 cm, or 50 cm discharging 1.0 to 4.0 litres of water per hour directly at the root zone.

### The Power of Fertigation
- **Water Soluble Fertilizers (WSF)**:
  - Apply 100% water-soluble grades such as **19:19:19 (NPK)**, **12:61:0 (MAP)**, **0:52:34 (MKP)**, and **13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate)**.
  - Granular fertilizers like DAP should never be dissolved in drip tanks (fillers and insolubles clog drippers).
- **Fertigation Advantages**: Nutrients are supplied in small, micro-doses directly to the active root hair zone, cutting nitrogen leaching to zero and boosting nutrient uptake efficiency by 40%.

## 2. Sprinkler Irrigation: Portable & Micro-Sprinklers
- **Portable Aluminum / HDPE Sprinklers**:
  - Quick-coupling pipes with rotating brass/plastic impact nozzles discharging water under 2.0 to 3.5 kg/cm² pressure in a circular pattern (12–18 meter diameter).
  - Perfect for closely spaced winter crops: **Mustard, Wheat, and Chickpea** in sandy soils where furrow flooding causes massive seepage losses.
- **Frost Mitigation Advantage**:
  - Running sprinklers for 30–45 minutes during sub-zero cold wave nights blankets the crop with latent heat released as water freezes on leaf surfaces, saving potato and mustard from frost damage.

## 3. PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (PDMC) Subsidy Norms in Uttar Pradesh

### Subsidy Slabs
Under the operational directives of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and UP Agriculture Department:
- **Small & Marginal Farmers (Holding up to 2 Hectares / 5 Acres)**:
  - Eligible for **55% Capital Subsidy** on total system cost (45% farmer share, funded via bank loan or cash).
- **Other / General Farmers (Holding >2 Hectares)**:
  - Eligible for **45% Capital Subsidy** on total system cost (55% farmer share).

### Mandatory Documents & Application Workflow
1. **Application Portal**: Apply online at the official UP Agriculture portal (**`upagriculture.com`**) under the "Micro Irrigation / Drip-Sprinkler Subsidy" tab.
2. **Required Documents**:
   - Land Ownership Records (Khatauni / *खतौनी*).
   - Valid Aadhaar Card and Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook.
   - Electricity bill or proof of tube-well / water source availability.
   - Small/Marginal Farmer Certificate (*लघु/सीमांत कृषक प्रमाण पत्र*).
3. **Approved Vendor Procurement**: Select from registered empaneled micro-irrigation manufacturers (Jain Irrigation, Netafim, EPC Mahindra, Finolex).
4. **Physical Verification & DBT**: Agriculture department engineers physically inspect the installed system in the farmer's field; the subsidy amount is credited directly to the farmer's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) or adjusted with the manufacturer.

## Maintenance: Preventing Dripper Clogging & Acid Treatment
- **Daily Filter Flushing**: Open the flush valves on disc and screen filters after every irrigation run to wash away accumulated silt.
- **Bi-Weekly Sub-Main Flushing**: Open the end caps of lateral pipes for 2 minutes while the pump is running to blow out fine silt settled in line ends.
- **Acid Treatment (*एसिड शोधन*) for Hard Water Carbonate Clogging**:
  - In tube-well areas with high water hardness (bicarbonates and calcium), dripper orifices clog with white mineral crusts.
  - Inject **Commercial Hydrochloric Acid (HCl 33%)** or **Phosphoric Acid** through the Venturi at a concentration of 0.5% (5 ml acid per litre of water) to lower line water pH to 4.0.
  - Turn off the system and allow acid water to stand inside laterals for 24 hours to dissolve calcium scale. Flush lines with clean water before next irrigation.

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Drip sinchai lagane par kitna kharcha aata hai aur kitni subsidy milti hai? (Cost and subsidy on drip irrigation?)**  
  *A*: For vegetables or potato, standard drip costs approx. ₹50,000 to ₹65,000 per acre. For small and marginal farmers (<5 acres), the government provides up to 55% subsidy, meaning the farmer pays only around ₹22,000 to ₹29,000 per acre.
- **Q: Drip me sadharan DAP ya Urea gholkar daal sakte hain? (Can normal DAP or Urea be dissolved in drip?)**  
  *A*: Neem-coated Urea can be dissolved after straining, but **never dissolve normal DAP** in drip systems. DAP contains insoluble clay fillers that instantly choke drippers and filters. Always use 100% Water Soluble Fertilizers (like 19:19:19 or 12:61:0) for fertigation.
