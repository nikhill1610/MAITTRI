# Micro-Irrigation: Drip and Sprinkler Systems (ड्रिप एवं फव्वारा सिंचाई प्रणाली)

## Benefits of Micro-Irrigation
Micro-irrigation achieves 85-95% water-use efficiency compared to only 35-45% in conventional flood/furrow irrigation.
- **Drip Irrigation (टपक सिंचाई)**:
  - Delivers water directly to the plant root zone drop-by-drop through emitters at low pressure (1-2 kg/cm²).
  - Best suited for wide-row crops and vegetables (Tomato, Potato, Cotton, Sugarcane, Orchards).
  - Enables **Fertigation**: applying soluble fertilizers (19:19:19, 13:0:45) directly through the irrigation water, reducing fertilizer waste by 30-40%.
- **Sprinkler Irrigation (फव्वारा सिंचाई)**:
  - Sprays water like artificial rain through rotating nozzles at pressure of 2-3 kg/cm².
  - Best suited for closely spaced crops (Wheat, Mustard, Gram, Groundnut) and undulating, sandy terrain where flood irrigation causes excessive seepage losses.
  - Mitigates light frost damage in winter crops by maintaining surface temperature during freezing nights.

## Government Subsidy Support
Under the **PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)** scheme:
- Small & Marginal Farmers (< 2 hectares): Eligible for **55% financial subsidy** on total system cost.
- Other/Large Farmers: Eligible for **45% financial subsidy**.
- Several states (e.g., Gujarat, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra) provide additional top-up subsidies up to 70-85%.
