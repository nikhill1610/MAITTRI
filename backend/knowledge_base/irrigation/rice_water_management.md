# Rice Water Management & Irrigation Frequency (धान में जल प्रबंधन एवं सिंचाई)

## Irrigation Frequency: How Often Should You Irrigate Rice?
Rice water requirements vary across growth stages:
1. **Transplanting to Early Rooting (Days 1 to 10 after transplanting)**:
   - Maintain a shallow standing water depth of **2 to 3 cm**. This prevents seedling dehydration, supports anchoring, and suppresses early weed germination.
2. **Tillering to Panicle Initiation (Vegetative Phase)**:
   - Practice **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD / कम पानी में सिंचाई)**: You do NOT need continuous standing water.
   - Frequency: Irrigate every **3 to 5 days** (in clay loam) or **2 to 3 days** (in light sandy soils), allowing the standing water to naturally disappear and hair-line cracks to just begin forming on the surface before re-flooding with 3-5 cm water.
   - AWD saves 25-30% irrigation water without any reduction in grain yield and reduces greenhouse methane emissions.
3. **Flowering & Booting Stage (Reproductive Phase - CRITICAL)**:
   - Maintain continuous standing water depth of **3 to 5 cm** from panicle emergence until milk stage. Water stress at flowering causes severe spikelet sterility and empty chaffy panicles.
4. **Ripening Phase (15-20 days before harvest)**:
   - Stop irrigation completely 10 to 15 days before harvest to allow uniform grain drying and ease mechanical combine harvesting.

## Key Irrigation Rules for Paddy
- Continuous deep submergence (>10 cm) is harmful: it reduces tillering, lowers soil temperature, and induces root rot.
- In Direct Seeded Rice (DSR / सीधी बिजाई), avoid standing water for the first 15 days to prevent crusting and seedling rot; give light, frequent irrigations at 4-7 day intervals.
