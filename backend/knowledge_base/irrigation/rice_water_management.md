# Rice Water Management & Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) Guide (धान में जल प्रबंधन एवं एडब्ल्यूडी विधि)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Rice (*धान*) is the most water-intensive crop grown in Uttar Pradesh, consuming an average of 3,000 to 5,000 litres of water to produce a single kilogram of milled grain. Traditional farmer practice demands maintaining 5 to 10 cm of continuous stagnant flood water throughout the entire 120–140 days of the crop. Agronomic science led by ICAR-NRRI and IRRI has proven that continuous ponding is **completely unnecessary for 60% of the season**. By adopting **Safe Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD / *वैकल्पिक गीला और सूखा तकनीक*)**, farmers can **save 25% to 30% of irrigation diesel/electricity costs**, prevent toxic root rot and lodging, curb sheath blight spread, and drastically reduce greenhouse methane ($CH_4$) emissions with **zero reduction in grain yield**.

## Stage-by-Stage Water Scheduling Framework
| Crop Stage | Timing (DAT) | Recommended Water Depth | Agronomic Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seedling Establishment** | 1 to 10 DAT | **2 to 3 cm continuous shallow ponding** | Anchors transplanted roots; prevents seedling dehydration and suppresses early weed emergence. |
| **Vegetative Tillering** | 11 DAT to Panicle Initiation (approx. 45 DAT) | **Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)**: Allow water level to recede until 15 cm below soil surface before re-flooding to 5 cm depth. | Aerates soil, stimulates deep root growth, prevents toxic sulfide accumulation, saves 3–5 tube-well pumping cycles. |
| **Reproductive Phase (Panicle Initiation to Flowering)** | 45 DAT to 75 DAT | **3 to 5 cm continuous ponding (CRITICAL)** | **STRICTLY NO AWD ALLOWED**. Even 3 days of moisture stress during flowering causes pollen sterility and empty chaffy panicles. |
| **Milking & Grain Filling** | 75 DAT to 95 DAT | Saturated to shallow 2 cm water | Ensures plump, heavy, starch-dense grains. |
| **Pre-Harvest Drainage** | 10 to 12 days before harvest | **Complete field drainage (*खेत का पानी सूखा देना*)** | Hardens soil for combine harvesters; induces uniform ripening; prevents post-harvest fungal infection. |

## The Safe AWD Technology: Field Water Tube Fabrication & Use

### 1. Tube Specifications & Fabrication
- Use a discarded plastic/PVC drainage pipe or bamboo cylinder:
  - Length: **30 cm**.
  - Diameter: **10 to 15 cm**.
- Drill multiple small holes (0.5 cm diameter, spaced 2 cm apart) across the **lower 20 cm** of the pipe.
- Leave the **top 10 cm unperforated** (no holes).

### 2. Installation in the Field
- Install the tube in a flat, representative part of the paddy field 10 to 12 days after transplanting.
- Push the tube vertically into the puddled soil until the lower 20 cm (perforated section) is submerged underground and the top 10 cm protrudes above the mud line.
- Scoop out the mud from inside the tube so the bottom is clear and open.
- The water level inside the pipe will naturally reflect the true water table level of the field.

### 3. The 15 cm Re-Irrigation Trigger ("Safe AWD")
- After flooding the field to 5 cm depth, allow the water to naturally infiltrate and evaporate.
- Check the water tube daily. The water level will drop below the soil surface inside the tube.
- **The Threshold**: As long as water is visible inside the tube within 15 cm below the soil surface, the soil root zone remains 100% saturated. **Do not irrigate**.
- When the water level inside the pipe drops **below 15 cm from the soil surface**, start the tube-well and re-flood the field to 5 cm depth.

## Agronomic & Economic Benefits of Safe AWD
1. **Economic Savings**: Eliminates 3 to 5 tube-well pumping runs, saving ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 per acre in diesel and electricity costs.
2. **Superior Root Anchorage**: Roots are forced to penetrate 20 to 30 cm deeper to seek moisture, anchoring the plant firmly and preventing lodging during late monsoon storms.
3. **Pest & Disease Suppression**: Lowering canopy humidity reduces the severity of **Sheath Blight (*Rhizoctonia*)** and drives away sap-sucking **Brown Plant Hoppers (BPH)**, which thrive in stagnant, warm water.
4. **Climate Protection**: Dry aerobic soil phases break anaerobic methanogenesis, slashing agricultural methane ($CH_4$) emissions by 30% to 50%.

## Farmer Decision Table
| Crop Phase | Water Level inside AWD Tube | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| Active tillering (30 DAT) | Water is 8 cm below soil line inside pipe; hair-line surface cracks visible | **Do not irrigate**. Soil root zone remains at full field capacity. |
| Active tillering (35 DAT) | Water level drops 15 cm below soil surface inside pipe | Re-flood field to 5 cm depth immediately. |
| Panicle initiation / Flowering stage | Any level | **Suspend AWD**. Maintain 3 to 5 cm standing water continuously until flowering ends. |
| 15 days before combine harvest | Standing water in furrows | Cut field bunds and drain all water out of the field completely. |

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Kya dhaan ke khet me lagatar pani bhara rakhna zaroori hai? (Is continuous standing water necessary?)**  
  *A*: NO. Continuous standing water is only required during the first 10 days after transplanting (for seedling survival) and during flowering (45–75 DAT). During the tillering stage, allowing the water to recede to 15 cm below the soil surface (AWD) saves water with zero yield penalty.
- **Q: AWD pipe khet me kaha lagani chahiye? (Where should the AWD pipe be installed?)**  
  *A*: Place the pipe near the middle of the plot, at least 5 meters away from the field bund and water inlet, on average-level ground.
