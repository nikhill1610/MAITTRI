# Inland Freshwater Aquaculture: Composite Carp Culture & Water Quality Guide (अंतर्देशीय मत्स्य पालन: मिश्रित कार्प पालन एवं जल प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Freshwater fish farming (*मत्स्य पालन*) under the **Composite Carp Culture (*मिश्रित कार्प पालन*)** system is one of the most profitable agricultural diversification avenues in Uttar Pradesh. By stocking compatible ecological fish species together in the same earthen pond—combining native **Indian Major Carps (Catla, Rohu, Mrigal)** with complementary exotic carps (Grass Carp, Common Carp)—farmers utilize 100% of available pond ecological niches (surface, column, and bottom layers) without inter-species food competition. A scientifically managed 1-hectare pond produces **4,000 to 5,000 kg of table fish annually**, generating net profits of ₹3.5 Lakh to ₹5.5 Lakh. Sustaining high productivity requires rigorous **water quality monitoring (especially Dissolved Oxygen)**, controlled liming, supplementary floating feed, and prophylactic health maintenance against **Epizootic Ulcerative Syndrome (EUS)**.

---

## The Composite Polyculture Principle & Species Ratios

To maximize productivity, stock fast-growing fingerlings across three complementary water layers:

| Ecological Niche | Compatible Fish Species | Feeding Habit & Natural Diet | Standard 3-Species Combination (%) | Standard 5-Species Combination (%) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **Surface Layer (*सतह पर रहने वाली*)** | **Catla (*कतला*)** & Silver Carp | Feeds on zooplankton and phytoplankton floating in top 0.5 m of water; fastest grower (reaches 1.2–1.5 kg in 10 months). | **35% to 40%** | Catla: 20%<br>Silver Carp: 15% |
| **Column Layer (*मध्यम जल में रहने वाली*)** | **Rohu (*रोहू / जयंती रोहू*)** | Feeds on filamentous algae, plant detritus, and submerged micro-vegetation in mid-water depths (0.5 to 1.5 m). | **30% to 35%** | Rohu: 30%<br>Grass Carp: 10% (Controls submerged hydrilla weeds) |
| **Bottom Layer (*तलहटी पर रहने वाली*)** | **Mrigal (*मृगल / नैन*)** & Common Carp | Bottom feeder; sifts through decaying organic detritus, benthic insects, and soft mud. | **30%** | Mrigal: 15%<br>Common Carp: 10% |

## Pre-Stocking Pond Preparation Protocol

Neglecting pond sterilization allows weed fish and aquatic predatory insects to consume up to 70% of introduced baby fingerlings:

1. **Eradication of Weed & Predatory Fish**:
   - Dewater and sun-dry the pond bottom until deep cracks appear (10–15 days).
   - If draining is impossible, apply **Bleaching Powder (Chlorine 35%) @ 350 kg/ha-meter** OR **Mahua Oil Cake (*महुआ की खली*) @ 2,000 to 2,500 kg/ha-meter**. Mahua cake contains saponin which asphyxiates weed fish within 6 hours, then decomposes after 14 days into rich organic fertilizer.
2. **Pond Liming (*तालाब में चूना डालना*)**:
   - Apply **Agricultural Lime / Quicklime (CaO) @ 250 to 400 kg/ha** dissolved in water buckets and broadcast evenly over the water surface.
   - *Functions*: Neutralizes soil acidity, buffers water pH at 7.5–8.2, clarifies suspended silt, and kills fungal spores.
3. **Organic Manuring & Natural Plankton Bloom**:
   - 7 days after liming, apply **Raw Cow Dung @ 5,000 to 8,000 kg/ha** + **Single Super Phosphate (SSP @ 50 kg/ha)**.
   - Within 10–12 days, a dense natural bloom of microscopic food (*zooplankton and phytoplankton*) develops, turning the water an ideal rich light green or light brown color.

## Stocking Density & Fingerling Standards

- **Stocking Material**: Never stock tiny fry or spawn directly into production ponds. Stock certified, active **Fingerlings aged 8 to 10 cm (minimum 15–20 g weight)**.
- **Stocking Density**: **5,000 to 7,500 fingerlings per hectare** (approx. 2,000 to 3,000 fingerlings per acre).
- **Stocking Season**: **July to August** (during active monsoon rains when water temperature is 26–30°C).
- **Acclimatization (*तापमान संतुलन*)**: Float fingerling transport polybags in the pond water for **20 to 30 minutes** before opening. Slowly mix pond water into the bag before releasing fingerlings to prevent thermal shock.

## Critical Water Quality Parameters & Emergency Management

Water quality is the invisible health regulator in aquaculture. Test parameters weekly:

| Water Parameter | Ideal Safe Range | Danger Threshold | Clinical Signs of Stress & Remedial Action |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** | **5.0 to 8.0 mg/L (ppm)** | **$< 3.0 \text{ mg/L}$** | **Fish gasping at surface at dawn with open mouths (*सतह पर आकर मुंह चलाना*)**. Emergency: Run paddle-wheel aerators; pump fresh borewell water; splash water vigorously with bamboo poles; apply Hydrogen Peroxide tablets. |
| **Water pH** | **7.5 to 8.5** | $< 6.5\text{ (Acidic)}$ or $> 9.5\text{ (Alkaline)}$ | Below 7.0: Apply quicklime @ 50 kg/ha; Above 9.0: Apply gypsum or alum. |
| **Transparency (Plankton)** | **30 to 40 cm** (Measured by Secchi Disc) | $< 20\text{ cm}$ (Algal bloom) or $> 50\text{ cm}$ (Clear water/Starvation) | If $<20\text{ cm}$: Stop all feeding and manuring immediately (risk of night-time oxygen crash). If $>50\text{ cm}$: Apply cow dung + SSP to stimulate plankton. |
| **Total Ammonia ($NH_3$)** | $< 0.1 \text{ mg/L}$ | $> 0.5 \text{ mg/L}$ | Toxic to fish gills. Stop feeding; exchange 25% pond water with fresh tube-well water. |

## Supplementary Feeding Schedule

Natural plankton supports only 1.5 tonnes of fish per hectare. Achieving 4 to 5 tonnes/ha requires supplementary feeding:
- **Pelleted Floating Feed (Recommended)**: Use extruded floating pellets with **28% to 32% crude protein**. Floating pellets ensure zero feed wastage at the pond bottom and allow farmers to observe fish appetite visually.
- **Feeding Rate**: Feed twice daily (morning 8:00 AM and afternoon 4:00 PM) at **2% to 3% of total standing fish biomass daily**.
- **Alternative Farm Mash**: Mix **50% Mustard Oil Cake + 50% De-Oiled Rice Bran (DORB)** into dough balls placed in feeding trays submerged 30 cm below water level.

## Fish Disease Management: Epizootic Ulcerative Syndrome (EUS)

- **Causal Factor**: Fungus *Aphanomyces invadans* triggered during sudden winter cold drops (December–January) when water temperature falls below 20°C.
- **Symptoms**: Small red pimples on skin that expand into deep, bleeding, crater-like rotting ulcers. Fish become sluggish and float near banks.
- **Therapeutic Intervention (ICAR-CIFA Standard)**:
  - Apply **CIFAX** (patented therapeutic formulation developed by ICAR-CIFA) @ **1.0 Liter per hectare-meter of water**. Dilute in 100 liters of water and spray evenly across the pond surface. One application completely arrests EUS within 7 days.
  - Prophylactic winter prevention: Apply **Potassium Permanganate ($KMnO_4$) @ 2.5 kg/ha-m** or quicklime @ 100 kg/ha in November.

## Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) Subsidies

- **Scheme Directives**: Funded by Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, GoI:
  - Construction of new earthen fish ponds (1 hectare unit cost benchmark: ₹7.0 Lakh).
  - Input cost subsidy for 1st year fish farming (Unit cost: ₹4.5 Lakh).
  - **Subsidy Rate**:
    - **60% financial assistance** for Women farmers, SC, and ST beneficiaries.
    - **40% financial assistance** for General category farmers.
- Applications submitted online through UP Fisheries Department portal (*fisheries.up.gov.in*).
