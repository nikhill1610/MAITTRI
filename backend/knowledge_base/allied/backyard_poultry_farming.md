# Backyard & Smallholder Poultry Farming: Improved Breeds, Brooding & Vaccination Guide (ग्रामीण मुर्गी पालन: उन्नत नस्लें, ब्रूडिंग एवं टीकाकरण)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Rural poultry farming (*ग्रामीण कुक्कुट पालन*) is a rapid-turnaround enterprise providing rural families with high-protein food and daily cash income. Traditional non-descript native (*Desi*) chickens produce only 50–60 small eggs annually and take 7–8 months to reach 1 kg body weight. By adopting **scientifically improved dual-purpose backyard breeds** developed by ICAR-CARI (Bareilly) and ICAR-DPR—such as **Kadaknath (*कड़कनाथ*)**, **Vanaraja (*वनराजा*)**, and **CARI Nirbheek (*कारी निर्भीक*)**—farmers harvest **140 to 180 eggs per bird annually** and achieve 1.5 to 2.0 kg body weight in just 10–12 weeks. High profitability requires three non-negotiables: scientific **artificial brooding (*तापमान प्रबंधन*)** during the first 4 weeks, **low-cost on-farm feed compounding**, and strict prevention of the lethal **Ranikhet Disease (*रानीखेत रोग*)**.

## Day-Old Chick Brooding Management (*चूजों का ब्रूडिंग प्रबंधन*)

Chicks cannot regulate their own body temperature for the first **21 to 28 days** of life. Chilling during brooding is responsible for 80% of early chick mortality:

1. **Litter Bed Preparation**:
   - Spread a **5 to 7 cm (2–3 inches) layer of dry rice husk (*धान की भूसी*)** or clean wood shavings on the floor. Cover the litter with brown Kraft paper for the first 3 days to prevent chicks from mistaking litter for feed.
2. **Hover Brooder & Temperature Schedule**:
   - Hang a metal hover or canopy equipped with **100-watt incandescent bulbs or infrared heating lamps** 30–45 cm above the floor.
   - Temperature guidelines:
     - **Week 1**: **33°C to 35°C (95°F)**.
     - **Week 2**: **30°C to 32°C (90°F)**.
     - **Week 3**: **27°C to 29°C (85°F)**.
     - **Week 4**: **24°C to 26°C (80°F)** (Wean off artificial heat thereafter unless winter frost occurs).
3. **Chick Behavior as a Thermometer**:
   - *Too Cold*: Chicks crowd and huddle directly beneath the heat bulb, crying loudly $\rightarrow$ Lower the bulb or add heat.
   - *Too Hot*: Chicks scatter against the outer brooder guard ring with open wings and panting $\rightarrow$ Raise the bulb.
   - *Drafty Wind*: Chicks bunch together in one corner $\rightarrow$ Block drafts.
   - *Ideal Comfort*: Chicks distribute evenly across the entire brooder area, eating and drinking quietly.
4. **First Drink & Feed**:
   - Provide clean lukewarm water containing **5% jaggery (*गुड़*) + Electrolytes (Oral Rehydration Salts)** for the first 24 hours to rehydrate exhausted chicks after transit.

## Low-Cost On-Farm Feed Compounding

Commercial branded poultry feed is expensive. Farmers can compound balanced mash on the farm using local agricultural grains:

### Standard 100 kg Grower / Backyard Supplement Feed Formula:
- **Crushed Yellow Maize (*मक्का दर्रा*)**: **50 kg** (High metabolizable energy).
- **Soybean Meal (*सोयाबीन खली*)** or Mustard cake (after water debittering): **20 kg** (Crude protein 44%).
- **De-Oiled Rice Bran (DORB / *चावल की पॉलिश*)**: **25 kg** (B-vitamins and fiber).
- **Mineral Mixture & Limestone Powder / Shell Grit (*चूना/सीप पाउडर*)**: **3.5 kg** (Calcium for strong bones and eggshells).
- **Common Salt**: **0.5 kg**.
- **Vitamin Premix ($A, D_3, E, K$) + Trace Minerals**: **1 kg**.

## Mandatory Avian Disease Vaccination Calendar

Avian viral epidemics (especially Ranikhet) have a **100% mortality rate**. Vaccination is the only reliable shield:

| Bird Age | Target Disease | Vaccine Name & Strain | Route of Administration | Protection & Clinical Signs if Unvaccinated |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Day 1** (At Hatchery) | **Marek's Disease (*मारेक्स*)** | HVT Live Vaccine | Subcutaneous injection at neck base | Paralysis of legs and wings, enlarged feather follicles. |
| **Day 5 to 7** | **Ranikhet Disease (*रानीखेत / ND*)** | **F1 / Lasota Strain** | **One drop in one eye or nostril (*आंख/नाक में बूंद*)** | High fever, gasping, greenish diarrhea, twisting of neck (*टोर्टिकोलिस*), 90–100% mortality. |
| **Day 14** | **Gumboro Disease (*गंबोरो / IBD*)** | IBD Intermediate Strain (Georgia) | **Drinking water** with skimmed milk powder stabilizer | Severe immunosuppression, trembling, soiled whitish watery vent. |
| **Day 24** | **Gumboro Booster** | IBD Intermediate Plus | Drinking water | Secures permanent immune response against field virus. |
| **Week 6 to 8** | **Fowl Pox (*माता रोग*)** | Fowl Pox Live Vaccine | **Wing-web puncture (*पंख की झिल्ली में छेद करके*)** | Warty eruptive scabs on comb, wattles, and around eyes. |
| **Week 8 to 10** | **Ranikhet Booster** | **R2B / Mukteswar Strain** | **0.5 ml Subcutaneous injection (*चमड़ी के नीचे टीका*)** | Provides solid lifetime protection for adult birds. (Do not give to chicks under 6 weeks). |

## Night Shelters & Predator Protection

In rural village settings, mongooses (*नेवला*), stray dogs, cats, and snakes kill more birds than diseases:
- Construct low-cost **predator-proof night shelters** elevated **60 cm (2 feet) off the ground** on wooden or bamboo stilts.
- Seal shelter walls with **0.5-inch GI hexagonal wire netting (*जाली*)**.
- Ensure dry perch poles (*बसेरा*) inside the shed so birds can roost naturally at night.
