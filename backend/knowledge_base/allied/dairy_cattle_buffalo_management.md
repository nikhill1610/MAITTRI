# Commercial Dairy Farming: Murrah Buffalo, Sahiwal Cattle, TMR Ration & Health Guide (व्यावसायिक डेयरी फार्मिंग: मुर्रा भैंस, साहीवाल गाय एवं पशु स्वास्थ्य)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Dairy farming is the primary source of daily agricultural cash flow across Uttar Pradesh, with the state ranking as one of India's largest milk producers. Profitability hinges on managing high-genetic-merit dairy animals—principally the **Murrah Buffalo (*मुर्रा भैंस*)** (yielding 14–20 liters/day with 7.0–8.0% butterfat) and elite indigenous **Sahiwal Cattle (*साहीवाल गाय*)** (yielding 12–16 liters/day of A2 milk). Transforming smallholder cattle keeping into a profitable commercial enterprise requires four foundational pillars: **Total Mixed Ration (TMR)** feeding with green fodder silage, strict screening against sub-clinical **Mastitis (*थनैला रोग*)**, mandatory preventative **vaccination (FMD, HS, BQ)**, and reproductive management via **Sex-Sorted Artificial Insemination**.

## Scientific Nutrition: Total Mixed Ration (TMR) Architecture

Traditional unscientific feeding (feeding dry straw and commercial pellets separately) causes acute rumen pH fluctuations and low milk fat. Modern commercial dairies feed a homogeneous **Total Mixed Ration (TMR)**:

### 1. Daily Dry Matter (DM) Requirement:
- Cattle: **2.5 to 3.0 kg Dry Matter per 100 kg body weight**.
- Buffaloes: **3.0 to 3.2 kg Dry Matter per 100 kg body weight**. (A 500 kg Murrah requires approx. 15–16 kg DM daily).

### 2. Standard TMR Composition Ratio (Dry Matter Basis):
- **Roughage to Concentrate Ratio**: **60 : 40** (or 55 : 45 for high yielders producing >18 L/day).
  - *Dry Roughage*: Wheat straw (*भूसा*) or chopped paddy straw (30–35% of diet).
  - *Green Fodder / Silage*: Berseem, Lucerne, Hybrid Napier, or Maize Silage (25–30% DM basis).
  - *Concentrate Mash*: 40% (Crushed grains: Maize/Barley 35%, Mustard/Soybean cake 32%, Wheat bran 30%, Mineral mix 2%, Salt 1%).

### 3. High-Yield Additives (Per Animal Daily):
- **Chelated Mineral Mixture**: **50 g daily** mixed in feed (prevents silent heat, repeat breeding, and retained placenta).
- **Rumen-Bypass Fat (*बाईपास फैट*)**: **100 to 150 g daily** during peak lactation (first 90 days post-calving) to prevent negative energy balance and ketone buildup.
- **Sodium Bicarbonate (Baking Soda - *मीठा सोडा*)**: **30 to 50 g daily** in the ration to buffer rumen pH at 6.2–6.8 and prevent sub-acute ruminal acidosis (SARA).

## Clean Milk Production & Mastitis (*थनैला रोग*) Prevention

Mastitis is the costliest disease in dairying, destroying glandular udder quarters. Over **80% of mastitis is "Sub-Clinical"** (invisible to the eye, but somatic cell count skyrockets and milk yield drops by 25%):

1. **Daily Strip Cup Test (*स्ट्रिप कप जांच*)**:
   - Strip the first 2 squirts of milk from each teat onto a black surface strip cup prior to milking. Look for flakes, clots, or watery discoloration. Never strip first milk onto the floor (spreads bacteria).
2. **California Mastitis Test (CMT - *सीएमटी जांच*)**:
   - Perform CMT paddle testing monthly across all lactating animals to detect hidden sub-clinical mastitis early before udder swelling occurs.
3. **Pre-Milking & Post-Milking Teat Disinfection (*टीट डिप*)**:
   - **Post-Milking Teat Dip (Crucial)**: Immediately after milking, dip all 4 teats into a teat dip cup containing **0.5% Povidone Iodine solution**.
   - *The Teat Sphincter Rule*: The teat sphincter muscle remains open for **30 to 45 minutes after milking**. Dipping seals the canal with an antiseptic barrier.
   - **Keep cows standing for 1 hour after milking** by providing fresh green fodder in the feeding trough; never allow freshly milked cows to lie down in wet dung or mud.

## Annual Mandatory Vaccination Calendar (Uttar Pradesh)

Preventative vaccination is legally mandated under the National Animal Disease Control Programme (NADCP):

| Target Disease | Vaccine Type | Mandatory Timing | Booster & Frequency | Clinical Signs if Infected |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD / *खुरपका-मुंहपका*)** | Inactivated Trivalent FMD Vaccine | **May** (Pre-monsoon) and **November** (Pre-winter) | **Biannual** (Every 6 months) for all animals above 4 months age. | High fever, ropy salivation, vesicles and painful sores on tongue, dental pad, and interdigital cleft of feet. |
| **Hemorrhagic Septicemia (HS / *गलघोंटू*)** | Alum Precipitated / Oil Adjuvant HS Vaccine | **May – June** (Strictly before monsoon arrival) | **Annual** (Before rainy season). | Acute high fever (106°F), severe painful swelling of throat and neck, grunting breathing, death within 24 hours. |
| **Black Quarter (BQ / *लंगड़ा बुखार*)** | BQ Polyvalent Anaculture Vaccine | **May – June** (Combined with HS or given separately) | **Annual** (Targeting young stock 6 months to 2 years). | Crepitating hot swelling on upper hindquarters, lameness, muscle necrosis. |
| **Brucellosis (*ब्रुसेलोसिस*)** | S19 Brucella Live Vaccine | Any month | **Once in a lifetime** strictly for **female heifer calves aged 4 to 8 months**. | Late-term abortions (7th–9th month) and retained placenta. (Zoonotic disease transmitted to humans!). |

## Housing & Summer Heat Stress Alleviation

Murrah buffaloes possess black skin and 1/6th the sweat gland density of zebu cattle, making them extraordinarily vulnerable to summer heat stress (>35°C), which shuts down milk synthesis and causes "Silent Heat":
1. **Comfort Architecture**: Minimum covered floor space: **3.5 to 4.0 sq. meters per cow**, plus 7.0 sq. meters open paddock. Roof height must be at least 3.5 to 4.0 meters at ridge with asbestos or insulated panels (never uninsulated tin sheets).
2. **Cooling Systems**:
   - Install **overhead circulating fans paired with misting nozzles / sprinklers** operating in 3-minute cycles every 15 minutes during summer noon.
   - Buffalo wallowing: Allow 1 hour wallowing in clean pond water or give 3 water spray baths daily in summer.
