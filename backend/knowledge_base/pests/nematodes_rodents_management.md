# Root-Knot Nematodes & Field Rodent Integrated Management Guide (सूत्रकृमि एवं खेत के चूहे नियंत्रण मार्गदर्शिका)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Subterranean pests—specifically microscopic **Root-Knot Nematodes (*सूत्रकृमि / गांठ रोग / Meloidogyne incognita*)** in vegetables and vertebrate **Field Rodents (*खेत के चूहे / Bandicota bengalensis*)** in grain and sugarcane systems—cause severe hidden losses of 20% to 50% across Uttar Pradesh. Nematodes inject secretions into plant root cells, causing roots to swell into giant galls that choke nutrient and water transport while opening entry wounds for lethal *Fusarium* wilt fungi. Field rats cut standing tillers at a 45-degree angle, burrow extensive tunnel labyrinths, and breach canal bunds. Controlling both problems requires strictly disciplined, non-chemical and chemical integrated protocols.

---

## PART 1: Root-Knot Nematode Management (*सूत्रकृमि प्रबंधन*)

### Diagnostic Differential: Nematode Root Galls vs Rhizobium Nodules
Farmers often mistakenly pull out healthy leguminous plants thinking root nodules are parasitic galls:

| Characteristic | Parasitic Nematode Root Galls (*सूत्रकृमि गांठ*) | Beneficial Rhizobium Legume Nodules (*राइजोबियम ग्रंथियां*) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Affected Crops** | Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli, Okra, Cucurbits, Potato, Carrot | Strictly Legumes (Gram, Pea, Lentil, Arhar, Soybean, Moong) |
| **Physical Attachment** | **Integral swelling of the root cylinder itself**; cannot be detached without tearing the root cortex. | **Side appendages attached by a thin fragile neck**; pops off effortlessly between thumb and fingernail. |
| **Internal Color** | Uniform translucent white or brown pith; zero pink pigmentation. | **Rich pink or red interior (*लेगहीमोग्लोबिन*)** indicating active nitrogen fixation. |
| **Foliage Health** | Stunted, pale yellow chlorotic leaves, mid-day wilting despite moist soil. | Deep green, vigorous vegetative foliage. |

---

### Non-Chemical & Biological Management of Nematodes

1. **Deep Summer Plowing & Soil Solarization (*मृदा सौरीकरण*)**:
   - Plow vegetable fields in May–June. Cover moist soil with **25-micron transparent polyethylene film for 4 to 6 weeks**. Soil temperatures beneath the plastic exceed 50–52°C, pasteurizing the top 15 cm of soil and killing 90% of nematode juveniles (*J2*) and egg masses.
2. **Neem Cake Enrichment (*नीम की खली*)**:
   - Apply well-powdered **Neem Cake @ 250–500 kg/ha** during final land preparation or 50 g per vegetable transplanting pit.
   - Neem cake releases bioactive *azadirachtin*, *nimbin*, and fatty acids upon decomposition that inhibit nematode egg hatching and juvenile motility.
3. **Biological Nematicide - *Purpureocillium lilacinum* (formerly *Paecilomyces lilacinus*)**:
   - *Purpureocillium lilacinum* is a specialized egg-parasitic fungus whose hyphae penetrate and destroy nematode egg shells and sedentary adult females.
   - *Dosage*: Mix **5.0 kg *P. lilacinum* 1% WP in 100 kg moist FYM/vermicompost**; incubate in shade for 7 days, then broadcast over 1 hectare before transplanting.
4. **Nematicidal Crop Rotation / Trap Cropping with African Marigold (*गेंदा*)**:
   - Intercrop or rotate with **African Marigold (*Tagetes erecta*)** (1 row of marigold every 4–5 rows of solanaceous vegetables).
   - Marigold roots exude **$\alpha$-terthienyl**, a potent natural allelochemical that immobilizes and kills soil nematodes within a 30 cm radius.

---

### Chemical Nematicide Intervention

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.
> Old carbamate/organophosphate nematicides (Phorate 10G, Carbofuran 3G) carry severe groundwater toxicity hazards and heavy regulatory restrictions.

- **Authoritative Modern Formulations**:
  - **Fluopyram 34.48% SC** @ **500 to 625 ml/ha (1.0 to 1.25 ml/L)** applied through drip irrigation or seedling drenching at 15–20 DAT.
  - Nursery Soil Treatment: Drench vegetable nursery beds with **Trichoderma harzianum + P. lilacinum** @ 10 g/sq.m.

## PART 2: Field Rodent Community Control (*खेत में चूहा नियंत्रण*)

Field rats—predominantly the **Lesser Bandicoot Rat (*Bandicota bengalensis*)** and **Soft-Furred Field Rat (*Millardia meltada*)**—are acute neophobic animals (naturally terrified of new food objects). **Throwing poisoned bait randomly in a field causes "Bait Shyness" (*चारा झिझक*)**, where rats eat a sublethal dose, fall sick, recover, and permanently refuse to touch the poison again.

Successful rodent extermination requires the **Strict 4-Day Community Baiting Protocol**:

---

### The 4-Day Scientific Burrow Baiting Protocol

```
Day 1: Burrow Mapping & Plugging (Close all holes with mud)
Day 2: Live Hole Identification & Pre-Baiting (Place plain grain bait)
Day 3: Poison Baiting (Replace with Zinc Phosphide 2% poisoned bait)
Day 4: Post-Bait Evaluation (Collect/bury dead rats; re-plug burrows)
```

#### Step 1 (Day 1 - Identification):
- In the morning, walk field bunds, canal banks, and sugarcane borders.
- **Plug every visible rat hole with loose mud**.

#### Step 2 (Day 2 - Pre-Baiting / सादा चारा खिलाना):
- Inspect all plugged holes the next morning. Holes that have been reopened are **Active Live Burrows (*सक्रिय बिल*)**. Ignore unopened holes.
- Place **10–15 g of Plain Pre-Bait** inside each reopened active burrow opening (6–8 inches deep).
  - *Pre-Bait Recipe*: **48 parts cracked wheat/millet grain + 48 parts roasted gram powder + 2 parts mustard oil + 2 parts powdered sugar/jaggery**.
  - This conditions the resident rat family to associate the feed with absolute safety.

#### Step 3 (Day 3 - Poison Baiting / विषैला चारा देना):
- Prepare the lethal bait using **Zinc Phosphide 2% Technical**:
  - *Poison Bait Recipe*: **48 parts cracked grain + 48 parts roasted flour + 2 parts mustard oil + 2 parts Zinc Phosphide (2%)**.
  - *Mixing Safety*: Always wear rubber gloves and a face mask. **Never use bare hands**; human scent on the bait repels rats.
  - Insert **10 g of poisoned bait** wrapped in a paper twist deep into each active hole. Plug the burrow mouth lightly with a turf of grass so livestock or birds cannot access it.

#### Step 4 (Day 4 - Collection, Carcass Disposal & Evaluation):
- Early in the morning, inspect treated burrows.
- **Collect all dead rat carcasses using tongs or gloves, and bury them at least 1 meter deep in the ground** to prevent secondary poisoning of scavengers, stray dogs, cats, and birds of prey (owls/hawks).
- Re-plug all burrows with mud.

---

### Maintenance & Chronic Anticoagulant Baiting
- For ongoing control in godowns, farm houses, or crop edges without acute poison hazard:
- Use **Bromadiolone 0.005% Ready-to-Use (RTU) Wax Cakes**.
  - Place 1 or 2 wax blocks (20 g each) inside bait stations or near runway corners.
  - Bromadiolone is a multi-dose second-generation anticoagulant that causes painless internal hemorrhage over 3 to 5 days without triggering bait shyness.

### Rodent Poison Safety & Antidote Information:
- **Zinc Phosphide**: Releases lethal phosphine gas ($PH_3$) upon contact with stomach gastric acid. There is **no specific chemical antidote**. Treatment is strictly symptomatic: gastric lavage with 0.1% potassium permanganate ($KMnO_4$) solution by a qualified medical practitioner.
- **Bromadiolone Antidote**: **Vitamin $K_1$ (Phytomenadione)** administered intravenously under emergency hospital supervision.
