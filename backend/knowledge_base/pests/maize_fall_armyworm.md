# Maize Fall Armyworm Management (मक्का में फॉल आर्मीवर्म कीट नियंत्रण)

## Symptoms & Identification
Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda / एफएडब्ल्यू) is an invasive voracious caterpillar attacking maize from seedling to cob stage.
- Characteristic Identification Marks:
  1. Inverted 'Y' shaped white mark on the head of mature larvae.
  2. Four dark dots arranged in a square on the 8th abdominal segment.
- Damage Pattern:
  - Young larvae scrape green leaf tissue, leaving silvery, papery "window panes" on leaves.
  - Older caterpillars bore inside the central leaf whorl (पत्तियों की गोभ/पोंगा), feeding heavily on developing tissues and filling the whorl with coarse, moist brown frass (मल/कीड़े की विष्ठा).
  - Ragged shot-holes and shredded leaf blades appear as leaves unroll.

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
1. Monitoring: Install pheromone traps @ 5 traps/acre to detect early adult moth flight.
2. Cultural & Mechanical: Handpick egg masses and caterpillars in early stages; apply dry sand, wood ash, or soil into central leaf whorls to suffocate young larvae.
3. Bio-control: Spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) kurstaki @ 2g/litre or Metarhizium rileyi @ 5g/litre in early larval stages.
4. Chemical Advisory (If ETL >10% damaged plants during whorl stage):
   - Whorl Application: Apply spray directly into the central plant whorl using a nozzle with coarse droplets.
   - Recommended formulations: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/litre (80 ml/acre in 200L water) or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/litre (80g/acre).
