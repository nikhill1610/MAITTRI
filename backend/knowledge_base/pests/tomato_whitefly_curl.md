# Tomato Whitefly & Leaf Curl Virus (टमाटर की सफेद मक्खी एवं पर्ण कुंचन रोग)

## Vector & Viral Disease Dynamics
- Vector Insect: Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci / सफेद मक्खी), tiny white winged sap-sucking insects swarming under the lower leaf surface.
- Viral Disease: Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV / पर्ण कुंचन या मरोड़िया रोग), transmitted within minutes of feeding by whiteflies.
- Symptoms:
  - Severe curling of leaves inward and upward, puckering, reduction in leaf lamina size, and thickened prominent veins.
  - Plants become bushy and stunted with shortened internodes.
  - Complete drop of flowers, resulting in zero fruit set or small deformed fruits.

## Integrated Management Strategy
1. Barrier Crops: Grow 2-3 border rows of tall maize or sorghum 15 days before tomato transplanting to act as a physical windbreak and barrier against flying whiteflies.
2. Protected Nursery: Raise tomato seedlings under 40-mesh nylon insect-proof net tunnels.
3. Yellow Sticky Traps: Install yellow sticky cards @ 12-15 traps/acre at canopy level.
4. Chemical / Insecticide Spray:
   - Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/litre, OR
   - Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1.25 ml/litre, OR
   - Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.4 g/litre.
   - Remove and destroy severely infected virus-stunted plants immediately to prevent whiteflies from spreading virus to healthy plants.
