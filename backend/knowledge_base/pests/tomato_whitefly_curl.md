# Whitefly & Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) Management Guide (टमाटर की सफेद मक्खी एवं पत्ता मरोड़ रोग)

## Farmer-friendly summary
The Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci / सफेद मक्खी*) is the single most dangerous insect pest of tomato in Uttar Pradesh, not merely because of the sap it sucks, but because it acts as the airborne biological vector for the devastating **Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV / *टमाटर का पत्ता मरोड़ / कुकड़ा रोग*)**. A single whitefly feeding for just 15 minutes can transmit the virus into a young tomato plant. Once inside the plant, **there is NO chemical cure for the virus**; leaves curl upwards into tight cupped leathery clusters, plants become severely dwarfed (*बौने पौधे*), flowers abort, and fruit yield drops by 80% to 100%. Managing leaf curl therefore requires strict **vector suppression**: starting from 40-mesh insect net nursery covers, planting ToLCV-resistant hybrids, erecting border barrier crops of tall maize, deploying yellow sticky traps, and applying targeted insecticides against whiteflies.

## When this guidance applies
- **High-Risk Geography**: All tomato growing zones of Uttar Pradesh, especially during the warm, dry autumn months (August to October) and spring/summer months (March to May).
- **Critical Vulnerability Window**: Nursery stage and the first 30 days after transplanting (early infection causes 100% loss; late infection after fruit set permits partial harvest).
- **Environmental Triggers**:
  - Warm temperatures (28°C to 35°C) and dry weather.
  - Mild winds that carry whiteflies into fields from neighboring cucurbits or cotton crops.

## Identification & Diagnostic Symptoms
1. **The Whitefly Insect (*Bemisia tabaci*)**:
   - Minute (1.0 to 1.5 mm long), pure chalk-white insects with soft bodies and yellowish heads.
   - Rest exclusively on the **underside of young leaves**.
   - Shaking a tomato branch causes hundreds of tiny white "snowflakes" to flutter into the air before quickly settling back under the leaves.
2. **Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) Symptoms on Plants**:
   - **Upward Curling**: Margins of young leaves roll and curl **upwards (*ऊपर की ओर मुड़ना*)**, forming boat-shaped or cup-shaped leaves.
   - **Leaf Thickening**: Leaflets become small, puckered, thick, brittle, and leathery, with prominent yellow chlorotic interveinal clearing.
   - **Stunting & Rosetting**: Internodes shorten drastically, transforming the tomato plant into a stunted, bushy rosette (*झाड़ीनुमा बौना पौधा*).
   - **Floral Abortion**: Flower buds drop off before opening; any small fruits that set remain hard, small, mottled, and unmarketable.

## Lookalike Discrimination: Whitefly vs. Aphid vs. Mite vs. Herbicide Damage
| Diagnostic Marker | Whitefly + ToLCV (*पत्ता मरोड़*) | Aphid Infestation (*माहू*) | Broad Mite Infestation (*माइट*) | 2,4-D Herbicide Drift |

## Recommended Action & IPM Management Protocols

### 1. Nursery Protection (Most Critical Stage)
- **40-Mesh Insect Proof Net Cover (MANDATORY)**:
  - Over 70% of ToLCV infections happen while seedlings are still in the nursery.
  - Cover the entire nursery bed with a 40-mesh white nylon insect net supported on bamboo arches. Bury the net borders under soil to prevent whiteflies from entering.
- **Seed Treatment**:
  - Treat tomato seed with **Imidacloprid 70 WS** @ 5.0 g/kg seed to provide systemic protection for 15 days following germination.

### 2. Varietal Resistance (The Ultimate Genetic Shield)
- Plant certified ToLCV-resistant varieties developed by ICAR-IIVR Varanasi:
  - **Kashi Vishesh (DVRT-2)**: High-yielding semi-determinate hybrid bred with *Ty-gene* resistance to Leaf Curl.
  - **Kashi Amrit**: Tolerant to leaf curl with firm transportable fruits.

### 3. Cultural & Agro-Ecological Barriers
- **Tall Barrier Crops (*जैविक अवरोधक फसलें*)**:
  - Plant 2 to 3 dense border rows of tall **Maize (*मक्का*) or Sorghum (*ज्वार*)** around the tomato perimeter 3 weeks prior to tomato transplanting.
  - *Why Effective*: The 2-meter tall vegetative maize wall physically filters out and blocks wind-borne whitefly swarms from landing in the tomato field.
- **Yellow Sticky Traps (*पीले चिपचिपे प्रपंच*)**:
  - Install bright **Yellow Sticky Traps** @ 10 to 12 traps per acre, positioned 15–20 cm above the crop canopy.
  - *Why Effective*: Whiteflies are strongly attracted phototactically to bright yellow wavelength; traps capture thousands of adult flies daily, breaking breeding cycles.
- **Roguing (*संक्रमित पौधों को उखाड़ना*)**:
  - During the first 30 days after transplanting, inspect fields every 3 days. Uproot and bury any plant showing initial leaf curl symptoms in a deep pit. Leaving an infected plant in the field turns it into an active virus reservoir for surrounding plants.

### 4. Chemical Control Protocol for Whiteflies (CIBRC Verified)
Because you cannot spray a chemical to cure the virus inside a plant, insecticidal sprays must be directed at killing the **whitefly insect vector**:
- **Spray Timing**: Spray early in the morning before 8:30 AM or in late afternoon when adult flies are resting on the underside of leaves. The spray nozzle must be angled **upwards** to wet the leaf underside.
- **Recommended Insecticides**:
  1. **Diafenthiuron 50% WP (Pegasus)** @ 1.25 g per litre of water (250 g in 200 L water per acre). *(Superior translaminar action killing both nymphs and adults).*
  2. **Pyriproxyfen 10% EC (Lano)** @ 1.5 ml per litre of water. *(Insect Growth Regulator that sterilizes adult female whiteflies and prevents nymphal molting).*
  3. **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC (Oberon)** @ 1.0 ml per litre of water.
  4. **Thiamethoxam 25% WG** @ 0.3 g per litre of water.

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Tamatar me patta modak rog aane ke baad kya koi dawa dalne se paudha theek ho sakta hai? (Can any spray cure a tomato plant already infected with leaf curl?)**  
  *A*: NO. Leaf curl is caused by a virus (ToLCV). Once the virus enters the plant cells, no chemical or tonic can cure it. All chemical sprays target the **whitefly insect** to stop it from infecting healthy neighboring plants. Immediately uproot and destroy the first few infected plants.
- **Q: Tamatar ke charo taraf makka lagane se safed makkhi kaise rukti hai? (How does border maize block whiteflies?)**  
  *A*: Whiteflies are weak fliers transported by wind currents at heights below 1.5 meters. Planting 2–3 dense rows of tall maize around the tomato field acts as a living physical filter that traps and deflects incoming whitefly swarms.
