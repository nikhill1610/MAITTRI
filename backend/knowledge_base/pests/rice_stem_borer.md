# Rice Stem Borer & Leaf Folder Management (धान का तना छेदक व पत्ती लपेटक)

## Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas / तना छेदक)
Yellow stem borer is the primary internal feeding insect pest in paddy.
- Vegetative Stage Symptom - "Dead Heart" (डेड हार्ट): Caterpillar bores into central tiller and cuts growing point, causing central shoot to dry, turn brown, and easily pull out with an unpleasant smell at the severed base.
- Reproductive Stage Symptom - "White Earhead" (सफेद बाली): Caterpillar cuts the panicle peduncle during flowering, causing panicles to emerge completely bleached white, erect, and devoid of grain (chaffy).

## Leaf Folder (Cnaphalocrocis medinalis / पत्ती लपेटक)
- Symptoms: Larva folds the leaf longitudinal edges together with silk threads and feeds by scraping green chlorophyll from inside the fold, creating prominent longitudinal white papery streaks.

## Integrated Pest Management
1. Clip seedling leaf tips before transplanting to eliminate stem borer egg masses.
2. Install Pheromone Traps @ 8-10 traps/acre for adult monitoring and mass trapping.
3. Release Trichogramma japonicum egg parasitoids @ 40,000/acre at weekly intervals.
4. Chemical Measures (At Economic Threshold Level - ETL: 5% dead hearts or 1 egg mass/sq.m):
   - Cartap Hydrochloride 4% G @ 7.5 to 10 kg/acre broadcasted in standing water, OR
   - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC foliar spray @ 0.3 ml/litre (60 ml/acre in 200L water), OR
   - Fipronil 5% SC @ 2 ml/litre.
