# Chickpea Pod Borer Management (चना में फली छेदक कीट नियंत्रण)

## Damage Pattern & Identification
Gram Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera / चना की सुंडी या फली छेदक) is the most destructive pest of pulses.
- Symptoms:
  - Young larvae feed on tender leaves, shoots, and flower buds.
  - Mature caterpillar (greenish to brownish with broken longitudinal stripes) cuts a neat circular hole in developing pods, thrusting its head inside to devour the seeds while keeping the rest of its body outside.
  - A single caterpillar can destroy 30 to 40 pods during its lifecycle.

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
1. Intercropping: Grow Chickpea with Mustard or Coriander (4:1 or 6:1 ratio) to deter egg laying and encourage natural predators.
2. Bird Perches (T-shaped bamboo perches): Install 15-20 bird perches per acre to attract insectivorous birds like king crows (Drongo) and mynas. Remove perches at flowering stage.
3. Pheromone Traps: Install 5-6 traps per acre using Helilure septa for monitoring moth activity.
4. HaNPV (Bio-virus): Spray Helicoverpa Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus (HaNPV) @ 250 LE/ha or Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 1.5 kg/ha on early instar larvae.
5. Chemical Measures (At ETL: 1 larva per meter row):
   - Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/litre (60 ml/acre) OR Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/litre (80 g/acre) in 200 litres of water.
