# Mustard Aphid Management (सरसों में माहू / चेपा कीट नियंत्रण)

## Symptoms & Damage Pattern
Mustard Aphid (Lipaphis erysimi / माहू, चेपा, मोयला) is the most destructive pest of rapeseed-mustard in India, appearing from late December to February during cloudy, foggy weather.
- Nature of Damage: Millions of tiny greenish-yellow soft-bodied insects cluster in dense colonies on tender shoots, inflorescences, leaves, and developing green pods.
- Symptoms:
  - Both nymphs and adults suck plant sap continuously, stunting vegetative growth, curling tender leaves, and turning flower buds dry and sterile.
  - Aphids excrete sticky sweet honeydew (चिपचिपा मीठा द्रव) on foliage, leading to secondary growth of black sooty mould fungus (काली फफूंद), which impairs plant photosynthesis.
  - Pods fail to fill, and seeds remain shriveled with reduced oil content.

## Eco-Friendly & Integrated Management (IPM)
1. Timely Sowing: Complete sowing before October 25. Early-sown crops flower before peak aphid multiplication in January.
2. Balanced Fertilizer: Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer doses, which produce lush soft foliage favored by aphids.
3. Yellow Sticky Traps: Install bright yellow sticky traps @ 10-12 traps per acre to trap flying alate aphids.
4. Conservation of Natural Enemies: Ladybird beetles (कोक्सीनेलिड्स / Coccinella septempunctata) and Syrphid fly larvae are voracious predators of aphids. Avoid early toxic broad-spectrum sprays.
5. Bio-Pesticide: Spray Neem Oil (Azadirachtin 0.03% or 10,000 ppm) @ 3-5 ml/litre on early colonies.
6. Chemical Control (When ETL >25 aphids per 10 cm terminal shoot on 10% plants):
   - Spray Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 ml/litre (300 ml/acre) OR Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/litre (60 ml/acre).
   - Spray preferably in the late afternoon (after 3 PM) to avoid harming foraging honeybees pollinating the mustard flowers.
