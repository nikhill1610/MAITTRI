# Wheat Crop Production Guide (गेहूं की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Wheat (*गेहूं*) is India's premier Rabi cereal crop. Achieving high grain yields (20–25 quintals per acre / 50–60 q/ha) in Uttar Pradesh and the North Indian plains requires five critical practices: sowing timely (November 1–15) using certified rust-resistant varieties (HD-3086, DBW-187, DBW-222), applying the first irrigation strictly at the Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage (20–25 days after sowing) with light water, balancing fertilizers using the 120:60:40 NPK formula with split urea application, and scouting early for yellow rust and Phalaris minor (gulli danda).

## When this guidance applies
- **Agro-climatic Zones**: North Western Plains Zone (Western UP, Haryana, Punjab) and North Eastern Plains Zone (Central & Eastern UP, Bihar).
- **Season**: Rabi season.
- **Sowing Window**: Timely irrigated: November 1 to November 15; Late sown: November 25 to December 15.
- **Soil Conditions**: Well-drained fertile loam or clay loam, pH 6.0 to 7.5.

## Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Optimal Temperatures**:
  - Seed Germination: 20°C to 25°C.
  - Active Tillering: 16°C to 20°C (cool weather promotes tillers).
  - Grain Filling & Ripening: 22°C to 25°C (temperatures above 32°C during anthesis cause terminal heat stress).
- **Soil Preferences**: Deep, well-drained loam or clay-loam soils with good water-holding capacity. Avoid waterlogged or saline-alkali soils without prior gypsum/drainage amendments.

## Seed Rate, Seed Treatment & Sowing Spacing
- **Seed Rate**:
  - Timely Sown: 100 kg/ha (40 kg/acre).
  - Late Sown (after Nov 25): Increase to 125 kg/ha (50 kg/acre) to compensate for reduced tillering.
- **Certified Seed Treatment**:
  - *Fungicide Treatment*: Treat seed before sowing with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.5–3.0 g/kg seed to prevent Loose Smut and Flag Smut.
  - *Biofertilizer Treatment*: Inoculate seed with *Azotobacter* and Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) culture @ 200 g each per 10 kg seed in jaggery water; shade dry for 30 minutes before drilling.
- **Spacing & Depth**:
  - Row-to-row spacing: 20–22.5 cm (timely sown); 18 cm (late sown).
  - Depth of sowing: Strictly 4–5 cm. Deep sowing (>6 cm) delays seedling emergence and reduces crown root vigor.

## Balanced Nutrient Management (NPK + Zinc)
For high-yielding irrigated wheat under medium fertility soils, the recommended nutrient dose is **120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare** (approx. 48 kg N : 24 kg P2O5 : 16 kg K2O per acre).

### Commercial Fertilizer Conversion & Timing
1. **Basal Application (at sowing)**:
   - Full dose of Phosphorus: DAP @ 130 kg/ha (52 kg/acre) **OR** Single Super Phosphate (SSP) @ 375 kg/ha (150 kg/acre).
   - Full dose of Potash: MOP @ 67 kg/ha (27 kg/acre).
   - One-third (1/3rd) Nitrogen: Urea @ 70 kg/ha (28 kg/acre). *(Note: If DAP is used, subtract the 23.5 kg N supplied by DAP from the basal urea dose).*
   - Zinc Sulphate: Apply 25 kg/ha (10 kg/acre) Zinc Sulphate (21% Zn) as basal in zinc-deficient soils. Never mix zinc directly with DAP in the fertilizer drill.
2. **First Top Dressing (at CRI stage, 20–25 DAS)**:
   - Apply 1/3rd Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre) just before or immediately after the 1st irrigation.
3. **Second Top Dressing (at Jointing/Tillering stage, 45–50 DAS)**:
   - Apply remaining 1/3rd Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre) with the 2nd irrigation. Complete all urea top dressing before booting/heading.

## Irrigation Scheduling & Critical Growth Stages
Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations depending on winter rains and soil texture. Never allow moisture stress during critical physiological transitions:

1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI) (20–25 DAS)**: **MOST CRITICAL STAGE**. The crown roots emerge about 2 cm below soil surface. Moisture stress here permanently restricts tiller development, reducing yield by 25–35%. Give a light irrigation (4–5 cm depth).
2. **Tillering Stage (40–45 DAS)**: Supports elongation of vigorous tillers.
3. **Late Jointing Stage (60–65 DAS)**: Stem elongation and rapid spikelet differentiation.
4. **Flowering / Anthesis (80–85 DAS)**: Pollination stage. Avoid deep standing water on windy days to prevent plant lodging.
5. **Milking Stage (100–105 DAS)**: Grain formation and kernel expansion.
6. **Dough Stage (115–120 DAS)**: Grain hardening. Give light irrigation if dry hot westerly winds prevail. Stop irrigation completely 10–12 days before harvest.

## Weed Management
- **Phalaris minor (Gulli Danda)** and **Avena ludoviciana (Wild Oat)** are the major grassy weeds.
- **Chenopodium album (Bathua)** is the primary broadleaf weed.
- Apply Clodinafop-propargyl 15% WP @ 60 g a.i./ha (400 g/ha) **OR** Sulfosulfuron 75% WG @ 25 g a.i./ha (33.3 g/ha) at 30–35 DAS after the first irrigation when weeds are at the 2–3 leaf stage.
- *Notice*: For details on resistant weed management, consult `weeds/wheat_phalaris_minor.md`.

## Major Diseases & Pest Alerts
- **Yellow Rust (Stripe Rust - Puccinia striiformis)**: Characterized by bright yellow powdery pustules forming continuous narrow parallel stripes on leaves. If detected, spray Tebuconazole 25.9% EC or Propiconazole 25% EC under agricultural department supervision. See `diseases/wheat_yellow_rust.md`.
- **Termites (*दीमक*)**: Cause seedlings to wilt and dry up. Treat seed with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC or Fipronil 5% SC prior to sowing in sandy soils.

## Farmer Decision Table
| Field Observation | Probable Cause | Action to Take |
|---|---|---|
| Crop is 21 days old, soil top layer dry | CRI root development stage | Apply light irrigation immediately. Follow with urea top dressing. |
| Older bottom leaves turn pale yellow in 'V' shape | Nitrogen starvation | Top dress 35 kg Urea/acre, or spray 2% Urea solution foliar. |
| 3rd and 4th leaves from top show rusty chlorosis | Zinc deficiency | Foliar spray of 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (21%) + 0.25% Lime solution. |
| Linear stripes of yellow powder on leaf surface | Yellow Rust infection | Alert local KVK immediately. Apply university-recommended triazole fungicide. |
| Wheat lodging after 4th irrigation | High winds on wet field | Never irrigate when weather forecast predicts winds >15 km/h. |

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Kya pehli sinchai 21 din par hi karni zaroori hai? (Is 21-day first irrigation mandatory?)**  
  *A*: Yes, between 20–25 days after sowing is the Crown Root Initiation (CRI) phase. Delaying this irrigation severely reduces tiller development. However, do not over-flood; a light irrigation (4–5 cm) is optimal.
- **Q: Gehu me urea kab tak dalna chahiye? (Until what stage can urea be applied?)**  
  *A*: All urea top dressing must be completed by 50–55 days after sowing (before ear emergence). Applying urea after heading only increases foliar disease and lodging without improving grain yield.
