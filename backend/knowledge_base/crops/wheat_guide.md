# Wheat Crop Production Guide (गेहूं की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Soil & Climate Requirements
Wheat is a prime Rabi cereal crop requiring a cool, moist climate during vegetative growth and warm, dry weather during grain filling and ripening. 
- Ideal Temperature: 20°C to 25°C for germination, 16°C to 20°C during tillering, and 23°C to 28°C during maturity.
- Soil Preference: Well-drained fertile loamy, clay-loam, or sandy-loam soils with a neutral pH of 6.5 to 7.5 are best suited. Heavy clay soils with poor internal drainage are prone to waterlogging, which severely restricts root aeration.

## Sowing Time, Seed Rate & Spacing
- Timely Sown Irrigated Wheat: First fortnight of November (Nov 1 to Nov 15) in North-Western and North-Eastern plains.
- Late Sown Conditions: Up to the first week of December.
- Seed Rate: 100 kg/ha (40 kg/acre) for timely sown; increase to 125 kg/ha (50 kg/acre) for late-sown conditions.
- Spacing: 20 to 22.5 cm between rows for timely sown, and 18 cm for late sown. Depth of sowing must not exceed 4 to 5 cm.

## General Nutrient Guidelines
For irrigated high-yielding wheat varieties:
- Recommended N:P:K ratio is 120:60:40 kg/ha (approx. 48:24:16 kg/acre).
- Basal Application: Full dose of Phosphorus (DAP/SSP), full dose of Potash (MOP), and 1/3rd of Nitrogen applied at the time of sowing.
- Top Dressing: Remaining 2/3rd Nitrogen applied in two equal splits: first split at 1st irrigation (CRI stage, 21 days after sowing) and second split at jointing/tillering stage (45-50 DAS).
- Micronutrient: Apply Zinc Sulphate (21% Zn @ 25 kg/ha or 33% monohydrate @ 15 kg/ha) in zinc-deficient soils once every 2-3 seasons.
