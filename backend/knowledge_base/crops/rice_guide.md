# Rice Crop Production Guide (धान की खेती)

## Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
Rice (धान) is the primary staple Kharif crop of India.
- Climate: Hot and humid climate with average temperature between 22°C and 32°C. High humidity and abundant sunshine are essential.
- Soil Preference: Deep, fertile clayey or clay-loam soils with high water retention capacity and rich organic matter. Soil pH between 5.5 and 7.0 is optimal.

## Nursery Raising & Transplanting
- Nursery Sowing: May to June depending on onset of monsoon. Use 20-25 kg certified seeds per hectare for transplanted puddle rice (DSR requires 15-20 kg/ha).
- Seed Treatment: Treat seeds with Carbendazim 2g/kg or Trichoderma viride 5g/kg seed to prevent seed-borne diseases.
- Transplanting: 21 to 25 days old seedlings should be transplanted at 2-3 seedlings per hill with 20 x 15 cm spacing.

## Fertilizer Management for Paddy
- Recommended N:P:K: 120:60:60 kg/ha for medium/long duration varieties; 100:50:50 kg/ha for short duration varieties.
- Split Application of Nitrogen: 25% as basal, 50% at active tillering (3 weeks after transplanting), and 25% at panicle initiation.
- Zinc Application: Basal application of Zinc Sulphate @ 25 kg/ha at puddling to prevent Khaira disease (खैरा रोग).
