# Rice Crop Production Guide (धान की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Rice (*धान*) is India's principal Kharif food grain. Securing yields of 22–28 quintals per acre (55–70 q/ha) in Uttar Pradesh requires healthy 21–25 day-old nursery seedlings, balanced puddling or calibrated Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) establishment, mandatory basal application of Zinc Sulphate (25 kg/ha) to prevent Khaira disease, 3-split Nitrogen management, water-saving Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) during tillering, and vigilant disease scouting against Blast and Sheath Blight.

## When this guidance applies
- **Agro-climatic Zones**: Eastern UP, Central UP, Western UP, and Terai belt.
- **Season**: Kharif (Monsoon) season; nursery sowing in May–June, transplanting in June–July.
- **Soil Type**: Heavy clay, clay loam, or silt loam capable of holding standing water; pH 5.5 to 7.5.

## Nursery Management & Seed Treatment
1. **Nursery Area**: For 1 hectare of transplanting (2.5 acres), prepare 1,000 m² (approx. 2.5 biswa per acre) of well-manured nursery beds.
2. **Seed Rate**:
   - Inbred coarse/medium varieties: 30–35 kg/ha (12–14 kg/acre).
   - Basmati varieties: 20–25 kg/ha (8–10 kg/acre).
   - Hybrid varieties: 15 kg/ha (6 kg/acre).
   - Direct Seeded Rice (DSR): 20–25 kg/ha with seed drill.
3. **Seed Treatment**:
   - Soak seeds in clean water for 24 hours.
   - Mix Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2.0 g/kg seed + Streptocycline @ 1.0 g per 10 kg seed in water to prevent seed-borne Blast, Brown Spot, and Bacterial Leaf Blight.
   - For organic treatment: Soak in *Trichoderma viride* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* suspension @ 10 g/kg seed.
4. **Seedling Age**: Transplant seedlings when 20–25 days old (at 4–5 leaf stage). Avoid aged seedlings (>30 days), as tillering capacity deteriorates sharply.

## Land Preparation & Transplanting Spacing
- **Puddling**: Plough field twice dry, then flood and puddle twice with disc plough or rotary tiller, followed by leveling plank (*patela*). Puddling destroys soil macropores to arrest water percolation.
- **Transplanting Spacing**:
  - Maintain 20 cm row-to-row and 15 cm plant-to-plant distance (approx. 33 hills per m²).
  - Plant strictly 2–3 seedlings per hill at a shallow depth of 2–3 cm. Deep planting (>4 cm) suffocates root aeration and delays tiller initiation.

## Balanced Nutrient Management (NPK + Zinc)
For high-yielding semi-dwarf rice varieties in Uttar Pradesh, the standard recommended dose is **120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare** (approx. 48:24:16 kg/acre).

### Fertilizer Splitting Schedule
1. **Basal Application (at final puddling before transplanting)**:
   - Full dose of Phosphorus: DAP @ 130 kg/ha (52 kg/acre) **OR** SSP @ 375 kg/ha (150 kg/acre).
   - Full dose of Potash: MOP @ 67 kg/ha (27 kg/acre). (Crucial for straw strength and BPH resistance).
   - One-third (1/3rd) Nitrogen: Urea @ 70 kg/ha (28 kg/acre). (Adjust if DAP is used).
   - **Zinc Sulphate (MANDATORY)**: Apply 25 kg/ha (10 kg/acre) Zinc Sulphate heptahydrate (21% Zn) or 15 kg/ha Zinc monohydrate (33% Zn). *Warning*: Never mix Zinc Sulphate with DAP in the same fertilizer slurry; apply Zinc separately.
2. **First Top Dressing (Active Tillering Stage, 20–25 DAT)**:
   - Top dress 1/3rd Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre).
3. **Second Top Dressing (Panicle Initiation Stage, 40–45 DAT)**:
   - Top dress remaining 1/3rd Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre). Complete all nitrogen application before flowering.

## Water Management: Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)
- **Establishment Stage (Day 1 to Day 10)**: Maintain 2–3 cm shallow standing water to settle transplanted roots and suppress weed emergence.
- **Vegetative Tillering Stage (Day 11 to Panicle Initiation)**: Adopt **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)**:
  - Install a 30 cm long, 10–15 cm diameter perforated plastic/PVC field tube 15 cm into the soil.
  - Re-irrigate to 5 cm depth only when the water level inside the tube drops 15 cm below the soil surface.
  - AWD saves 25–30% irrigation water, improves root anchorage, and prevents methane gas accumulation.
- **Reproductive Stage (Panicle Initiation to Grain Filling)**: Maintain continuous 3–5 cm ponding. Moisture stress at flowering causes floral sterility and empty panicles.
- **Pre-Harvest Drainage**: Drain field completely 10–12 days before harvest to hasten uniform ripening and facilitate mechanical combine harvesting.

## Major Pests & Diseases (Triage & Links)
- **Khaira Disease (Zinc Deficiency)**: Midrib stays green while chlorotic brown rusty lesions cover older leaves; stunted root growth. Rectify by foliar spraying 0.5% Zinc Sulphate + 0.25% Lime. See `fertilizers/micronutrients_zinc_iron.md`.
- **Blast (Pyricularia oryzae)**: Spindle-shaped lesions with grey centers and dark reddish borders on leaves; blackening of panicle node (neck blast). See `diseases/rice_blast.md`.
- **Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)**: Irregular greenish-grey snake-skin lesions on leaf sheaths just above the water line. See `diseases/rice_sheath_blight.md`.
- **Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas)**: Causes 'dead heart' in tillering stage and 'white earhead' (empty white panicles) at maturity. See `pests/rice_stem_borer.md`.

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Dhaan me zinc kab aur kaise dale? (When and how to apply zinc in rice?)**  
  *A*: Zinc Sulphate (21% @ 10 kg/acre) should ideally be broadcast as a basal application during final puddling. Never mix Zinc Sulphate with DAP directly. If deficiency symptoms appear in standing crop, spray 0.5% Zinc Sulphate neutralized with 0.25% Lime.
- **Q: Kya dhaan ke khet me lagatar pani bhara rakhna zaroori hai? (Is continuous standing water necessary?)**  
  *A*: No. Continuous standing water is only required during the first 10 days after transplanting and during flowering. During the vegetative phase, allowing water to recede until hair-line cracks appear (AWD method) saves water and prevents root suffocation.
