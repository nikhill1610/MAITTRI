# Mustard Crop Production Guide (सरसों / राई की खेती)

## Soil & Agro-Climatic Requirements
Mustard (सरसों) is a premier Rabi oilseed crop.
- Climate: Cool, dry subtropical weather with temperatures between 15°C and 25°C. Requires bright sunny days during flowering and pod development.
- Soil Preference: Light to medium loamy soils with good drainage. Extremely tolerant to moderate soil salinity compared to pulses. Optimum soil pH is 6.0 to 7.5.

## Sowing & Seed Rate
- Optimum Sowing Window: October 10 to October 25. Timely sowing escapes peak mustard aphid infestation in December/January.
- Seed Rate: 4 to 5 kg/ha (1.5 to 2 kg/acre).
- Spacing: 30 to 45 cm between rows and 10 to 15 cm between plants after thinning (at 15-20 DAS). Sowing depth: 2 to 3 cm in moist soil.

## Balanced Fertilizer & Crucial Sulphur Need
- Recommended Dose: 80:40:40 kg N:P:K/ha for irrigated areas; 40:20:20 kg/ha for rainfed areas.
- Nitrogen Splitting: Half N with full P and K as basal at sowing; remaining half N top-dressed after first irrigation (30-35 DAS).
- Vital Sulphur Requirement: Mustard requires 20-30 kg Sulphur per hectare (via Gypsum or elemental sulphur or Single Super Phosphate - SSP) to synthesize oil and glucosinolates. Applying SSP instead of DAP provides both Phosphorus (16% P) and Sulphur (12% S) in balanced form.
