# Mustard & Rapeseed Crop Production Guide (सरसों एवं राई की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Indian Mustard (*Brassica juncea / सरसों, राई*) is North India's primary winter commercial oilseed. Achieving high seed yields (8–12 quintals per acre / 20–30 q/ha) and high oil recovery (38–42%) requires timely sowing in October (which allows the crop to flower before winter aphid peak), rigorous thinning at 15–20 days to ensure single-plant vigor, mandatory application of Sulphur (20–30 kg/ha via Gypsum or Bentonite Sulphur) to synthesize oil, 2 carefully timed light irrigations, and strict late-afternoon spraying protocols to protect honeybee pollinators.

## When this guidance applies
- **Agro-climatic Zones**: Western UP, Central UP, Bundelkhand, and Eastern UP.
- **Season**: Rabi season.
- **Sowing Window**:
  - *Toria*: September 15 to September 30.
  - *Timely Sown Mustard*: October 1 to October 25 (**Optimal window** to escape severe aphid attack).
  - *Late Sown Mustard*: Up to November 10 (use early-maturing varieties).
- **Soil Conditions**: Well-drained sandy loam or light loam soils; pH 6.0 to 7.5. Mustard does not tolerate water stagnation.

## Seed Rate, Sowing & Mandatory Thinning
- **Seed Rate**:
  - Pure Irrigated Crop: 4.0 to 5.0 kg/ha (1.5 to 2.0 kg/acre).
  - Mixed / Intercropping with Wheat (9:1 ratio): 1.5 to 2.0 kg/ha.
- **Seed Treatment (Prevents Seedling Rot & Early Insect Attack)**:
  - *Fungicide*: Treat seed with Thiram 75% WP or Captan 50% WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed to protect against damping-off and Alternaria blight.
  - *White Rust Prophylaxis*: Apron 35 SD (Metalaxyl) @ 6 g/kg seed where white rust has a history.
  - *Early Insect Protection*: Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 5 g/kg seed protects young seedlings against Painted Bug (*Bagrada hilaris*) and flea beetles for 15 days.
- **Spacing & Sowing Depth**:
  - Row-to-row distance: 45 cm for bold varieties (RH-749, Giriraj); 30 cm for short varieties.
  - Depth: Strictly 3–4 cm in firm moist seedbed.
- **Mandatory Thinning (*छटनी*) at 15–20 DAS**:
  - Crucial step: Remove crowded and weak plants to maintain 10–15 cm distance between plants in the row. Crowded plants develop thin, spindly stems with fewer branches and low pod count.

## Balanced Nutrient Management (NPK + SULPHUR)
Recommended dose for irrigated mustard in medium fertility soils is **80 kg N : 40 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O : 30 kg Sulphur per hectare** (approx. 32:16:16:12 kg/acre).

### The Critical Role of Sulphur (S)
Mustard belongs to the Cruciferae family, which synthesizes glucosinolates and oil fatty acids requiring high sulphur. Applying 20–30 kg/ha of elemental Sulphur:
- Increases seed oil content by 2% to 4%.
- Boosts overall seed yield by 15% to 25%.
- Source options:
  1. **Agricultural Gypsum** (contains 18% S): Apply 150–200 kg/ha (60–80 kg/acre) as basal at field preparation.
  2. **Single Super Phosphate (SSP)**: Using SSP (contains 11–12% S) instead of DAP automatically satisfies sulphur requirements.
  3. **Bentonite Sulphur granules (90% S)**: Apply 25 kg/ha (10 kg/acre) as basal.

### Fertilizer Application Schedule
1. **Basal Application (at final harrowing)**:
   - Full dose of Phosphorus: SSP @ 250 kg/ha (100 kg/acre) [supplies 40 kg P2O5 + 30 kg Sulphur].
   - Full dose of Potash: MOP @ 67 kg/ha (27 kg/acre).
   - Half (1/2) Nitrogen: Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre).
2. **First Top Dressing (at First Irrigation, 30–35 DAS)**:
   - Apply remaining half Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 90 kg/ha (36 kg/acre) immediately following first irrigation.

## Irrigation Scheduling & Moisture Care
Mustard has low water requirements compared to wheat (requiring only 2 to 3 irrigations):
1. **First Irrigation (Rosette / Branching Stage, 30–35 DAS)**: Crucial for vigorous branching and flower bud formation. Give light irrigation (avoid heavy flood).
2. **Second Irrigation (Siliquae / Pod Formation Stage, 60–65 DAS)**: Supports pod elongation and seed development.
- *Drainage Warning*: Mustard is extremely vulnerable to waterlogging. Water standing in the field for more than 12 hours causes root rot and premature plant yellowing.

## Major Pest & Disease Management
- **Mustard Aphid (*माहू / चेपा* - Lipaphis erysimi)**:
  - *Damage*: Aphids form dense colonies on terminal shoots, inflorescence, and young pods from late December through February, sucking sap and secreting honeydew.
  - *Economic Threshold Level (ETL)*: Spray only when aphid colony reaches 1.5–2.0 cm on terminal shoots of 20% sampled plants, or 50 aphids per plant.
  - **CRITICAL POLLINATOR SAFETY RULE**: Mustard relies heavily on honeybees (*Apis mellifera*) for cross-pollination. **NEVER SPRAY IN THE MORNING**. Always apply insecticidal sprays in the late afternoon (after 3:30 PM) when honeybee foraging ceases.
  - *Recommended Spray*: Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.0 ml/litre or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.2 g/litre. See `pests/mustard_aphids.md`.
- **White Rust (*Albugo candida*)**:
  - *Symptoms*: Chalky white blisters on the underside of leaves; floral stems become swollen, twisted, and malformed into sterile "stagheads".
  - *Control*: Prophylactic spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/litre; if stagheads appear, apply Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil MZ) @ 2 g/litre. See `diseases/mustard_white_rust.md`.

## Farmer Decision Table
| Field Observation | Likely Cause | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| Dense clusters of green aphids on top 2 inches of flowering twigs | Mustard Aphid exceeding ETL | Spray Thiamethoxam or Dimethoate strictly in late afternoon after 3:30 PM. |
| Chalky white spots on leaf underside; flower stalk thick and distorted | White Rust (*Albugo candida*) | Spray Mancozeb 75% WP (2 g/L) or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (2 g/L). |
| Leaves pale green, yellowing on young leaves first; low oil yield | Sulphur deficiency | Apply 25 kg/ha Bentonite Sulphur or broadcast 150 kg/ha Agricultural Gypsum. |
| Seedlings crowded, thin stems, few branches | Failure to thin seedlings | Immediately thin plants to 12–15 cm apart within 20 days of emergence. |

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Sarson me mahu ke liye dawa kab chhidkni chahiye? (When should aphid spray be applied in mustard?)**  
  *A*: Spray only when aphids cover 1.5–2 cm of terminal twigs on 20% of plants. Always spray in the late afternoon (after 3:30 PM). Spraying in the morning kills honeybees, drastically reducing pod set and seed yield.
- **Q: Sarson me sulphur (gandhak) kyu zaroori hai? (Why is sulphur essential for mustard?)**  
  *A*: Sulphur is mandatory for oil synthesis in mustard seeds. Applying 20–30 kg/ha Sulphur increases oil content by 2–4% and grain yield by 15–20%. Use Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or Gypsum.
