# Maize Crop Production Guide (मक्का की खेती)

## Soil & Agro-Climatic Requirements
Maize (मक्का) is known as the "Queen of Cereals" due to its high genetic yield potential.
- Climate: Warm season crop growing best between 21°C and 30°C. Highly sensitive to waterlogging, frost, and extreme heat during flowering.
- Soil Preference: Deep, fertile, well-drained loamy to silty loam soils rich in organic matter. Soil pH between 6.5 and 7.5 is ideal. Good surface drainage is non-negotiable.

## Sowing & Seed Rate
- Kharif Sowing: With onset of monsoon (June to July). Rabi Maize: October to November.
- Seed Rate: 20 kg/ha for hybrids, 25 kg/ha for composites, 10-12 kg/ha for baby corn / sweet corn.
- Spacing: 60 cm between rows and 20 cm between plants. Depth: 4-5 cm.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
- Hybrids response: Recommended N:P:K is 120-150:60:60 kg/ha.
- Nitrogen Splitting (Critical for High Yield):
  1. 20% N as basal at sowing with full P and K.
  2. 40% N at Knee-high stage (घुटने की ऊंचाई, 30-35 DAS).
  3. 30% N at Tasseling / Silking stage (नर मंजरी व भुट्टा बनते समय, 50-60 DAS).
  4. 10% N at grain filling stage.
- Zinc Sulphate: Apply 25 kg/ha basal to avoid white bud of maize (जिंक की कमी से सफेद कली).
