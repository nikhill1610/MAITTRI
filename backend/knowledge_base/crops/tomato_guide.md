# Tomato Crop Production Guide (टमाटर की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
Tomato is a warm-season crop widely cultivated across India.
- Temperature: Optimum temperature for fruit set and lycopene (red color) synthesis is 20°C to 24°C. Above 35°C, flower drop increases significantly.
- Soil Preference: Well-drained sandy loam or clay loam soil rich in organic matter. Soil pH 6.0 to 7.0 is best. Very sensitive to waterlogging.

## Nursery & Transplanting
- Sowing Seasons: Autumn-winter crop (transplanted in Oct-Nov), spring-summer crop (transplanted in Jan-Feb), and rainy season crop (transplanted in July-Aug).
- Seed Rate: 400-500 g/ha for open-pollinated varieties, 150-200 g/ha for F1 hybrids.
- Spacing: 60 x 45 cm for non-staked determinate varieties; 75-90 x 45 cm for indeterminate staked hybrids.

## Staking & Pruning
- Staking (सहारा देना): Indeterminate varieties must be staked with bamboo poles, wires, or strings 30 days after transplanting to keep foliage and fruit off wet soil, preventing fruit rot and leaf spots.
- Fertilizer Dose: 120:80:80 kg N:P:K/ha for open pollinated; 150-180:100:100 kg/ha for hybrids. Apply 1/3rd N with full P and K at transplanting, and remaining N in two splits at 30 and 45 days after transplanting.
