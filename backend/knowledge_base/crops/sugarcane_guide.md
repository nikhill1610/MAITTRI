# Western Uttar Pradesh Sugarcane Production, Ratoon Management & Sugar Mill Integration Guide (पश्चिमी उत्तर प्रदेश गन्ना उत्पादन एवं पेड़ी प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Sugarcane (*गन्ना / Saccharum officinarum*) is the economic backbone of Western Uttar Pradesh, spanning over 2.8 million hectares across Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Bijnor, and Saharanpur divisions. While variety **Co-0238** revolutionized yields for a decade, widespread emergence of **Red Rot (*लाल सड़न रोग / Colletotrichum falcatum*) Pathotype CF-13** has made variety replacement an urgent priority. Farmers in Western UP must transition to certified early elite varieties like **Co-15023**, **Co-0118**, and **CoLk-14201**, adopt **deep trench planting (120 cm row-to-row)** with sett treatment, apply systematic **ratoon stover mulching**, and coordinate harvest schedules with the **UP E-Ganna / Cane Commissioner supply ticket calendar**.

---

## Seasonal Planting Windows & Recommended Varieties

### Planting Seasons:
1. **Autumn Planting (*शरदकालीन बुवाई*)**: **15 September to 31 October**. Delivers 20–25% higher cane yield and 0.5% higher sugar recovery than spring planting. Excellent for intercropping with mustard, potato, garlic, or pulses.
2. **Spring Planting (*बसंतकालीन बुवाई*)**: **15 February to 31 March**. The standard planting season after harvest of mustard, toria, or potato.
3. **Summer/Late Planting (*ग्रीष्मकालीन बुवाई*)**: **April to May (After Wheat Harvest)**. Requires sprouted polybag settlings or pre-germinated single-bud setts to avoid drastic yield drops.

### Variety Replacement Matrix (UP Cane Department Directive):

| Variety Category | Recommended Varieties | Characteristics & Cane Yield | Sugar Recovery | Management Focus |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Early Elite (High Sugar)** | **Co-15023** | 85–95 t/ha; rapid early tillering, soft rind | 12.5–13.2% | Requires earthing-up to prevent lodging; immune to red rot. |
| **Early Standard** | **Co-0118** | 80–90 t/ha; erect, non-lodging, thick cane | 12.0–12.8% | High ratoonability; excellent cold tolerance. |
| **Mid-Late Elite** | **Co-98014 (Karan 1)** | 90–100 t/ha; deep green foliage, vigorous ratoon | 11.8–12.2% | Suitable for normal to moisture-constrained soils. |
| **Specialized Subtropical** | **CoLk-14201** | 85–95 t/ha; non-flowering, solid pith | 12.2–12.7% | High red rot resistance; suitable for Central & Western UP. |
| **Phase-Out Alert** | **Co-0238** | Severely attacked by Red Rot across Western UP | High | **Do NOT use Co-0238 seed from affected plots**. Replace urgently with Co-15023 or Co-0118. |

## Land Preparation, Trench Planting & Sett Selection

### 1. Land Preparation & Deep Tillage
- Deep subsoiling (30–40 cm) or disc chiseling every 3 years breaks the subsurface hard pan.
- 2 plowings with cultivator followed by fine rotavator harrowing and leveling.
- Apply **15–20 tonnes/ha well-decomposed FYM or pressmud compost** 20 days prior to planting.

### 2. Deep Trench Method (ICAR-IISR Standard)
- Open trenches **25–30 cm deep** with a tractor-drawn trench-opener at **120 cm (4 feet) row-to-row spacing** (or paired rows at 30:120 cm).
- *Benefits*: 30% irrigation water saving, facilitates mechanical inter-culture and intercropping, prevents lodging during monsoon storms, and produces robust tillers.
- Place setts end-to-end in the trench furrow and cover with only **5–7 cm loose soil**. Never bury setts under more than 8 cm soil as it hinders bud emergence.

### 3. Seed Cane & Sett Treatment Protocol
- Select healthy seed cane aged **8 to 10 months** from pest- and disease-free nurseries.
- Discard bottom 20% hard nodes and top spongy pith. Cut setts cleanly with **2 or 3 healthy buds** per sett.
- **Seed Rate**:
  - Two-bud setts: **45,000 to 50,000 setts/ha** (approx. 70–80 quintals/ha).
  - Three-bud setts: **30,000 to 35,000 setts/ha** (approx. 75–85 quintals/ha).
- **Hot Water Treatment (HWT)**: Treat setts at **52°C for 30 minutes** or Moist Hot Air Treatment (MHAT at 54°C, 2.5 hours) to eliminate internal *Colletotrichum* (red rot) and grassy shoot phytoplasma.
- **Sett Chemical Dip**:
  - Submerge cut setts for **15 minutes** in a solution of **Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2.0 g/L water** or **Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP @ 1.5 g/L water**.
  - Add **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L water** to the sett treatment solution to protect against early termites and subterranean white grub.

## Balanced Nutrient Management (Western UP Standard)

Sugarcane is a heavy feeder with a 10 to 12-month lifecycle. Fertilizer doses for plant crop (Western UP alluvial loam):

| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Commercial Source & Split Application Timing |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **180–200 kg/ha** (Plant) / **220–250 kg/ha** (Ratoon) | - 1/3rd basal at planting (along with P and K).<br>- 1/3rd at first tillering (45–60 DAP).<br>- 1/3rd at onset of monsoon / before earthing-up (90–120 DAP). Avoid late N after July. |
| **Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$)** | **60–80 kg/ha** | 100% basal in trench bottoms using **SSP (375–500 kg/ha)** or **DAP (130–175 kg/ha)**. |
| **Potassium ($K_2O$)** | **60 kg/ha** | 100% basal in trench bottoms using **MOP (100 kg/ha)**. Crucial for cane sucrose accumulation and drought tolerance. |
| **Zinc Sulphate (33%)** | **25 kg/ha** | Basal broadcast in trenches. Do NOT mix directly with water-soluble phosphate fertilizers. |
| **Ferrous Sulphate ($FeSO_4$)** | **15–20 kg/ha** | In calcareous/alkaline soils (pH > 8.0) exhibiting interveinal iron chlorosis; or 2 foliar sprays of 1% $FeSO_4$ + 0.1% citric acid. |

## Complete Ratoon Management (*पेड़ी प्रबंधन*)

Ratoon crops account for >50% of the sugarcane area in Western UP, saving seed costs and maturing 3 to 4 weeks earlier than plant crops. Neglecting ratoon management leads to 30–50% yield drops:

1. **Harvesting Flush with Ground Level (*जमीन की सतह से कटाई*)**:
   - Harvest cane flush with the ground using sharp sickles or mechanical harvesters. Leaving 5–10 cm aerial stubbles leads to weak aerial tillers susceptible to pests.
   - If cut high, immediately perform **stubble shaving (*ठूंठ छंटाई*)** flush with ground level within 48 hours.
2. **Off-Barring & Root Pruning (*किनारे की जुताई*)**:
   - Plow 10–15 cm away from both sides of the stubble row using a narrow disc or cultivator to prune old root systems and encourage vigorous new secondary roots.
3. **Trash Mulching (*पाती पलवार*)**:
   - Do NOT burn sugarcane dry leaves (trash). Burning kills beneficial predators and wastes 6–8 tonnes of organic biomass.
   - Retain 8–10 cm trash blanket between inter-row furrows. This suppresses weeds, conserves 35% soil moisture, and adds 40 kg N and 15 kg K per hectare upon decomposition.
   - Accelerate trash decomposition by spraying **10 kg Urea + 5 kg Single Super Phosphate + Trichoderma / bio-decomposer in 200 L water/ha** over the mulched leaves.
4. **Gap Filling**:
   - Fill gaps (>45 cm without shoots) within 25–30 days using pre-sprouted polybag settlings or tillers separated from dense clumps.
5. **Additional Ratoon Nitrogen**:
   - Ratoon crops require **20–25% higher Nitrogen** (220–250 kg N/ha) than plant cane due to slower soil mineralisation under stubbles. Apply first N dose immediately after off-barring and stubble shaving.

## Pest & Disease Management

### 1. Major Insect Pests
- **Early Shoot Borer (*Chilo infuscatellus*)**: Attires dead hearts during April–June before internode formation.
  - *Management*: Trash mulching; light earthing up at 45 days; install pheromone traps @ 10/ha.
- **Top Borer (*Scirpophaga excerptalis*)**: Central leaf spindle dries into "bunchy top" with characteristic shot-holes in leaves during July–August.
  - *Management*: Collect and destroy egg masses; release *Trichogramma chilonis* egg parasitoids @ 50,000/ha at 10-day intervals.

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.

### CIBRC Approved Chemical Formulations for Sugarcane:
- **For Termites & Early Shoot Borer at Planting**:
  - **Fipronil 0.3% GR** @ **25–30 kg/ha** applied along furrow trenches over planted setts prior to soil covering; OR
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC** @ **375 ml/ha (150 ml/acre)** in 1,000 L water directed as a coarse spray along furrow cane setts at planting or at 60 DAP before earthing-up.
- **For Top Borer (3rd brood in June-July)**:
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR** @ **18.75 kg/ha (7.5 kg/acre)** applied to root zone soil followed by immediate irrigation; OR
  - **Cartap Hydrochloride 4% GR** @ **25 kg/ha**.

## Sugar Mill Supply Ticket (Parchi) Calendar Integration

1. **UP Cane Commissioner Portal (*kisan.upagriculture.com* & *E-Ganna App*)**:
   - All Western UP cane growers must verify survey acreage, variety classification (Early vs General), and bonded quota on the E-Ganna platform before mill crushing begins in October/November.
2. **Crushing Priority**:
   - Sugar mills issue priority purchase supply tickets (*सप्लाई पर्ची*) first for **Ratoon crops of early varieties (Co-15023, Co-0118)** in November, followed by early plant cane, and general varieties in January–March.
3. **Maturity Determination**:
   - Field brix refractometer reading should reach **18–20% Brix** with a purity quotient >85% for peak sugar payment incentives.

## Decision Table: Troubleshooting Sugarcane Emergencies

| Symptom / Field Observation | Probable Cause | Urgent Corrective Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Third leaf turns yellow, top dries, internal cane shows red patches with white transverse bands | **Red Rot (*Colletotrichum falcatum*)** | Rogue and burn entire clump immediately. Drench spot with 0.2% Carbendazim. Discontinue using setts from this plot. |
| Spindle leaves turn black, whip-like dusty coil emerges | **Smut (*Sporisorium scitamineum*)** | Bag the smut whip in polythene, rogue entire clump and burn. Never leave uncovered whips in field. |
| Dead heart in young shoots (April-May) that emits foul smell when pulled | **Early Shoot Borer** | Apply Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 150 ml/acre or Fipronil granules; irrigate field. |
| Yellowing between green veins in top leaves on high pH soils | **Iron (Fe) Chlorosis** | Foliar spray 1.0% $FeSO_4$ (1 kg Ferrous Sulphate + 100 g Citric Acid per 100 L water). |
