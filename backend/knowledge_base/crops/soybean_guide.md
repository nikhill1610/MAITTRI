# Soybean (*Glycine max*) Production, Broad Bed Furrow & Yellow Mosaic Virus Guide (सोयाबीन की उन्नत खेती, बीबीएफ तकनीक एवं कीट प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Soybean (*सोयाबीन / Glycine max*) is an essential dual-purpose oilseed and high-protein legume cultivated across southern Uttar Pradesh (especially the Bundelkhand districts of Lalitpur, Jhansi, and Jalaun). In heavy black clay soils (*Mar* and *Kabar*), traditional flat-bed soybean frequently suffers from two climate extremes: **waterlogging during heavy monsoon downpours** (causing root rot and asphyxiation) and **prolonged dry spells at pod-filling**. Successful harvest requires planting on **Broad Bed Furrows (BBF)** or Ridge-and-Furrow systems, specific **Bradyrhizobium seed inoculation**, early management of the **Girdle Beetle (*गर्डल बीटल*)**, and control of the whitefly vector transmitting **Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)**.

---

## Sowing Window, Seed Rate & Varietal Selection

### 1. Optimal Sowing Window:
- **20 June to 10 July** (onset of monsoon when the soil profile has received at least **75–100 mm of cumulative rainfall**).
- Sowing into dry soil before sufficient monsoon soaking results in seed rot and patchy emergence.

### 2. Recommended High-Yielding Varieties:
- **JS-20-34**: Early maturity (85–88 days), drought-tolerant, non-shattering, excellent for Bundelkhand rainfed conditions.
- **JS-20-98**: Mid-maturity (95–98 days), high resistance to Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV) and charcoal rot.
- **NRC-127** & **NRC-138**: Released by ICAR-IISR, resistant to lodging, high oil content (19–20%).
- **RVS-2001-4**: High pod-bearing capacity and field tolerance to stem flies and girdle beetles.

### 3. Seed Rate & Germination Test:
- Seed Rate: **65 to 75 kg/ha** for medium-seeded varieties (100-seed weight 10–12 g).
- *Mandatory Germination Test*: Test 100 seeds between moist gunny bags prior to sowing. **Minimum germination percentage must be at least 70%**. If germination is 60–69%, increase seed rate proportionately. If <60%, do NOT use as seed.

---

## Land Preparation & Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) Technology

Heavy black clay soils (*Kabar*) swell when wet and crack deeply when dry. The **Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) system** is the single most effective climate-resilience practice for soybean:
- **BBF Dimensions**: A tractor-drawn BBF planter forms raised beds **120 cm wide** separated by drainage/irrigation furrows **30 cm wide and 15 cm deep**.
- **Row Configuration**: Sow 3 or 4 soybean rows on top of each raised bed at **30 cm row-to-row spacing** and **5–7 cm plant-to-plant spacing**. Sowing depth should strictly be **3 to 4 cm**.
- **Dual Advantage**:
  - *Excess Rain*: The furrows serve as safe surface drainage channels, preventing waterlogging around plant crowns.
  - *Dry Spells*: The furrows trap and retain runoff moisture, extending soil moisture availability by 10–14 days during critical pod-fill droughts.

---

## Bio-Inoculation & Seed Treatment Protocol

Soybean requires a species-specific symbiont—***Bradyrhizobium japonicum***—which is absent in soils without prior soybean history. Follow the **FIR Sequence (Fungicide $\rightarrow$ Insecticide $\rightarrow$ Biofertilizer)**:

1. **Step 1 - Fungicide Dressing**:
   - Treat seed with **Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.5 g/kg seed** OR **Trichoderma viride @ 8–10 g/kg seed**.
2. **Step 2 - Insecticide Dressing**:
   - Coat with **Thiamethoxam 30% FS @ 10 ml per kg seed**. Protects young seedlings for 25–30 days against subterranean stem flies, girdle beetles, and early sucking vectors.
3. **Step 3 - Dual Bio-Inoculation**:
   - Inoculate with **Bradyrhizobium japonicum culture @ 5 g/kg seed** + **Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 5 g/kg seed** using a 10% jaggery (*gud*) solution as an adhesive. Shade dry for 45 minutes before drilling into soil.

## Balanced Nutrient Management

Soybean satisfies 60–70% of its Nitrogen needs through atmospheric fixation after 25–30 DAS. Excessive basal chemical Nitrogen suppresses nodulation:

| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Source & Application Method |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **20–25 kg/ha (Starter Dose)** | Basal at sowing via DAP or SSP to support early seedling growth before nodulation. |
| **Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$)** | **60 kg/ha** | 100% basal placed 5 cm below the seed line using **Single Super Phosphate (SSP @ 375 kg/ha)**. SSP is strongly preferred over DAP because it supplies 11% free Calcium and 12% Sulphur. |
| **Potassium ($K_2O$)** | **40 kg/ha** | 100% basal via MOP (67 kg/ha) to enhance seed boldness and drought resistance. |
| **Sulphur (S)** | **20–25 kg/ha** | Essential for oil and methionine protein synthesis. Provided automatically when using SSP, or apply 200 kg/ha Agricultural Gypsum. |

## Critical Pest & Disease Management

### 1. Girdle Beetle (*Obereopsis brevis - गर्डल बीटल / छल्ला कीट*)
- **Symptoms**: Adult female beetles make two parallel circular incisions / rings (*चूड़ीदार छल्ले*) around the stem petiole or main stem and lay eggs between them. The portion of the stem above the girdle wilts, dries up, and droops within 48 hours.
- **IPM Action**: Hand-pick and destroy cut petioles showing fresh girdling rings during early vegetative checks (20–30 DAS).

### 2. Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV - पीला मोजेक वायरस)
- **Symptoms**: Bright yellow speckling and mosaic patches on leaves that coalesce into solid golden-yellow foliage. Pods become small, curved, and produce shrivelled brown grains.
- **Transmission**: Transmitted by the **Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)**.
- **Control Strategy**:
  - Plant YMV-resistant cultivars (**JS-20-98, JS-20-34**).
  - Rogue out and bury isolated yellow-diseased plants within the first 30 days.
  - Control the whitefly vector immediately upon detection.

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.

### CIBRC Approved Formulations for Soybean:
- **For Whitefly (YMV Vector) & Sucking Pests**:
  - **Thiamethoxam 25% WG** @ **100 g/ha (0.2 g/L water)**; OR
  - **Acetamiprid 20% SP** @ **100 g/ha (0.2 g/L water)**.
- **For Girdle Beetle, Semilooper & Spodoptera Pod Borers**:
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC** @ **150 ml/ha (0.3 ml/L water)** in 500 L water; OR
  - **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG** @ **220 g/ha (0.44 g/L water)**.

## Harvesting, Threshing & Seed Viability Precautions

1. **Maturity Indicator**: Harvest when **95% of pods turn golden brown** and leaves drop naturally. Seed moisture should be between **14–16%**.
2. **Avoiding Shattering**: Harvest in early morning hours when pods are slightly pliable with dewdrops to prevent shattering losses.
3. **Threshing Speed Caution**:
   - Soybean seed has a delicate seed coat and fragile embryo.
   - Adjust thresher cylinder speed strictly to **300 to 400 RPM**. High cylinder speed (>500 RPM) cracks the seed coat internally, destroying germination viability for next year's seed.
4. **Storage Moisture**: Dry grains thoroughly in shade down to **9–10% moisture content** before packing in multi-layer polythene or moisture-proof bags.
