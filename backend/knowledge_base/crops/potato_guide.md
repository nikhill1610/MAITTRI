# Potato Crop Production Guide (आलू की वैज्ञानिक खेती)

## Soil & Climate Requirements
Potato (आलू) is a cool-season vegetable crop.
- Temperature: Vegetative growth occurs best at 20°C to 24°C, but critical tuber initiation and development requires lower night temperatures around 16°C to 20°C. Tuber formation stops if soil temperatures exceed 30°C.
- Soil: Well-aerated, friable, loose sandy-loam or loamy soil rich in organic matter. Loose soil allows unrestricted tuber expansion. pH 5.2 to 6.4 is optimum (slightly acidic reduces common scab incidence).

## Planting & Spacing
- Planting Time: In plains, October 15 to November 5 (main crop).
- Seed Tuber Selection: Healthy, certified, disease-free seed tubers weighing 35-50 g with 2-3 active sprouts.
- Spacing: 60 cm between ridges and 20 cm between tubers. Plant on ridges to facilitate drainage and earthing up.
- Earthing Up (मिट्टी चढ़ाना): First earthing up at 30-35 DAS when plants are 15-20 cm high, covering developing tubers to prevent greening (solanine development).

## Fertilizer Application
- Recommended Dose: 150:80:100 kg N:P:K per hectare plus 20-25 tonnes of well-rotted FYM/compost.
- Nitrogen application: Half N along with full P and K at planting as basal, and remaining half N applied at first earthing up (30 DAS).
- Potassium preference: Use Potassium Sulphate (SOP) instead of Potassium Chloride (MOP) if available, to improve dry matter and tuber chip quality.
