# Potato Crop Production Guide (आलू की उन्नत खेती एवं कंद प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Potato (*आलू*) is Uttar Pradesh's leading commercial horticultural cash crop, with key hubs in Agra, Farrukhabad, Kannauj, and Barabanki. Producing high yields of quality table and processing tubers (140–180 quintals/acre / 350–450 q/ha) requires certified sprouted seed tubers (40–50 g size), loose well-aerated sandy loam soil, heavy basal Potassium nutrition (100–120 kg K2O/ha) to support bulking and storage firmness, light furrow irrigations that never flood the ridge crests, preventive fungicide protection against Late Blight during foggy humid weather, and mandatory dehaulming 10–12 days prior to harvest for skin hardening.

## When this guidance applies
- **Agro-climatic Zones**: Central Plain, Western Plain, and South-Western Semi-Arid Zone of UP.
- **Season**: Rabi winter crop.
- **Planting Window**:
  - *Early Crop*: September 25 to October 10 (marketed early for festive demand).
  - *Main Crop*: October 15 to November 5 (**Optimal window** for maximum bulking and yield).
  - *Late Crop*: November 15 to December 5 (requires early-maturing cultivars).
- **Soil Type**: Deep, loose, friable sandy loam or silt loam rich in organic matter; pH 5.2 to 6.5. (Note: Slightly acidic soils naturally inhibit *Common Scab*).

## Seed Tuber Handling, Chitting & Seed Treatment
1. **Seed Selection**: Use certified seed tubers of 30–45 mm diameter weighing 40–50 g with 3–4 active eyes. Planting undersized marble tubers (<20 g) drastically lowers plant vigor and yield.
2. **Cold Store Removal & Chitting (Sprouting)**:
   - Bring seed tubers out of cold storage 7 to 10 days before planting.
   - Spread tubers in a well-ventilated, shaded shed under diffused light.
   - Allow dark green, thick sprouts (2–3 mm long) to emerge. Never plant freshly taken-out dormant cold tubers in warm soil (causes seed rot).
3. **Seed Tuber Disinfection**:
   - Dip whole or cut tubers for 10 minutes in a suspension of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Emisan-6 (MEMC 6%) @ 2.5 g/litre to prevent Black Scurf and rotting.
   - If tubers are cut, keep the knife clean and allow cut pieces to heal for 24–48 hours in shade to develop a suberin skin layer before planting.

## Planting Method, Spacing & Earthing-Up
- **Ridges and Furrows**:
  - Distance between ridges: 60 cm.
  - Distance between tubers within the ridge: 20 to 25 cm.
  - Planting depth: 5–7 cm below soil surface, covered with a 8–10 cm soil ridge.
- **Earthing-Up (*मिट्टी चढ़ाना*)**:
  - Perform the first earthing-up at 25–30 days after planting (when plants are 15–20 cm high).
  - Perform second earthing-up at 40–45 days.
  - *Why Mandatory*: Uncovered developing tubers exposed to sunlight synthesize chlorophyll and toxic **Solanine**, turning tubers green and unmarketable.

## Balanced Nutrient Management (NPK + High Potash)
Potato is a heavy feeder with massive nutrient demands for tuber bulking: **150–180 kg N : 80–100 kg P2O5 : 100–120 kg K2O per hectare** (approx. 60:35:45 kg/acre).

### The Essential Role of Potassium (K)
- Potassium is responsible for starch translocating from leaves into tubers.
- It enhances cell wall thickness, reducing bruising, internal brown spot, and rotting during transit and cold storage.
- *Potash Source*: While Muriate of Potash (MOP) is standard for table potatoes, **Sulphate of Potash (SOP / 0-0-50)** is superior for seed tubers and processing varieties (Chipsona) as it increases tuber dry matter and reduces chloride-induced wateriness.

### Fertilizer Application Schedule
1. **Basal Application (in furrows before ridge making)**:
   - Full dose of Phosphorus: DAP @ 180–200 kg/ha (75–80 kg/acre).
   - Half (1/2) Potash: MOP @ 100 kg/ha (40 kg/acre).
   - Half (1/2) Nitrogen: Urea @ 110 kg/ha (45 kg/acre). (Adjust for DAP nitrogen).
2. **Top Dressing (at First Earthing-Up, 25–30 DAS)**:
   - Apply remaining half Nitrogen: Neem-coated Urea @ 110 kg/ha (45 kg/acre).
   - Apply remaining half Potash: MOP @ 100 kg/ha (40 kg/acre), followed by immediate soil ridging and light irrigation.

## Irrigation Scheduling & Moisture Precision
Potato roots are shallow (top 30–40 cm) and sensitive to both drought and waterlogging:
1. **First Irrigation**: Light irrigation 4–6 days after planting if soil moisture was low during ridge making.
2. **Vegetative & Tuber Initiation (20–45 DAP)**: Irrigate every 7 to 10 days.
3. **Tuber Bulking Stage (45–75 DAP)**: Critical stage. Maintain uniform 65–70% field capacity moisture.
- **Ridge Submergence Warning**: Water in the furrows should strictly rise to only two-thirds (2/3rd) of the ridge height. **Never allow water to overtop the ridges**. Over-flooding causes tuber lenticels to swell into white warts, leading to bacterial soft rot.
- **Pre-Harvest Water Cut-Off**: Stop irrigation completely 10–12 days before harvest to allow the soil to dry and the tuber skin to cure.

## Dehaulming & Skin Curing
- **Dehaulming (*बेल काटना*)**: Cut the above-ground potato haulms (vines) at ground level with a sickle or haulm cutter 10–12 days before digging.
- **Benefits**:
  1. Arrests aphid-mediated virus transmission into tubers.
  2. Thickens and cures the outer tuber periderm (skin), preventing peeling and bruising during harvesting and cold store handling.

## Major Diseases & Triage
- **Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)**:
  - *Symptom*: Water-soaked, irregular blackish-brown spots on leaf tips and margins. In cool (12–18°C), cloudy, foggy weather with relative humidity >90%, white cottony fungal growth develops on the leaf underside. Entire fields can collapse within 4–5 days.
  - *Prophylaxis*: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0–2.5 g/litre before foggy weather sets in.
  - *Curative*: If symptoms appear, apply Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/litre under agricultural officer advice. See `diseases/potato_late_blight.md`.

## Farmer Decision Table
| Field Observation | Likely Cause | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green tubers visible on ridge sides | Sunlight exposure (insufficient earthing) | Immediately earth up soil around ridges to block light. |
| Water-soaked dark lesions on leaf tips with white cottony mold under leaves in foggy morning | Late Blight outbreak | Spray systemic fungicide (Metalaxyl + Mancozeb) immediately; suspend furrow flooding. |
| Tubers skin peeling easily during digging | Harvested without dehaulming / skin curing | Cut foliage 10–12 days before harvesting; allow tubers to cure in soil. |
| Rough corky scabs on tuber surface | Common Scab (*Streptomyces scabies*) in high pH alkaline soil | Maintain soil moisture during tuber initiation; avoid fresh uncomposted cow dung. |

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Aalu me dehaulming (bel katna) kyu zaroori hai? (Why is dehaulming necessary in potato?)**  
  *A*: Cutting potato vines 10–12 days before digging hardens the tuber skin (*chhilka pakka hota hai*), preventing skin peeling and bruising during transport and preventing late aphid virus infection into seed tubers.
- **Q: Aalu me late blight (jhulsa) se kaise bache? (How to prevent late blight in potato?)**  
  *A*: Keep a close watch on weather. When cool, cloudy, foggy weather with morning dew persists, apply a preventive spray of Mancozeb (2.5 g/L). Never flood the ridges, and avoid sprinkler irrigation during blight alerts.
