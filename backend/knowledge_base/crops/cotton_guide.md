# Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*) Production, Whitefly CLCuD & Pink Bollworm Guide (कपास की उन्नत खेती, सफेद मक्खी एवं गुलाबी सुंडी प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Cotton (*कपास / Gossypium hirsutum*) is cultivated as an important commercial cash fiber crop in the South-Western tract of Uttar Pradesh (**Aligarh, Mathura, Hathras, Agra, and Bulandshahr**). Maximizing seed-cotton yield (25–30 quintals/ha) under North Indian irrigated conditions requires early pre-monsoon planting, strict adherence to **Bt Cotton Refuge Crop guidelines** to prevent resistance breakdown, proactive vector control against the devastating **Cotton Leaf Curl Disease (CLCuD - *पत्ती मरोड़ रोग*)**, and intensive pheromone trap monitoring against the **Pink Bollworm (*गुलाबी सुंडी / Pectinophora gossypiella*)**.

---

## Sowing Window, Varietal Selection & Refuge Requirement

### 1. Optimal Sowing Window:
- **15 April to 15 May** (Pre-monsoon with pre-sowing irrigation / *palewa*).
- *Strict Caution*: Sowing delayed past **May 25** exposes young vegetative plants directly to peak whitefly populations in June–July, causing up to 80% incidence of Cotton Leaf Curl Virus and heavy squaring drop.

### 2. High-Yielding Hybrids & Varieties:
- Select approved **Bollgard II (BG-II)** cotton hybrids possessing dual *Cry1Ac* and *Cry2Ab* genes providing broad protection against American bollworm and spotted bollworm.
- Popular regional hybrids: **RCH-650 BG II**, **Bioseed-6588 BG II**, **Ankur-3028 BG II**.

### 3. The Non-Bt Refuge Planting Rule (Mandatory):
- To prevent Pink Bollworm and Helicoverpa from evolving genetic resistance to Bt endotoxins, farmers must maintain a **Refuge Crop**:
  - **Border Refuge**: Plant **5 outer border rows** of non-Bt cotton around the entire periphery of the Bt cotton plot; OR
  - **Refuge in Bag (RIB)**: Mix 5% non-Bt seed uniformly within the bag.
- *Never spray synthetic insecticides inside the refuge rows*, as susceptible moths emerging from non-Bt rows must mate with any rare surviving moths from Bt rows to dilute resistant alleles.

---

## Land Preparation, Spacing & Sowing Method

1. **Soil & Tillage**: Deep well-drained alluvial loams with good subsoil percolation (pH 7.0–8.2). Avoid saline/alkali soils with shallow water tables.
2. **Ridge-and-Furrow Planting**:
   - Spacing for Bt Hybrids: **90 cm to 100 cm row-to-row $\times$ 60 cm plant-to-plant** (Approx. 16,500 to 18,500 plants/ha).
   - Sow seeds on the shoulder of ridges at **3 to 4 cm depth**. Ridge planting prevents waterlogging around the root collar during monsoon downpours.

## Balanced Nutrient Management

Cotton has a long vegetative and reproductive overlap requiring balanced nutrition:

| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Application Method & Split Timing |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **120–150 kg/ha** | - 25% basal at sowing.<br>- 50% at square initiation (*दोडी बनते समय* 40–45 DAS).<br>- 25% at peak flowering (70–75 DAS). Avoid late Nitrogen to prevent excessive vegetative growth. |
| **Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$)** | **60 kg/ha** | 100% basal in furrows via Single Super Phosphate (SSP @ 375 kg/ha). |
| **Potassium ($K_2O$)** | **60 kg/ha** | 50% basal + 50% top-dressed at peak flowering. Crucial for boll size, fiber strength, and drought resistance. |
| **Magnesium Sulphate ($MgSO_4$)** | **25 kg/ha** | Basal broadcast or 2 foliar sprays of **1.0% $MgSO_4$** at 60 and 80 DAS. Eliminates physiological reddening of leaves (*पत्तियों का लाल होना*). |
| **Boron** | **1.0 kg/ha (Borax 10 kg/ha)** | Foliar spray of 0.1% Solubor at peak squaring to prevent flower bud and young boll shedding. |

## Critical Pests & Integrated Management

### 1. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci - सफेद मक्खी*) & Cotton Leaf Curl Disease (CLCuD)
- **Damage Mechanism**: Adults and nymphs suck sap from leaf undersides, secreting honeydew that fosters black sooty mold. Crucially, the whitefly is the sole vector transmitting the **Cotton Leaf Curl Begomovirus (CLCuD)**.
- **CLCuD Symptoms**: Upward curling of leaves, thickening of veins, and formation of leaf-like cup outgrowths (**enations**) on leaf undersides. Severe stunting leads to zero boll formation.
- **Vector Control Protocol**:
  - Install **Yellow Sticky Traps** @ 25–30 traps/ha.
  - Spray **Neem Oil (Azadirachtin 1500 ppm) @ 3.0 ml/L** at early nymphal presence.
  - Economic Threshold Level (ETL): **6 to 8 whitefly adults or nymphs per leaf**.

### 2. Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella - गुलाबी सुंडी*)
- **Damage Mechanism**: Young larvae bore into developing flower squares and young green bolls. Larvae spin silk that ties flower petals together, creating distinctive **"Rosette Flowers" (*गुलाब जैसा फूल*)**. Inside the green bolls, caterpillars feed on maturing lint and seeds, causing internal rotting and staining fiber yellow-brown.
- **Monitoring & IPM**:
  - Install **Delta Pheromone Traps (Gossyplure bait)** @ 5 traps/ha at 45 DAS.
  - ETL: **8 moths per trap per night for 3 consecutive nights** OR **10% green boll damage**.
  - Collect and destroy rosette flowers weekly during July–August.

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.

### CIBRC Approved Formulations for Cotton:
- **For Whiteflies, Jassids & Early Sucking Pests**:
  - **Diafenthiuron 50% WP** @ **600 g/ha (1.2 g/L water)**; OR
  - **Pyriproxyfen 10% EC** @ **1,000 ml/ha (2.0 ml/L water)** (Insect growth regulator against whitefly nymphs/eggs); OR
  - **Flonicamid 50% WG** @ **150–200 g/ha (0.3–0.4 g/L water)**.
- **For Pink Bollworm & Spodoptera on Green Bolls**:
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC** @ **150 ml/ha (0.3 ml/L water)**; OR
  - **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG** @ **220 g/ha (0.44 g/L water)**; OR
  - **Spinetoram 11.7% SC** @ **420 ml/ha (0.84 ml/L water)**.

## Clean Picking & Post-Harvest Storage

1. **Defoliation (*पत्तियां गिराना*)**: In high-density irrigated cotton, spray **Dropp / Thidiazuron 50% SC @ 200 ml/ha** when 60–70% bolls are open to drop foliage cleanly, accelerating uniform boll bursting and eliminating dry leaf trash in harvested lint.
2. **Picking Protocol**:
   - Pick cotton only after morning dew has completely evaporated (after 10:00 AM) to maintain fiber moisture below 8%.
   - Store harvested cotton in clean, dry cotton cloth bags. **Never use plastic/fertilizer woven sacks**, as shredded polypropylene filaments contaminate lint and cause severe mill deductions.
