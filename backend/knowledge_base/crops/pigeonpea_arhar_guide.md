# Pigeonpea / Arhar (*Cajanus cajan*) Production, Phytophthora Stem Blight & Pod Fly Guide (अरहर की उन्नत खेती, तना झुलसा एवं फली मक्खी प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Pigeonpea (*अरहर / तुअर / Cajanus cajan*) is the premier pulse crop of Uttar Pradesh, particularly in Eastern and Central plains where deep-rooted, long-duration varieties (**Bahar, Narendra Arhar-1**) yield 25–30 quintals/ha over a 240–270 day lifecycle. The crop enriches soil through biological nitrogen fixation and deep subsoil root aeration. However, two silent killers threaten pigeonpea profitability: **Phytophthora Stem Blight (*तना झुलसा रोग*)** during cloudy, waterlogged monsoon periods, and the **Pod Fly (*फली मक्खी / Melanagromyza obtusa*)**, whose internal maggots consume grains within green pods without any visible external pod hole. Securing high yields requires **ridge-and-furrow planting**, strict avoidance of water stagnation, resistant cultivars, and targeted pest sprays at early pod set.

## Variety Selection: Long Duration vs Short Duration

UP farmers must match variety selection to their cropping system:

| Cropping System | Variety Selection | Duration (Days) | Distinctive Agronomic Features | Yield Potential |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---:|
| **Eastern & Central UP (Sole Crop / Long Duration)** | **Bahar** | 240–260 | High branching, deep root system, field tolerant to sterility mosaic; highly popular in Varanasi/Mirzapur. | 25–30 q/ha |
| **Eastern UP (Wilt Resistant)** | **Narendra Arhar-1 (NDA-1)** | 240–250 | Bold brown seeds, high resistance to Fusarium wilt and Sterility Mosaic Disease (SMD). | 24–28 q/ha |
| **Alluvial Loam / Frost Tolerant** | **Amar (NDA-2)** | 250–270 | Compact plant type, high pod bearing on primary branches. | 26–32 q/ha |
| **Arhar-Wheat Rotation (Short Duration)** | **UPAS-120** & **Pusa-992** | 125–140 | Matures in October/November, vacating the field cleanly for timely wheat sowing by November 20. | 16–20 q/ha |

## Sowing Window, Ridge Planting & Seed Rate

### 1. Sowing Time:
- **15 June to 10 July** (immediately with pre-monsoon showers or first soaking rain).
- *Caution*: Late sowing of long-duration varieties after July 20 drastically reduces branching nodes and lowers grain yield by 20–30%.

### 2. Ridge Planting (*मेड़ों पर बुवाई*) - Mandatory for Drainage:
- Pigeonpea roots cannot tolerate standing water for even **24–36 hours**; water stagnation induces immediate root asphyxiation and triggers lethal *Phytophthora* stem blight.
- Sow seeds on **ridges 15–20 cm high** formed at:
  - **75 to 90 cm row spacing $\times$ 25 to 30 cm plant spacing** (Long duration: Bahar, NDA-1).
  - **60 cm row spacing $\times$ 15 cm plant spacing** (Short duration: UPAS-120).

### 3. Seed Rate & Dual Bio-Inoculation:
- Seed Rate: **12 to 15 kg/ha** for long duration; **18 to 20 kg/ha** for short duration.
- *FIR Treatment Protocol*:
  1. Fungicide: **Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.5 g/kg seed** or *Trichoderma viride* @ 8 g/kg.
  2. Biofertilizer: Inoculate with **Rhizobium (Pigeonpea strain) @ 5 g/kg seed** + **PSB @ 5 g/kg seed** using 10% jaggery syrup.

## Balanced Nutrient Management

Pigeonpea requires deep root exploration and starter nutrients:

| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Application Method & Timing |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **20–25 kg/ha** | Basal application as starter nitrogen. Avoid urea top-dressing during active growth. |
| **Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$)** | **50–60 kg/ha** | 100% basal placed 5 cm deep in furrows using **Single Super Phosphate (SSP @ 350 kg/ha)**. |
| **Potassium ($K_2O$)** | **30 kg/ha** | 100% basal via MOP (50 kg/ha) for drought tolerance and stalk strength. |
| **Sulphur (S)** | **20 kg/ha** | Provided automatically via SSP, or apply 150 kg/ha gypsum basal. Increases seed protein by 1.5–2.0%. |
| **Zinc Sulphate (33%)** | **15 kg/ha** | Basal broadcast in deficient soils. |

## Major Pests & Diseases Management

### 1. Phytophthora Stem Blight (*Phytophthora drechsleri f. sp. cajani - तना झुलसा*)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked lesions appear on stems and primary branches during warm, humid, rainy spells (July–August). Lesions turn dark brown to black and girdle the stem. The stem snaps easily at the lesion point, and foliage above withers while still attached.
- **Management**:
  - Plant on raised ridges to ensure rapid surface drainage.
  - Prophylactic seed treatment with **Metalaxyl 35% WS @ 3 g/kg seed**.
  - On early symptom detection, spray **Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ @ 2.5 g/L water)** directed at lower stem bases.

### 2. Pod Fly (*Melanagromyza obtusa - फली मक्खी*) — The Invisible Yield Robber
- **Damage Mechanism**: The adult fly lays microscopic eggs inside young, developing green pods. The emerging white apodous maggots feed internally by mining and grooving into the developing seeds. There is **zero external entry hole** on the pod wall until the adult fly cuts an exit "window" hole just before harvest.
- **Loss**: Destroys 30–60% of harvested grain weight, causing severe bitterness and high rejection at processing mills.
- **Management**:
  - Two mandatory targeted sprays: **1st spray at 50% pod formation**, **2nd spray 15 days later**.

### 3. Gram Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera - फली छेदक*)
- **Symptoms**: Large round circular holes bored into green pods; caterpillar feeds with its head inside the pod and body outside.
- **Management**: Install bird perches (T-poles) @ 40/ha; spray **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 150 ml/ha (0.3 ml/L)** at early instar larvae stage.

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.

### CIBRC Approved Formulations for Pigeonpea:
- **For Pod Fly (*Melanagromyza obtusa*) & Pod Borers at Pod Setting**:
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC** @ **150 ml/ha (0.3 ml/L water)** in 500 L water (PHI: 29 days); OR
  - **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG** @ **220 g/ha (0.44 g/L water)**; OR
  - **Indoxacarb 14.5% SC** @ **350–400 ml/ha (0.75 ml/L water)**.
- *Notice*: Monocrotophos is strictly prohibited/restricted on vegetable and pulse food crops.
