# Chilli (*Capsicum annuum*) Production, Murda Leaf Curl Complex & Anthracnose Guide (मिर्च की उन्नत खेती, मुरड़ा रोग एवं फल सड़न प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Chilli (*मिर्च / Capsicum annuum*) is an intensive, high-value commercial vegetable and cash spice cultivated widely across Uttar Pradesh (Varanasi, Jaunpur, Mirzapur, Bareilly, and Barabanki). High profitability requires harvesting 150–200 quintals/ha of green chillies or 25–30 quintals/ha of dry red chillies. The two foremost agronomic threats that cause massive crop abandonment are the **Murda Leaf Curl Complex (*मुरड़ा / कुकड़ा रोग*)**—a devastating syndrome induced by **Thrips, Yellow Mites, and Whitefly-borne Gemini viruses**—and **Anthracnose Fruit Rot / Dieback (*फल सड़न एवं डाई-बैक*)**. Managing these challenges requires raised nursery beds, silver-black plastic mulching with drip fertigation, precise differential diagnosis between thrips and mites, and rotational chemical protection.

## Seasonal Windows & Recommended High-Yielding Cultivars

| Season | Nursery Sowing | Field Transplanting | Harvest Window | Recommended Cultivars |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Kharif (Monsoon)** | May 15 to June 15 | July to August | October – January | **Kashi Anmol**, **Kashi Tej**, **Pant C-1**, **Pusa Jwala** |
| **Spring-Summer** | November 15 to December 15 (Poly-tunnel protected) | January to February | April – June | **Kashi Anmol**, **Kashi Ratna**, **Surajmukhi** |

- **Kashi Anmol**: Released by ICAR-IIVR Varanasi; semi-erect, high fruit pungency, field tolerant to Chilli Leaf Curl Virus (ChiLCV).
- **Pant C-1**: Highly tolerant to mosaic and anthracnose; medium pungent fruits.

## Nursery Raising & Raised Bed Transplanting

1. **Protected Nursery on Raised Beds**:
   - Seed rate: **800 to 1,000 g/ha** (open pollinated varieties); **200 to 250 g/ha** (hybrids).
   - Sow on raised beds (15 cm high $\times$ 1.0 m wide) covered with **40-mesh nylon insect-proof net** to prevent early whitefly and thrips vector transmission of leaf curl virus.
   - Seed treatment: Slurry coat with **Trichoderma viride @ 8–10 g/kg seed** or **Thiram 75% WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed**.
2. **Plastic Mulch & Drip Transplanting**:
   - Transplant healthy 35–40 day old seedlings at **60 cm row-to-row $\times$ 45 cm plant-to-plant spacing** on raised broad beds (90 cm bed top).
   - Use **25-micron Silver-Black Polyethylene Mulch film**:
     - The silver reflective surface repels incoming winged thrips and aphids by 60–70%.
     - Conserves 40% soil moisture and eliminates hand weeding completely.

## Balanced Nutrition & Fertigation Schedule

Chilli produces continuous flushes of flowers and fruits over 4 to 6 months, requiring split nutrient feeding:

| Nutrient | Total Dose (kg/ha) | Application Method |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **120–150 kg/ha** | 30% basal; remaining 70% supplied in weekly fertigation splits from 15 DAT to 120 DAT. |
| **Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$)** | **60–80 kg/ha** | 100% basal in bed bottoms via SSP (375–500 kg/ha) or applied as 12:61:0 (MAP) through drip. |
| **Potassium ($K_2O$)** | **80–100 kg/ha** | 40% basal via MOP/SOP; remaining 60% applied as 0:0:50 during fruit bulking to boost pungency and red capsaicin luster. |
| **Calcium Nitrate & Boron** | **15 kg/ha + 5 kg/ha** | Apply through drip at flowering (45–60 DAT) or 2 foliar sprays of **0.5% Calcium Nitrate + 0.1% Solubor** to prevent blossom drop and fruit rot. |

## Differential Diagnosis: The Murda Leaf Curl Complex

Farmers frequently spray the wrong pesticide because thrips damage and mite damage look superficially similar. Use this differential table before spraying:

| Diagnostic Feature | Chilli Thrips (*Scirtothrips dorsalis*) | Yellow Mite (*Polyphagotarsonemus latus*) | Leaf Curl Gemini Virus (Whitefly Vector) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Leaf Curling Direction** | **Upward Curling (*नाव की तरह ऊपर की ओर मुड़ना*)** resembling a boat or cup. | **Downward Curling (*उल्टे प्याले की तरह नीचे की ओर मुड़ना*)** resembling an inverted umbrella. | Irregular puckering, severe vein thickening, stunting of entire crown. |
| **Leaf Texture & Color** | Silvery-white scratches on leaf underside; brown corky lines on leaf veins. | Leaves turn deep dark green, leathery, brittle, and develop a bronze sheen on underside. | Mosaic green-yellow mottling; leaves remain thin and wrinkled. |
| **Location on Plant** | Prefers young top tender shoots and open flower buds. | Prefers slightly older leaves on lower and middle canopy under shade. | Systemic infection across all branches from crown downward. |
| **Actionable Spray** | Fipronil or Spinetoram (Insecticide) | Spiromesifen or Diafenthiuron (Acaricide/Miticide) | Rogue out infected plants + spray Acetamiprid/Thiamethoxam for Whitefly |

## Anthracnose Fruit Rot & Dieback (*Colletotrichum capsici*)

- **Dieback Phase**: Twigs begin drying from the tip downwards (*डाई-बैक*). The dry twigs turn grayish-white with minute black dot-like fruiting bodies (*acervuli*).
- **Fruit Rot Phase**: Sunken, circular or oblong dark brown water-soaked lesions appear on ripe red fruits. Lesions develop concentric rings of pink/salmon-colored fungal spore masses. Fruits shrivel and bleach dirty white in storage.
- **Control Strategy**:
  - Collect and burn dry diseased twigs during pruning.
  - Prophylactic spray at flowering: **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L** OR **Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L**.
  - On fruit rot symptoms: Spray **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L** OR **Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml/L**.

## Statutory Chemical Safety Gate

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.

### CIBRC Approved Formulations for Chilli:
- **For Thrips & Yellow Mite Complex (Murda Complex)**:
  - **Diafenthiuron 50% WP** @ **600 g/ha (1.2 g/L water)** (Controls both thrips and broad mites simultaneously); OR
  - **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC** @ **400 ml/ha (0.8 ml/L water)** (Specialized miticide against yellow mite; PHI: 5 days); OR
  - **Fipronil 5% SC** @ **1,000 ml/ha (2.0 ml/L water)** (Against thrips).
- **For Anthracnose Fruit Rot & Dieback**:
  - **Azoxystrobin 23% SC** @ **500 ml/ha (1.0 ml/L water)** (PHI: 3 days); OR
  - **Difenoconazole 25% EC** @ **250 ml/ha (0.5 ml/L water)**.
