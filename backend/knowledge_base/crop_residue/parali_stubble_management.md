# In-Situ & Ex-Situ Crop Residue (Parali) Management Guide (फसल अवशेष एवं पराली प्रबंधन की वैज्ञानिक विधियां)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Burning paddy stubble (*पराली जलाना*) during the tight 15 to 20 day window between rice harvest and wheat sowing in October–November is an enormous economic and ecological loss. Every ton of burnt paddy straw destroys **5.5 kg of Nitrogen, 2.3 kg of Phosphorus, 25 kg of Potash, 1.2 kg of Sulphur, and 400 kg of valuable Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)**, while incinerating billions of beneficial soil microbes and polluting North India's winter air. By adopting **In-Situ mechanization (Super Seeder, Happy Seeder, Pusa Bio-Decomposer)** or **Ex-Situ baling**, farmers conserve ₹2,500 worth of nutrients per acre, improve soil moisture retention, suppress winter weeds, and qualify for government machinery subsidies under the Central Crop Residue Management (CRM) scheme.

## Nutrient Value Preserved per Ton of In-Situ Residue
| Nutrient Element | Quantity Retained per Ton of Straw | Soil Fertility Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | **5.5 kg** | Converted into slow-release organic nitrogen over time. |
| **Phosphorus (P2O5)** | **2.3 kg** | Feeds soil microorganisms and secondary roots. |
| **Potassium (K2O)** | **25.0 kg** | **Massive Potash gain**: Rice straw is exceptionally rich in potassium; recycling it drastically cuts MOP fertilizer demand. |
| **Sulphur (S)** | **1.2 kg** | Essential for succeeding wheat and mustard crops. |
| **Soil Organic Carbon** | **350 – 400 kg** | Builds soil water holding capacity and buffers against terminal heat. |

## 1. In-Situ Management Machinery (खेत में पराली प्रबंधन)

### A. Super Seeder (*सुपर सीडर*)
- **Working Principle**: Combines a rotary tiller with a seed-cum-fertilizer drill. In a single tractor pass, it cuts standing and loose paddy stubble, finely chops and incorporates it into the top 5–7 cm of soil, and simultaneously drills wheat seeds and basal fertilizer at precise depth, followed by a press roller.
- **Tractor Power Requirement**: Requires a **50 to 60 HP tractor** with double clutch and 4-wheel drive capability.
- **Agronomic Advantage**: Eliminates the need for 3–4 pre-sowing tillage passes, saving ₹1,500/acre in diesel while creating an ideal firm seedbed.

### B. Happy Seeder / Smart Seeder (*हैप्पी सीडर*)
- **Working Principle**: Sows wheat seed directly into standing stubble without tilling the soil. Flail blades cut the straw ahead of the sowing tines and deposit the chopped straw as a uniform **surface mulch (*सतही पलवार*)** behind the drill.
- **Tractor Requirement**: Can be operated with standard **45 to 50 HP tractors**.
- **Mulch Advantage**: The surface straw mulch conserves soil moisture (saving the 1st pre-sowing irrigation), keeps the soil 2°C cooler in March (mitigating terminal heatwave shock), and smothers the emergence of **Phalaris minor (*गुल्ली डंडा*)** weeds by blocking sunlight.

### C. Pusa Bio-Decomposer (*पूसा बायो-डीकंपोजर - ICAR-IARI*)
- **Microbial Formulation**: A consortium of 7 fungal species (*Aspergillus, Trichoderma, Chaetomium*) engineered by ICAR-IARI that produce cellulase and ligninase enzymes, rapidly decomposing tough paddy straw into rich humus.
- **Preparation & Application Protocol (for 1 Hectare / 2.5 Acres)**:
  1. Boil 150 grams of jaggery (*गुड़*) in 5 litres of water; allow to cool.
  2. Mix 50 grams of chickpea flour (*बेसन*) and 4 capsules of Pusa Decomposer into the solution.
  3. Keep in a warm shaded room for 4–5 days until a thick fungal mat forms on top.
  4. Dilute this mother culture into 200 litres of water per acre.
  5. Spray evenly onto shredded paddy residue immediately following combine harvest, incorporate with a single rotavator pass, and give a light irrigation. Residue decomposes in **20 to 25 days**.

## 2. Ex-Situ Management (खेत के बाहर पराली का उपयोग)

### Straw Balers (*स्ट्रॉ बेलर*)
- Automatic tractor-mounted square or round balers collect loose combine straw, compacting it into dense, transportable **15 to 25 kg rectangular bales** or **250 kg large round rolls**.
- **Commercial Buyers for Paddy Bales**:
  1. **Thermal Power Plants (NTPC)**: Mandated by the Ministry of Power to co-fire 5% to 10% biomass pellets with coal.
  2. **2G Bio-Ethanol Refineries**: Facilities such as the Indian Oil 2G Ethanol Plant in Panipat purchase bales @ ₹1,500 to ₹2,000 per ton.
  3. **Packaging, Cardboard & Paper Mills**: Replace wood pulp with compressed straw.
  4. **Commercial Mushroom Growers**: Use pasteurized straw bales as the growing substrate for Button and Oyster mushrooms.

## 3. Crop Residue Management (CRM) Scheme Subsidies in UP

Under the Central Sector Scheme for In-Situ Residue Management in Punjab, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh:
- **Individual Farmer Subsidy**: **50% capital subsidy** on the purchase of approved machinery (Super Seeder, Happy Seeder, Straw Baler, Shrub Master, Zero Till Drill).
- **Custom Hiring Centers (CHCs) & FPOs**: **Up to 80% project subsidy** (maximum project cost ₹15 lakh) to establish village-level Custom Hiring Centers where small and marginal farmers can hire machinery at low hourly rates.
- **Application Process**: Apply online through the UP Agriculture Portal (**`upagriculture.com`**) during the annual booking window (typically July–September).

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Super Seeder aur Happy Seeder me kya mukhya antar hai? (Main difference between Super Seeder and Happy Seeder?)**  
  *A*: Super Seeder mixes the stubble into the soil with a rotavator while sowing, leaving a clean, bare soil surface (requires 55+ HP tractor). Happy Seeder does not till the soil; it deposits the chopped straw on top of the soil as a protective mulch blanket (operates on 45 HP tractor and conserves moisture).
- **Q: Kya parali ko khet me milane se gehu me deemak (termites) lagti hai? (Does incorporating straw cause termites in wheat?)**  
  *A*: Only in dry, sandy soils if the residue remains unmixed and dry. If properly incorporated with adequate soil moisture (*ओट*) and wheat seed is treated with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC or Fipronil 5% SC, termites do not attack. In fact, decomposed straw builds soil organic carbon that retains moisture.
