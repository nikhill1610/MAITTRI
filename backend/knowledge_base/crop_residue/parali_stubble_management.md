# Crop Residue (Parali) Management (फसल अवशेष एवं पराली प्रबंधन)

## In-Situ Residue Management Machinery (खेत में ही पराली प्रबंधन)
In-situ management incorporates or retains paddy straw directly on the field without burning, enriching soil organic matter and moisture:
1. **Super Seeder (सुपर सीडर)**:
   - Integrates rotary rotavator with seed-cum-fertilizer drill.
   - Operates directly in standing paddy stubble, simultaneously chopping residue, blending it into the soil, and sowing wheat in a single pass. Saves diesel and time.
2. **Happy Seeder / Smart Seeder (हैप्पी सीडर)**:
   - Cuts standing stubble, deposits chopped straw as surface mulch across rows, and places wheat seed and fertilizer cleanly into the moist soil underneath.
   - Surface mulch retains soil moisture, regulates soil temperature, and suppresses winter weeds (Phalaris minor / गुल्ली डंडा).
3. **PUSA Bio-Decomposer (पूसा बायो-डीकंपोजर)**:
   - Microbial fungal consortium developed by ICAR-IARI (contains 7 cellulolytic fungal strains).
   - Rate: Spray 4 capsules dissolved in 200L water with 150g jaggery and 50g chickpea flour, or ready liquid formulation @ 300 ml/acre after light rotavator pass.
   - Decomposes tough ligno-cellulose paddy straw into nutrient-rich organic humus within 20 to 25 days.

## Ex-Situ Management & Economic Utilization (खेत से बाहर उपयोग)
- **Straw Balers**: Compress loose straw into dense square/round bales (15-25 kg each).
- **Industrial Buyers**:
  - Biomass pellet plants for coal blending in thermal power stations (NTPC mandated 5-10% co-firing).
  - 2G Bio-ethanol refineries (e.g., IOCL Panipat 2G Bioethanol plant).
  - Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) under SATAT scheme.
  - Animal fodder, mushroom cultivation bedding, and paper/cardboard manufacturing.

## Government Machinery Subsidies (CRM Scheme)
Under the Central Crop Residue Management (CRM) Scheme:
- **Individual Farmers**: Eligible for **50% subsidy** on purchase of Happy Seeder, Super Seeder, Straw Chopper, and Balers.
- **Custom Hiring Centers (CHCs) / Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)**: Eligible for **80% financial assistance** up to ₹10-15 lakh project cost.
