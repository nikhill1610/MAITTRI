# Management of Phalaris minor (Gulli Danda / Canary Grass) in Wheat (गेहूं में गुल्ली डंडा / मंदूसी नियंत्रण)

## Farmer-friendly summary
*Phalaris minor* (known locally as **Gulli Danda (*गुल्ली डंडा*)**, **Mandusi (*मंदूसी*)**, **Kanki**, or **Gehunsa**) is the single most destructive grassy weed infesting irrigated wheat in the Indo-Gangetic Plains. Because its young seedlings look nearly identical to wheat, farmers often fail to detect it until heavy tillering, by which time it has absorbed vital moisture and fertilizer, causing **30% to 80% yield loss**. A single weed plant produces over 10,000–30,000 seeds that mature and shatter right before wheat harvest. Because *Phalaris* has developed widespread resistance to older herbicides like Isoproturon and partial resistance to Clodinafop, successful control requires an **Integrated Weed Management (IWM)** strategy combining clean seed, crop rotation, timely pre-emergence sprays, and proper post-emergence herbicide rotation.

---

## 1. Morphological Identification & The "Pink Stem" Test (पहचान के लक्षण)

During early vegetative stages (15–30 days after sowing), *Phalaris minor* mimics wheat seedlings closely. Farmers can accurately distinguish them using these specific morphological markers:

| Diagnostic Feature | *Phalaris minor* (Gulli Danda) | Wheat Seedling (*Triticum aestivum*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Basal Stem Node Color** | **Distinct pink to violet/reddish pigmentation** when pulled out and gently scraped at the crown root junction. | Whitish-green, never bright pink. |
| **Ligule & Auricles** | Long, prominent, translucent membranous ligule; **auricles (hairy claw-like appendages) completely absent**. | Prominent hairy collar auricles hugging the stem. |
| **Leaf Texture & Color** | Narrower, paler light-green foliage; smoother texture with upright habit. | Broader, dark bluish-green leaves with subtle pubescence. |
| **Crushed Sap** | Sap pressed between fingers reveals clear, watery residue. | Standard grassy sap. |

## 2. Integrated Cultural & Agronomic Management (कृषि एवं भौतिक नियंत्रण)

Chemical herbicides alone cannot sustainably control resistant *Phalaris minor*. Cultural practices dramatically deplete the soil weed seed bank:

1. **Crop Rotation (फसल चक्र)**:
   - Breaking the continuous Rice-Wheat monoculture by rotating with **Potato**, **Mustard**, **Sugarcane**, or fodder crops like **Berseem** for just 1–2 seasons reduces *Phalaris* seed germination by **80% to 90%**, as weed seedlings are destroyed during potato earthing or berseem repeated cuttings.
2. **Stale Seedbed Technique (*डब पद्धति*)**:
   - Apply a light pre-sowing irrigation (*paleva*). Allow weeds to flush over 10–14 days, then destroy them with a light shallow harrowing or non-selective knockdown herbicide before wheat drilling without disturbing deep soil seeds.
3. **Zero-Tillage / Happy Seeder Sowing**:
   - Sowing directly into retained paddy straw mulch using Happy Seeder or Super Seeder suppresses *Phalaris minor* germination by **40% to 60%** due to straw light-exclusion and physical impedance.
4. **Higher Seed Rate & Dense Row Spacing**:
   - Increase wheat seed rate by 15–20% (125 kg/ha instead of 100 kg/ha) and reduce line spacing to 18 cm or adopt bidirectional criss-cross sowing. A rapid, dense crop canopy smothers emerging weed seedlings.

## 3. Chemical Herbicide Management & Label Compliance (रासायनिक खरपतवार नियंत्रण)

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY SAFETY GATE**: Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local state agricultural authorities. Always apply with a **flat-fan or flood-jet nozzle** using 150–200 litres of clean water per acre under calm wind conditions. Never use hollow cone nozzles designed for insecticides.

### Option A: Pre-Emergence Herbicide (बुवाई के 0–3 दिन के अंदर)
Apply immediately after sowing before crop and weed emergence in presence of adequate seedbed moisture:

- **Pendimethalin 30% EC**:
  - **Dose**: 1,000 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **3.3 litres/ha** or **1.3 litres/acre**) diluted in 200 litres water.
  - *Alternative Formulation*: **Pendimethalin 38.7% CS** @ **700 ml/acre** in 200 L water.
  - **Action**: Forms a chemical vapor barrier on the soil surface inhibiting cell division in germinating weed radicles and plumules.

### Option B: Post-Emergence Herbicides (बुवाई के 30–35 दिन बाद, प्रथम सिंचाई उपरांत)
Apply at 2–3 leaf stage of weeds (typically 3–5 days after the Crown Root Initiation first irrigation when soil moisture is optimal):

- **For Grassy Weed Dominance (*Phalaris minor* & Wild Oat)**:
  - **Clodinafop-propargyl 15% WP**:
    - **Dose**: 60 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **400 g/ha** or **160 g/acre**) in 150 litres water.
    - *Target*: Highly selective ACCase inhibitor.
  - **Pinoxaden 5.1% EC** (For Clodinafop-resistant biotypes):
    - **Dose**: 40 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **800–900 ml/ha** or **350–400 ml/acre**) in 150–200 litres water.
    - *Action*: Highly effective against multi-resistant canary grass populations.

- **For Mixed Infestation (*Phalaris minor* + Broadleaf Weeds like Bathua, Chenopodium)**:
  - **Sulfosulfuron 75% WG**:
    - **Dose**: 25 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **33.3 g/ha** or **13.5 g/acre**) mixed with companion surfactant in 150 litres water.
    - *Precaution*: Do not sow sorghum, maize, or mustard in succession after sulfosulfuron due to residual soil carryover.
  - **Clodinafop-propargyl 15% + Metsulfuron-methyl 1% WP (Premix)**:
    - **Dose**: **160 g/acre** in 150 litres water.

## 4. Herbicide Resistance Mitigation Rules (प्रतिरोधक क्षमता से बचाव के नियम)

To prevent *Phalaris minor* from developing complete resistance:
1. **Never under-dose or over-dose**: Sub-lethal doses accelerate metabolic resistance mutations.
2. **Rotate Chemical Modes of Action (MOA)**: Never use Clodinafop continuously for more than 2 consecutive years; alternate with Pinoxaden, Metribuzin combinations, or pre-emergence Pendimethalin.
3. **Calibrate Equipment**: Ensure uniform boom height (45–50 cm above canopy) to avoid skipped strips and overlapping double doses.
4. **Prevent Seed Shedding**: Hand-rogue any surviving weed escapes in February–March before their seed heads ripen and shatter onto the field.
