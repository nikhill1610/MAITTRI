# Management of Echinochloa Weeds (Barnyard Grass / Sanwa / Shama) in Rice (धान में सावां / शमा खरपतवार नियंत्रण)

## Farmer-friendly summary
*Echinochloa* species—chiefly **Barnyard Grass (*Echinochloa crus-galli* / सावां / Sanwa)** and **Jungle Rice (*Echinochloa colona* / शमा / Shama / Sawank)**—are the most destructive grassy weeds affecting rice production in India. In Direct Seeded Rice (DSR), uncontrolled *Echinochloa* can cause up to **50% to 80% yield loss**, while in puddled transplanted rice, it deprives crops of up to 40 kg of nitrogen per acre. Young *Echinochloa* seedlings mimic rice so perfectly that manual weeding often misses them. However, they can be easily distinguished by the **complete absence of a ligule and auricles** at the leaf collar. Effective management requires combining proper water ponding, mechanical weeding, and timely herbicide application.

---

## 1. Morphological Diagnostic: The "Collar Ligule" Identification Rule (पहचान के अचूक लक्षण)

To distinguish *Echinochloa* from true paddy seedlings at the 2 to 4-leaf stage before tillering:

| Plant Trait | *Echinochloa* species (Sanwa / Shama) | Rice Seedling (*Oryza sativa*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ligule (झिल्ली / पत्ती का जोड़)** | **Completely Absent**. The leaf blade merges smoothly into the sheath without any membranous flap. | **Prominent, membranous ligule** (whitish collar flap, 2–15 mm long). |
| **Auricles (कान जैसी मूंछें)** | **Completely Absent**. No hairs or clasping structures at the collar junction. | **Distinct hairy auricles** (claw-like appendages) clasping around the stem. |
| **Leaf Sheath & Midrib** | Often exhibits a reddish-purple tint at the base; prominent whitish central midrib. | Solid green or pale yellowish-green sheath base. |
| **Growth Habit** | More spreading, aggressively tillering, and rapidly outpaces rice height within 30 days. | More upright and compact tillering habit. |

---

## 2. Cultural & Water Management Control (कृषि एवं जल प्रबंधन)

Water is the most potent and economical natural weed control tool in transplanted rice:

1. **Standing Water Layer in Transplanted Paddy**:
   - Maintain a continuous shallow standing water layer of **2 to 5 cm for the first 15 to 20 days after transplanting (DAT)**.
   - *Scientific Principle*: *Echinochloa* seeds require oxygen to germinate; anaerobic conditions beneath 3–5 cm standing water suppress *Echinochloa* seed germination by **80% to 90%**.
2. **Stale Seedbed in Direct Seeded Rice (DSR)**:
   - Irrigate the field 10–14 days prior to planned sowing. Allow the first flush of weed seeds to germinate, then destroy them with shallow rotavator tilling or a non-selective knockdown herbicide before drilling rice seed.
3. **Mechanical Cono-Weeding (*कोनो-वीडर*)**:
   - In line-transplanted or drum-seeded rice, run a mechanical rotary weeder / cono-weeder at 15–20 DAT and repeat at 35 DAT. This buries emerging weed seedlings into the mud and aerates rice root zones.

## 3. Chemical Herbicide Management & Label Compliance (रासायनिक खरपतवार प्रबंधन)

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY SAFETY GATE**: Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application. Apply with a **flat-fan or flood-jet nozzle** using 150–200 litres of water per acre.

### A. For Transplanted Puddled Rice (रोपाई वाले धान में)

#### 1. Pre-Emergence Herbicide (रोपाई के 0 से 3 दिन के भीतर)
Apply when standing water (2–3 cm) is present in the field:
- **Pretilachlor 50% EC**:
  - **Dose**: 500–750 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **1.0 to 1.5 litres/ha** or **400 to 600 ml/acre**).
  - **Application**: Mix thoroughly with 20–25 kg dry sand and broadcast evenly across the standing water, or spray evenly. Maintain water for 4–5 days without draining.
- **Pyrazosulfuron-ethyl 10% WP**:
  - **Dose**: 20–25 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **200–250 g/ha** or **80–100 g/acre**) broadcast with sand or sprayed on 2–3 cm standing water at 3–5 DAT. Controls *Echinochloa*, broadleaf weeds, and sedges.

#### 2. Post-Emergence Herbicide (रोपाई के 15 से 25 दिन बाद)
Apply at 2 to 4-leaf stage of weeds when pre-emergence herbicide was missed or weed escapes occur:
- **Bispyribac-sodium 10% SC**:
  - **Dose**: 25 g a.i./ha (equivalent to **250 ml/ha** or **100 ml/acre**) diluted in 120–150 litres clean water.
  - **Application Technique**: **Drain standing water completely from the field before spraying** so that weed leaves are fully exposed to the spray droplets. **Re-irrigate and pond water 48 hours after application** and hold for 5–7 days to maximize weed mortality.

---

### B. For Direct Seeded Rice (DSR / सीधी बिजाई वाले धान में)

In Direct Seeded Rice, *Echinochloa* emerges concurrently with rice seedlings, making timing critical:

#### 1. Pre-Emergence (बुवाई के 0 से 2 दिन के भीतर)
- **Pendimethalin 30% EC**: Apply @ **3.3 litres/ha** (**1.3 litres/acre**) or **Pendimethalin 38.7% CS @ 700 ml/acre** in 200 litres water onto moist seedbed immediately after drilling rice seed.

#### 2. Early Post-Emergence (बुवाई के 15 से 22 दिन बाद, 2–4 पत्ती अवस्था)
- **Bispyribac-sodium 10% SC @ 100 ml/acre** OR **Cyhalofop-butyl 10% EC @ 400 ml/acre** (specifically targeted at grassy weeds without any broadleaf injury).
- **Penoxsulam 24% SC**: Apply @ **100–125 ml/ha** (**40–50 ml/acre**) in 150 litres water for broad-spectrum grassy and broadleaf control.

---

## 4. Herbicide Stewardship & Safety Rules

1. **Avoid Sub-Lethal Dosages**: Always measure doses accurately with measuring cylinders; lower concentrations trigger metabolic resistance in *Echinochloa*.
2. **Do Not Spray on Drought-Stressed Weeds**: Weeds under severe water stress close their stomata and thicken leaf cuticles, causing poor herbicide translocation.
3. **Nozzle Calibration**: Use a knapsack sprayer fitted with a flood-jet (WJG) or flat-fan (deflector) nozzle to deliver an even, non-drifting spray sheet.
