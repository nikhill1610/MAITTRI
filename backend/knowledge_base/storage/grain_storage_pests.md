# Safe Grain Storage, Pest Management (Ghun / Pulse Beetle) & Moisture Control (सुरक्षित अनाज भंडारण एवं कीट नियंत्रण)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Post-harvest grain losses in India range between **10% and 15%** of total production, with stored-grain insects like the Rice Weevil (*घुन / Sitophilus oryzae*) in cereals and the Pulse Beetle (*ढोरा / Callosobruchus*) in pulses causing severe quantitative weight loss, grain hollowing, and fungal aflatoxin contamination. The cornerstone of scientific storage is reducing grain moisture to **below 10–12%** before bagging. Chemical-free modern innovations—such as **triple-layer hermetic bags (PICS bags)**—suffocate insect pests by depleting oxygen without using hazardous chemicals. For large-scale godowns, statutory safety protocols must govern chemical fumigation.

---

## 1. Primary Stored Grain Pests & Damage Profiles (प्रमुख कीट एवं पहचान)

Stored grain insect pests are categorized into primary borers (capable of boring into sound, whole grains) and secondary pests (feeding on cracked grains and flour dust):

| Common Name & Hindi Term | Scientific Name | Target Grains | Diagnostic Damage Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rice / Wheat Weevil (*घुन*)** | *Sitophilus oryzae* | Wheat, Paddy, Maize, Sorghum | Adults possess a prominent slender snout (proboscis). Larvae develop entirely inside the grain kernel, hollowing it out and leaving characteristic circular exit holes (*दाने खोखले हो जाना*). |
| **Lesser Grain Borer (*सूंडी / बोरर*)** | *Rhyzopertha dominica* | Wheat, Barley, Paddy | Adults bore into grains producing large quantities of fine, powdery white flour dust at the bottom of bins. |
| **Pulse Beetle / Dhora (*ढोरा / दालों का घुन*)** | *Callosobruchus chinensis / C. maculatus* | Chickpea, Pigeonpea, Lentil, Moong, Urad | Tiny white cylindrical eggs glued to seed coats. Emerging larvae bore directly inside, feeding on cotyledons and leaving perfectly round emergence holes. |
| **Khapra Beetle (*खपरा भृंग*)** | *Trogoderma granarium* | Wheat, Maize | Brownish, densely hairy larvae. Highly resistant to drought and low humidity; attacks germ portion of seeds. |
| **Red Flour Beetle (*आटा सुंडी*)** | *Tribolium castaneum* | Broken grains, flour, bran | Secondary pest; produces pungent, unpalatable odor due to quinone secretions. |

## 2. The Critical Rule of Grain Moisture Control (नमी नियंत्रण का स्वर्णिम नियम)

High grain moisture combined with tropical temperatures is the root cause of insect multiplication and fungal spoilage (*Aspergillus flavus* producing carcinogenic aflatoxins):

| Commodity | Maximum Safe Storage Moisture Level (%) |
|---|---|
| **Wheat (*गेहूं*)** | **$\le 10.0\% - 12.0\%$** |
| **Paddy / Rice (*धान / चावल*)** | **$\le 12.0\% - 13.0\%$** |
| **Maize (*मक्का*)** | **$\le 11.0\% - 12.0\%$** |
| **Pulses / Dals (*चना, अरहर, मसूर*)** | **$\le 9.0\% - 10.0\%$** |
| **Oilseeds (*सरसों, मूंगफली*)** | **$\le 7.0\% - 8.0\%$** |

### Farmer's Rapid Field Moisture Check (दांत से दबाने का परीक्षण):
- Take 4–5 dried grains and crush them between the front teeth. 
- If the grain produces a **sharp, audible crack sound (*कट की आवाज़*)**, moisture is below 12% and safe for storage.
- If the grain dents, flattens, or chews softly, it retains excess moisture (>14%) and must undergo further sun-drying for 2–3 full days on clean canvas tarpaulins.

## 3. Preventive Hygiene & Storage Bin Preparation (भंडारण पात्र की सफाई)

1. **Clean Bins Thoroughly**: Remove all residual grains, dust, and cobwebs from bins, silos, or rooms. Old residual grains harbor latent weevil populations.
2. **Sun Drying**: Dry cleaned grains on concrete floors or plastic sheets for 3 consecutive days under bright midday sun (canopy temperatures exceed 45°C, destroying insect eggs and larvae).
3. **Plastering / White Washing**: Seal wall cracks, crevices, and rodent holes with cement or lime whitewash (*कली की पुताई*).
4. **Surface Disinfection**: Spray empty godown walls and floors (before grain loading) with **Malathion 50% EC @ 30 ml/litre** of water. **CRITICAL**: Never spray Malathion directly onto grain meant for human consumption.

---

## 4. Modern Hermetic Storage Technology: PICS Bags (पिक्स बैग - बिना दवा सुरक्षित भंडारण)

Purdue Improved Crop Storage (PICS) bags are a proven, chemical-free technology developed for smallholder farmers:

- **Construction**: Consists of three sealed layers—two internal high-density polyethylene (HDPE) hermetic liners (80 microns thick each) encased in an outer woven polypropylene protective sack.
- **Scientific Mechanism**: When dry grains are loaded and the inner liners are tied tightly, the respiration of remaining insects, microbes, and grains consumes the trapped oxygen, dropping $O_2$ levels from 21% to **below 1% within 5–10 days**, while elevating $CO_2$.
- **Result**: All life stages of weevils and pulse beetles (eggs, larvae, pupae, adults) die of asphyxiation and desiccation. Grains remain insect-free for over **12 to 24 months** without a single drop of chemical insecticide.
- **Guidelines for Use**:
  - Dry grains strictly below 10–12% moisture before loading.
  - Fill bag firmly to eliminate excess headspace air.
  - Tie each of the two inner plastic liners separately and securely with strong twine, folding the neck over (*swan-neck tie*), then tie the outer woven sack.
  - Store off the floor on wooden pallets (*chawki*) away from rodents.

## 5. Traditional Non-Chemical Grain Protectants (पारंपरिक जैविक उपचार)

For small household quantities of seed or food grains:
1. **Neem Leaf Treatment**: Mix well-dried neem leaves (*Azadirachta indica*) @ **2% to 3% by weight** (2–3 kg dried leaves per 100 kg grain). Azadirachtin acts as a natural oviposition deterrent and insect repellent.
2. **Edible Vegetable Oil Coating for Pulses**:
   - Coat whole pulses (chana, arhar, moong) with **Mustard Oil or Castor Oil @ 5 ml per kg of pulse** (500 ml per quintal).
   - *Mechanism*: The thin oil film blocks oxygen diffusion through the beetle egg shell, preventing larvae from hatching without affecting pulse germination or cooking taste.
3. **Inert Dust / Dry Wood Ash**: Mix clean sieved wood ash or diatomaceous earth @ 1–2% by weight to abrade insect epicuticles, causing lethal dehydration.

## 6. Chemical Godown Fumigation & Statutory Safety Warning (रासायनिक धूमन एवं सुरक्षा)

> [!CAUTION]
> **STATUTORY REGULATORY SAFETY GATE**: Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (PPQS).
> 
> **Aluminium Phosphide (Phosphine gas / सल्फास)** tablets are legally classified as **"Restricted Use Pesticides" (RUP)** in India. They are strictly prohibited from being sold loose or used in residential rooms, bedrooms, or unsealed traditional mud granaries (*कोठिला*). Lethal phosphine gas emissions can cause fatal inhalation poisoning in humans and livestock.

### Rules for Commercial / Large-Scale Metal Bin Fumigation:
- **Structure**: Allowed strictly in gas-tight outdoor metal silos, airtight warehouses, or under gas-proof rubberized fumigation tarpaulins (*fumigation covers*).
- **Dosage**: **Aluminium Phosphide 56% Tablets** @ **1 tablet (3 grams)** per metric tonne of grain (or 3 tablets per 1,000 cubic feet volume).
- **Exposure Period**: Keep the bin or tarpaulin completely sealed airtight for a minimum of **7 days**.
- **Aeration**: After opening, aerate the grain for **48 hours** before human handling or milling. Never allow phosphine tablets to come in direct contact with liquid water, as rapid explosive generation of phosphine and diphosphine gas occurs.
