# Protected Cultivation, Naturally Ventilated Polyhouses (NVPH) & Shade Nets Guide (संरक्षित खेती: पॉलीहाउस एवं शेड नेट प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Protected cultivation under **Naturally Ventilated Polyhouses (NVPH - *प्राकृतिक रूप से हवादार पॉलीहाउस*)** and **Insect-Proof Shade Net Houses** creates an engineered micro-climate that shields crops from erratic rainfall, hailstorms, scorching summer heat, and insect-borne virus epidemics. By controlling humidity, temperature, and drip fertigation, farmers in peri-urban Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Barabanki, Varanasi, Meerut, and NCR) produce 3 to 5 times higher yields of export-quality, off-season vegetables—principally **Coloured Bell Peppers (*लाल एवं पीली शिमला मिर्च*)** and seedless **Parthenocarpic English Cucumbers (*बिना बीज का खीरा*)**—reaping net profits of ₹6 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh per 1,000 sq. meter unit annually.

## Structural Specifications: Naturally Ventilated Polyhouse (NVPH)

Standard specifications approved by the **National Horticulture Board (NHB)** and **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**:

| Structural Parameter | Technical Specification (NHB Standard) | Agronomic Purpose |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Frame Material** | Hot-dip galvanized iron (GI) tubular pipes (Class B, min. 2 mm wall thickness, 275 g/sq.m zinc coating). | Rust-proof structural integrity engineered to withstand **wind velocity up to 120 km/hr**. |
| **Height Parameters** | **Gutter Height**: 4.0 to 4.5 meters.<br>**Total Ridge Height**: 6.0 to 6.5 meters. | Creates a massive air buffer overhead to exhaust hot buoyant air through top roof vents without burning plants. |
| **Cladding Film** | **200-micron (800-gauge) UV-stabilized multi-layered polyethylene** with anti-dust, anti-condensation, and IR-reflective additives. | Diffuses 80–85% PAR sunlight evenly; prevents burning top leaves; lasts 3 to 5 years. |
| **Ventilation System** | **Fixed Top Ridge Vent**: Continuous 1.0 m opening covered with 40-mesh net.<br>**Side Curtains**: 2.0 m operable roll-up curtains with rack-and-pinion or manual winder. | Convection-driven natural chimney effect: cool air enters sides, hot air escapes continuously through top ridge. |
| **Insect Netting Barrier** | **40-mesh or 50-mesh UV-stabilized nylon insect-proof net** sealing all side curtains and roof vents. | Completely blocks entrance of tiny viral vectors: **Whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*)**, **Thrips**, and **Aphids**. |
| **Double Door Entry Chamber** | 2.5 m $\times$ 2.0 m sealed buffer ante-room with air curtain or foot dip. | Prevents pest entry when operators enter the polyhouse. |

## High-Value Crop Production Packages

### 1. Coloured Bell Pepper / Capsicum (*लाल एवं पीली शिमला मिर्च*)
- **Varieties**: Red: **Inspiration**, **Bachata**; Yellow: **Orobelle**, **Swarna**.
- **Bed Preparation**: Raised beds **90 cm wide, 30 cm high**, with 50 cm walking pathways. Incorporate 25 kg decomposed FYM + 250 g neem cake per 10 sq.m bed.
- **Mulching**: Cover beds with **25–30 micron Silver-Black polyethylene mulch film**.
- **Spacing**: Paired rows at **45 cm $\times$ 30 cm** (approx. 3.0 to 3.3 plants per sq. meter).
- **Pruning & Trellising**: Prune plants to maintain strictly **2 or 4 main stems**. Train stems vertically upward along UV-stabilized plastic twine suspended from overhead GI support trellis wires (3.0 m height).
- **Yield Potential**: **10 to 12 kg/sq. meter** (100–120 tonnes/ha) over an 8 to 9-month harvesting cycle.

### 2. Parthenocarpic English Cucumber (*बीज-रहित खीरा*)
- **Varieties**: **Kian**, **Hilton**, **Multi-Star** (100% gynoecious, fruits develop without insect pollination).
- **Trellising**: Single stem trained vertically; remove all side branches (*nodes 1 to 5*) to stimulate strong vegetative ascent; allow 1 fruit per node from node 6 upward.
- **Cycle & Yield**: 3 crop cycles per year (each 100–110 days); yields **15 to 18 kg/sq. meter per cycle** (total 45–50 kg/sq.m/year).

## Climate & Humidity Control Protocol

- **Summer Cooling (April to June)**:
  - Operate overhead **micro-foggers / misters (4-way cross nozzles, 7 L/hr discharge)** in short 30-second pulses every 10–15 minutes during peak noon heat (11:00 AM to 3:00 PM).
  - Pull overhead **50% Green/Aluminet Shade Net screens** horizontally beneath the roof to reflect excessive heat while misting drops internal ambient temperature by **5°C to 8°C**.
- **Winter Humidity & Fog Management (December to January)**:
  - High relative humidity (>85%) inside polyhouses causes rapid outbreaks of Grey Mold (*Botrytis*) and Powdery Mildew.
  - Open side curtains 30–50 cm between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM on sunny days to purge humid air.

## Daily Fertigation Recipe (Per 1,000 sq. meter Polyhouse)

Automated pressure-compensated drip emitters (1.2–2.0 L/hr) supply soluble nutrients directly to root plugs:

| Crop Stage | Primary Water-Soluble Fertilizers (Daily or Alternate Days) | Target Electrical Conductivity (EC) & pH |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| **Vegetative Stage (1 to 30 DAT)** | 19:19:19 @ 1.0 kg + 12:61:0 (MAP) @ 0.5 kg + Magnesium Sulphate @ 0.5 kg per day. | EC: 1.2–1.5 mS/cm<br>pH: 5.8–6.2 |
| **Flowering & Fruit Set** | 13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate) @ 1.5 kg + Calcium Nitrate @ 1.0 kg (apply Calcium separately to avoid tank precipitation). | EC: 1.8–2.0 mS/cm<br>pH: 6.0–6.5 |
| **Peak Fruit Bulking & Picking** | 0:0:50 (SOP) @ 2.0 kg + 13:0:45 @ 1.0 kg + Chelated Micronutrient Mix (Fe, Zn, B, Mn) @ 50 g per day. | EC: 2.2–2.5 mS/cm<br>pH: 6.0–6.5 |

## Pest & Disease Safety Protocol

Under polyhouse conditions, pests multiply exponentially due to warm windless air:
- **Red Spider Mites (*Tetranychus urticae*)**: Thrive in hot dry microclimates; yellow stippling and fine webbing on leaf underside.
  - *Spray*: **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1.0 ml/L** OR **Abamectin 1.9% EC @ 0.5 ml/L**. Direct spray strictly at leaf undersides.
- **Western Flower Thrips (*Frankliniella occidentalis*)**: Causes silvery streaks and distorted fruit tips.
  - *Spray*: **Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 1.0 ml/L**; install **Blue Sticky Traps @ 1 trap per 50 sq.m**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.
> Observe strict Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) in polyhouse vegetables: Spiromesifen (5 days), Spinetoram (3 days).

## Government Capital Subsidy Schemes (NHB & MIDH)

1. **National Horticulture Board (NHB) - Commercial Scheme**:
   - Project Cost Benchmark for NVPH: **₹935 to ₹1,060 per sq. meter**.
   - **Subsidy Rate**: **50% credit-linked back-ended capital subsidy** (up to maximum cap of ₹56 Lakh per project) for general areas, disbursed through commercial banks.
2. **Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH - State Horticulture Dept)**:
   - Covers polyhouse structures from **500 sq.m to 4,000 sq.m**.
   - Farmers apply online through the UP Horticulture portal (*horticulture.up.gov.in*).
