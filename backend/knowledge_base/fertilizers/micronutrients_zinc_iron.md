# Micronutrient Deficiencies: Zinc, Iron & Sulphur (जिंक, लोहा व सल्फर की कमी)

## 1. Zinc (Zn) Deficiency & Khaira Disease
Zinc is the most widespread micronutrient deficiency in Indian soils, affecting over 40% of agricultural lands.
- Rice "Khaira" Disease (खैरा रोग):
  - 2 to 3 weeks after transplanting, small reddish-brown or bronze spots appear on lower leaves.
  - Leaves become brittle, growth is stunted, and root development ceases.
  - Remedy: Basal soil application of Zinc Sulphate (21% heptahydrate @ 25 kg/ha or 33% monohydrate @ 15 kg/ha) at field preparation. For standing crop, foliar spray of 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (5g/L) neutralized with 0.25% slaked lime (2.5g/L).
- Maize "White Bud" (सफेद कली):
  - Severe zinc deficiency causes new unfolding leaves in the whorl to emerge bleached white or pale yellow.
- Wheat: Dusty brown spots on middle leaves with stunted growth.

## 2. Iron (Fe) Deficiency (Interveinal Chlorosis)
- Symptoms: Unlike Nitrogen, Iron is immobile; symptoms always appear on the **youngest upper new leaves**.
  - New leaves develop intense interveinal chlorosis (शिराओं के बीच पीलापन): the leaf blade turns bright ivory white or lemon yellow while the fine veins remain sharply green.
  - Common in calcareous, high-pH, sandy, or flooded soils.
- Remedy: Foliar spray of Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 0.5% to 1.0% (5-10g per litre of water) along with 0.1% citric acid for better chelation. Spray twice at 10-day intervals.

## 3. Sulphur (S) Deficiency
- Symptoms: Younger leaves turn light green to pale yellow first, similar to Nitrogen deficiency but localized to upper leaves.
- High Impact: Oilseeds (Mustard, Groundnut) and pulses require high sulphur for amino acid and oil synthesis.
- Remedy: Apply Gypsum @ 200 kg/ha or Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or Bentonite Sulphur @ 10 kg/acre at sowing.
