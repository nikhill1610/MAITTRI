# Biofertilizers, Microbial Inoculants & On-Farm Organic Manures Guide (जैव उर्वरक एवं जैविक खाद प्रबंधन मार्गदर्शिका)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Soil organic carbon in intensive Indo-Gangetic agricultural soils has dropped below **0.4% to 0.5%**, severely diminishing fertilizer use efficiency and soil biological activity. Integrating **carrier-based or liquid biofertilizers** (*जैव उर्वरक*) and scientifically prepared **on-farm organic manures** (*गोबर की खाद, वर्मीकम्पोस्ट, नाडेप*) restores soil microbial diversity, fixes 20–40 kg of atmospheric nitrogen per hectare, and solubilizes up to 25–30 kg of insoluble native soil phosphorus. Applying high-quality microbial inoculants allows farmers to safely reduce chemical Nitrogen and Phosphorus inputs by **20% to 25%** while building long-term soil drought resilience.

---

## Controlled Microbial Inoculants (Biofertilizers) Matrix

Biofertilizers are living cultures of beneficial microorganisms that colonize the rhizosphere or seed coat. They are **not chemical fertilizers** and must be handled as living biological cultures:

| Biofertilizer Group | Target Microorganism | Applicable Crops | Biological Mechanism & Benefit | Recommended Dosage |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Symbiotic Nitrogen Fixer** | **Rhizobium** (*Cicer, Lens, Phaseolus, Glycine* specific strains) | Pulses (Gram, Lentil, Arhar, Pea, Urad, Moong, Soybean) | Enters root hairs to form nodules; fixes **30–50 kg N/ha** directly for host plants. | **200 g carrier powder** or **50 ml liquid** per 10 kg seed. |
| **Free-Living Nitrogen Fixer** | **Azotobacter chroococcum** | Non-legumes: Wheat, Mustard, Maize, Potato, Cotton, Vegetables | Aerobic free-living fixation; adds **20–25 kg N/ha** and synthesizes growth-promoting auxins (IAA). | Seed treatment (200 g/10 kg seed) or Soil drench (1 kg in 50 kg FYM/ha). |
| **Associative Nitrogen Fixer** | **Azospirillum brasilense** | Sugarcane, Sorghum, Pearl millet, Paddy | Colonizes root cortex; fixes **20–30 kg N/ha** in graminaceous crops. | Sett dip or root dip (500 ml liquid/ha). |
| **Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB)** | **Bacillus megaterium / Pseudomonas putida** | **All Crops** | Secretes gluconic, citric, and lactic organic acids that dissolve fixed insoluble tricalcium phosphates. | **200 g carrier** or **50 ml liquid** per 10 kg seed. |
| **Mycorrhiza (VAM)** | **Glomus intraradices / Funneliformis mosseae** | Maize, Cotton, Pulses, Vegetables, Fruit trees (Non-cruciferous) | Fungal hyphae expand root exploration area by **100-fold**; absorbs water, Phosphorus, and Zinc from deep micro-pores. | **4 kg/ha granular** mixed with FYM basal or seedling root dip. |

## Standard Seed Treatment Protocol (FIR Sequence)

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.
> Seed dressing fungicides and insecticides must be applied strictly according to label specifications before biofertilizer coating.

Farmers often mix biofertilizers directly with chemical fungicides, killing the living bacteria. Strictly observe the **FIR Order**:

1. **F - Fungicide / Insecticide**: Treat seed with chemical fungicide/insecticide first. Allow seed to dry in shade for at least **12 to 24 hours**.
2. **I - Inoculation (Biofertilizer)**:
   - Prepare a sticky slurry by boiling **50 g jaggery (*गुड़*) in 250 ml water**; cool to room temperature.
   - Mix the required packet of *Rhizobium* / *Azotobacter* + *PSB* into the cool jaggery syrup.
   - Gently coat the seed mass until every seed shows a uniform, thin dark coating without peeling the seed coat.
3. **R - Rest & Sowing**:
   - Spread the inoculated seed on a clean plastic sheet or gunny bag under **deep shade for 30–45 minutes**.
   - **Never expose treated seed to direct midday sunlight**, as ultraviolet rays kill beneficial bacteria within 15 minutes.
   - Sow into moist soil within **6 to 8 hours** of inoculation.

## On-Farm Organic Manure Standards

### 1. Well-Decomposed Farm Yard Manure (FYM - *गोबर की खाद*)
- **Quality Standard**: Fully decomposed FYM is dark brown to black, crumbly, odorless (no foul ammonia smell), with C:N ratio < 20:1.
- **Nutrient Profile**: Contains approximately **0.5% N, 0.25% $P_2O_5$, and 0.5% $K_2O$**.
- **Application**: **10 to 15 tonnes/ha** broadcast 3–4 weeks prior to sowing and immediately incorporated into soil. Leaving raw manure heaped in open sunshine volatilizes 60% of Nitrogen as ammonia gas.

### 2. Vermicompost (*केंचुआ खाद*) - *Eisenia fetida* Technology
- **Decomposition**: Earthworms (*Eisenia fetida*) digest agricultural biomass, cow dung, and leaves into nutrient-rich worm castings in 60–75 days.
- **Nutrient Profile**: **1.5–2.0% N, 1.0–1.5% $P_2O_5$, 1.0–1.5% $K_2O$**, enriched with beneficial actinomycetes and growth enzymes.
- **Application**: **2.5 to 3.0 tonnes/ha** for field crops; **1.0 to 1.5 kg per vegetable plant / fruit basin**.

### 3. NADEP Method Compost
- Uses an aerated brick enclosure (10 ft $\times$ 6 ft $\times$ 3 ft) filled with alternating layers of dry farm biomass, cow dung slurry, and sifted garden soil.
- Produces 3 tonnes of balanced compost per tank in 90–120 days using only 100 kg cow dung per tonne of organic waste.

## Critical Evidence Review: Biostimulants, Amino Acids & Humic Acid

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Regulatory & Agronomic Guidance on Commercial Formulations (GAP-003 Compliance)**:
> Under the **Fertilizer (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Seventh Amendment Order, 2021**, all commercial biostimulants sold in India must be registered under Schedule VI of FCO with proven chemical specification and bio-efficacy.
> 
> Currently, commercial dosage charts for proprietary "seaweed extracts", "amino-acid complexes", and "humic/fulvic drops" heavily circulate in marketing brochures with extravagant yield claims (30–50% yield boosts).
> 
> **Maitri Agronomic Position**:
> 1. **Scientific Evidence Status**: **`SOURCE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED`** for unverified proprietary commercial mixes.
> 2. While research confirms that soil-applied humic acid improves cation exchange capacity (CEC) in alkaline soils and seaweed (*Kappaphycus*) extracts possess cytokinin activity, **they cannot substitute for primary macro-nutrients (N-P-K-S) or proven biofertilizers (Rhizobium/PSB)**.
> 3. Farmers should never divert essential fertilizer budgets to purchase expensive unlabelled commercial biostimulant bottles. Priority must remain on certified biofertilizers (Rhizobium, PSB, Azotobacter), organic FYM/vermicompost, and Soil Health Card-based chemical nutrient balancing.
