# Potassium Deficiency in Crops (पोटाश की कमी के लक्षण एवं एमओपी प्रबंधन)

## Visual Deficiency Symptoms
Potassium (K) regulates plant stomatal opening, water balance, osmotic pressure, enzyme activation, and disease resistance.
- Marginal Chlorosis & Necrosis ("Leaf Scorch"):
  - Symptoms appear first on older lower leaves.
  - Leaf tips and margins turn yellowish, quickly becoming brown, dry, and scorched as if burnt by fire (किनारों का झुलसना).
- Weak Stems & Lodging (फसल गिरना):
  - In cereals (wheat, rice, maize), low potassium results in weak, brittle internodes, causing extensive crop lodging during high winds or rain.
- Susceptibility to Pest & Disease: Potassium-deficient plants have thinner cell walls, making them highly prone to fungal diseases (blast, rust, leaf spots) and sap-sucking pests.
- Poor Fruit / Tuber Quality: In potatoes, tomatoes, and fruit crops, low potassium reduces fruit size, sugar content, and storage shelf-life.

## Correction & Management Guidelines
1. Soil Application:
   - Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP 0:0:60) @ 20 to 30 kg/acre as basal dose at sowing.
   - For light sandy soils prone to leaching, split potassium into two equal applications: 50% as basal and 50% at flowering/active tillering.
2. Sulphate of Potash (SOP 0:0:50) for Chloride-Sensitive Crops:
   - For Potato, Tobacco, and high-value vegetables, use SOP instead of MOP to avoid chloride toxicity and improve tuber dry matter.
3. Foliar Spray:
   - Foliar application of Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 10g/litre (1%) or Potassium Sulphate (0:0:50) @ 10g/litre at flowering and grain/fruit filling stages significantly boosts test weight and yield.
