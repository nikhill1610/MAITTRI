# Agricultural Soil Types & Field Management (कृषि मृदा के प्रकार एवं प्रबंधन)

## Major Agricultural Soil Types in India

### 1. Loamy Soil (दोमट मिट्टी / Sandy Loam)
- **Characteristics**: Ideal agricultural soil comprising a balanced mixture of sand (40%), silt (40%), and clay (20%).
- **Properties**: Excellent water-holding capacity, porous internal aeration, easy root penetration, and high fertility.
- **Suitable Crops**: Wheat, Mustard, Maize, Potato, Tomato, Sugarcane, Pulses.
- **Management**: Maintain organic matter through regular FYM/compost application (5-10 tonnes/ha) and crop rotation.

### 2. Clayey Soil (चिकनी मिट्टी / Heavy Clay)
- **Characteristics**: Very fine particles with high nutrient retention and high water holding capacity, but poor internal drainage and slow permeability.
- **Properties**: Becomes sticky and waterlogged when wet; hardens and forms deep cracks upon drying.
- **Suitable Crops**: Lowland Rice (Paddy), Cotton, Wheat (under good drainage).
- **Management**: Avoid tilling when wet to prevent hardpan formation. Add organic compost, green manure (Dhaincha/Sunhemp), and sand or gypsum to improve soil aggregation and aeration.

### 3. Sandy Soil (बलुई मिट्टी / Light Soil)
- **Characteristics**: Coarse, large sand particles with rapid water infiltration, low water-holding capacity, and high nutrient leaching.
- **Suitable Crops**: Pearl Millet (Bajra), Groundnut, Watermelon, Gram, Mustard.
- **Management**: Frequent light irrigations (drip or sprinkler); split fertilizer application to prevent leaching losses; heavy addition of organic farmyard manure (FYM) to enhance moisture retention.

### 4. Black Soil (काली मिट्टी / Regur Soil)
- **Characteristics**: Derived from Deccan basalt; rich in montmorillonite clay, Calcium, Magnesium, and Potassium, but deficient in Nitrogen and Phosphorus.
- **Properties**: High swelling and shrinkage property ("self-ploughing" cracks in dry summer).
- **Suitable Crops**: Cotton, Soybean, Pigeonpea (Arhar), Sorghum, Wheat (Rabi under moisture conservation).
