# Soil Health Card & Detecting Nitrogen Deficiency in Soil (मृदा स्वास्थ्य कार्ड एवं मिट्टी में नाइट्रोजन की पहचान)

## How to Identify Nitrogen Deficiency in Soil (मिट्टी में नाइट्रोजन की कमी कैसे पहचानें?)
Nitrogen is the most depleted plant nutrient across Indian farmlands. You can identify nitrogen deficiency in soil through three complementary approaches:

### 1. Visual Plant Indicators (फसलों पर दिखने वाले लक्षण)
- **Older Lower Leaves Yellowing First**: Because plants easily mobilize stored nitrogen toward young growing tips, nitrogen deficiency always shows up as uniform yellowing (chlorosis) of the oldest, lower leaves first.
- **Inverted 'V' Yellowing Pattern**: In cereals (Wheat, Maize, Rice), yellowing begins at the leaf tip and travels backward along the central midrib, while outer leaf margins stay green temporarily.
- **Stunted Canopy**: Spindly, thin stems with severely limited branching or tillering.

### 2. Soil Characteristics & Organic Carbon (मृदा लक्षण व जैविक कार्बन)
- **Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) Correlation**: Over 95% of native soil nitrogen is bound within soil organic matter (humus).
  - Low Organic Carbon (< 0.5% OC): Soil is universally deficient in available nitrogen (available N < 280 kg/ha).
  - Medium Organic Carbon (0.5% - 0.75% OC): Medium available nitrogen (280 - 560 kg/ha).
  - High Organic Carbon (> 0.75% OC): Sufficient available nitrogen (> 560 kg/ha).
- **Physical Soil Texture**: Light sandy soils with coarse texture and low cation exchange capacity (CEC) lose nitrate rapidly through leaching, resulting in perpetual nitrogen starvation.

### 3. Soil Health Card (SHC) Lab Analysis (प्रयोगशाला परीक्षण)
- Available Nitrogen in soil is measured using the **Alkaline Potassium Permanganate (KMnO4) method**:
  - **Low**: Available Nitrogen < 280 kg/ha (Requires 25% higher fertilizer dose).
  - **Medium**: 280 to 560 kg/ha (Standard recommended package of practices).
  - **High**: > 560 kg/ha (Can reduce nitrogen fertilizer by 20-25%).

## The 12 Parameters Tested in Soil Health Card
1. Macro-nutrients (3): Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K).
2. Secondary nutrient (1): Sulphur (S).
3. Micro-nutrients (5): Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), Boron (B).
4. Physical/Chemical parameters (3): pH, Electrical Conductivity (EC / Salinity), Organic Carbon (OC).
