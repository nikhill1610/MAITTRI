# Soil pH, Salinity & Sodic (Usar) Land Reclamation Guide (मृदा पीएच, खारी मिट्टी एवं ऊसर भूमि सुधार)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Over **1.3 million hectares of fertile land in Uttar Pradesh**—spanning Unnao, Rae Bareli, Sultanpur, Hardoi, Mainpuri, Etah, and Pratapgarh—lie unproductive or under-yielding due to **Sodic "Usar" (*ऊसर, रेह, कल्लर*) and Saline (*खारी*) soil degradation**. When soil pH exceeds **8.5** and Exchangeable Sodium Percentage (ESP) crosses **15%**, sodium ions destroy the soil structure, causing clay particles to disperse. In wet weather, Usar soil turns into an impermeable, slippery black grease where water stagnates for weeks; in dry weather, it bakes into a rock-hard crust with white powdery salt efflorescence (*रेह*). Following the scientific **Gypsum + Leaching + Dhaincha Green Manuring** package developed by ICAR-CSSRI restores these barren lands back to prime double-cropped productivity within two seasons.

## 1. Classification & Scientific Discrimination of Problem Soils

| Soil Category | Local Name | Soil Reaction (pH) | Electrical Conductivity (EC) | Exchangeable Sodium (ESP) | Physical Condition & Core Remedy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal Healthy Soil** | दोमट / मटियार | **6.5 – 7.5** | **< 1.0 dS/m** | **< 15%** | Favorable crumb structure; optimal nutrient availability. |
| **Saline Soil** | खारी मिट्टी / सफेद ऊसर | **< 8.5** | **> 4.0 dS/m** | **< 15%** | Excess neutral soluble salts (chlorides/sulphates). **Remedy: Pure water leaching through drainage (NO Gypsum needed).** |
| **Sodic / Alkali Soil** | **ऊसर / रेह / कल्लर** | **> 8.5** (Often 9.0–10.5) | **< 4.0 dS/m** (Variable) | **> 15%** (Often 40–70%) | Excess Sodium on clay particles. Clay disperses; zero internal drainage. **Remedy: Chemical Gypsum + Water Leaching.** |
| **Saline-Sodic Soil** | मिश्रित ऊसर | > 8.5 | > 4.0 dS/m | > 15% | High salts + high exchangeable sodium. **Remedy: Gypsum first, followed by heavy freshwater leaching.** |
| **Acidic Soil** | अम्लीय मिट्टी | **< 6.0** | < 1.0 dS/m | < 5% | Aluminum/Manganese toxicity; high P fixation. **Remedy: Agricultural Lime or Dolomite.** |

## 2. The 6-Step Sodic Usar Land Reclamation Protocol (ICAR-CSSRI)

### Step 1: Soil Testing for Gypsum Requirement (GR)
- Sample soil down to 15 cm depth and send to an authorized laboratory for a **Gypsum Requirement (GR)** test.
- The GR test calculates the exact metric tons of Agricultural Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$) needed per hectare to displace exchangeable sodium.
- As a general rule for moderate Usar soils (pH 8.8–9.5), the requirement is **50% of GR**, typically **8 to 12 tons per hectare (3 to 5 tons per acre)**.

### Step 2: Strong Field Bunding & Precision Laser Leveling (May–June)
- Build strong perimeter earthen bunds (*मजबूत मेड़बंदी*) 35 to 40 cm high around each plot to retain water and prevent saline runoff into neighboring fields.
- Laser level the plot to an absolute grade. Uneven plots cause water to pool in hollows while sodium concentrates on high spots.

### Step 3: Application of Agricultural Gypsum
- Plough field shallowly (10–12 cm depth). Never perform deep sub-soiling or deep ploughing, which brings toxic calcium carbonate kankar pan up to the surface.
- Broadcast the recommended quantity of powdered Agricultural Gypsum (contains minimum 70% $CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$) uniformly onto moist soil in late May or early June.
- Mix gypsum into the top 8–10 cm soil with a single light disc or cultivator pass.

### Step 4: Freshwater Ponding & Leaching (June)
- **The Chemical Displacement Reaction**:
  $$\text{Clay-Na}^+ + \text{Gypsum } (CaSO_4) \longrightarrow \text{Clay-Ca}^{2+} + \mathbf{\text{Sodium Sulphate } (Na_2SO_4 \text{ Soluble Salt})}$$
  Calcium ions displace sodium from the clay particles, flocculating dispersed soil into healthy crumbs.
- Flood the plot with clean irrigation/canal water to a depth of **10 to 15 cm**.
- Keep water ponded continuously for **10 to 15 days**.
- Open drainage ditches at the lower end of the field to flush out the dissolved sodium sulphate effluent.

### Step 5: Green Manuring with Dhaincha (*ढैंचा - Sesbania aculeata*)
- In mid-June, sow seeds of **Dhaincha (*Sesbania aculeata*) @ 50 kg/ha (20 kg/acre)** without applying any nitrogen fertilizer (Dhaincha fixes atmospheric N).
- Dhaincha possesses exceptional tolerance to alkaline sodic soils (survives pH up to 9.5).
- Its deep roots puncture through hard subsurface soil pans, creating root channels for water drainage.
- At **45 days of age (early flowering stage)**, incorporate the green Dhaincha crop into the flooded soil using a disc plough or rotavator.
- *Why Dhaincha is Magic for Usar Soil*: Decomposing Dhaincha releases vast quantities of carbon dioxide ($CO_2$), carbonic acid, and organic acids, which directly neutralize alkaline sodium carbonates and drop soil pH by 0.5 to 1.0 unit!

### Step 6: Salt-Tolerant Rice-Wheat Cropping Cycle
- Immediately following Dhaincha puddling, transplant salt-tolerant rice varieties:
  - **Rice**: **CSR-30 (Basmati)**, **CSR-36**, or **CSR-43** (25-day-old seedlings, 3–4 seedlings per hill).
- In the succeeding Rabi season, sow salt-tolerant wheat varieties:
  - **Wheat**: **KRL-210**, **KRL-19**, or **DBW-187**.
- Apply **25% extra Nitrogen** and **25 kg/ha Zinc Sulphate**, as young sodic soils exhibit poor nitrogen mineralization.

## 3. Acid Soil Reclamation (pH < 6.0)
- **Occurrence**: Prevalent in high-rainfall foothill zones (Terai, parts of Eastern UP and Northeast India).
- **The Danger**: Soil acidity releases toxic levels of soluble Aluminum ($Al^{3+}$) and Manganese ($Mn^{2+}$), which stunt root growth and lock up all applied DAP into insoluble aluminum phosphates.
- **Reclamation**:
  - Broadcast **Agricultural Limestone ($CaCO_3$)** or **Dolomitic Lime ($CaCO_3 \cdot MgCO_3$)** @ 2.0 to 4.0 tons/ha once every 3 years.
  - Lime neutralizes free $H^+$ and $Al^{3+}$ ions, raising soil pH to the optimal 6.5 neutral zone and releasing locked phosphorus.

## Farmer Decision Table
| Field Diagnostic Observation | Problem Classification | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| pH > 9.0; water stands in puddles like black grease; white salt crust when dry | Sodic Usar Soil | Apply Agricultural Gypsum (4 t/acre); pond water 15 days; grow Dhaincha green manure. |
| pH = 7.8; EC > 4.0 dS/m; seed germination patchy; salt water well | Saline Soil | **Do not apply gypsum**. Provide deep surface drainage channels; leach with fresh canal water. |
| pH = 5.2; pulse crops failing; fertilizer DAP not working | Acid Soil | Broadcast 1.5–2.0 tons/acre Agricultural Lime; incorporate 3 weeks before sowing. |
| Reclaimed Usar land in 1st year of cropping | Transition stage | Transplant salt-tolerant CSR-36 or CSR-43 paddy; apply 25 kg/ha Zinc Sulphate. |

## Common Farmer Questions & Answers (FAQ)
- **Q: Kya usar khet me gobar ki khad dalne se fayda hota hai? (Does FYM/compost help reclaim Usar soil?)**  
  *A*: Yes, immensely. Applying 10 to 15 tons/ha of well-decomposed Farmyard Manure (FYM), pressmud, or Dhaincha green manure produces organic acids that dissolve native calcium carbonate already present in the soil, speeding up sodium leaching.
- **Q: Gypsum dalne ke baad khet me kitne din pani bhara rehna chahiye? (How many days to keep water ponded after applying gypsum?)**  
  *A*: Pond clean water (10–15 cm depth) continuously for **10 to 15 days**. This provides sufficient time for calcium to displace sodium from clay particles and for the dissolved sodium sulphate to leach down or drain out.
