# Soil pH & Problem Soil Reclamation (मृदा पीएच एवं क्षारीय/अम्लीय सुधार)

## Understanding Soil pH Ranges
Soil pH measures the acidity or alkalinity of soil water solution on a scale of 0 to 14:
- **Acidic Soils (अम्लीय मृदा)**: pH < 6.5. (Prevalent in high rainfall regions like Eastern India, Kerala, Coastal belts).
  - Issues: Fixes Phosphorus as insoluble iron/aluminum phosphates; toxic levels of Aluminum (Al) and Manganese (Mn); microflora activity is stunted.
- **Neutral / Ideal Range**: pH 6.5 to 7.5. Optimum nutrient availability for almost all crops.
- **Saline Soils (लवणीय मृदा / सफेद ऊसर)**: High soluble salts (EC > 4 dS/m, pH < 8.5, ESP < 15%). White crust of salts on surface.
- **Alkali / Sodic Soils (क्षारीय मृदा / काला ऊसर)**: High exchangeable sodium (ESP > 15%, pH > 8.5). Soil disperses, forms slick crust, zero infiltration, black organic matter slick.

## Reclamation Methods

### 1. Acid Soil Reclamation (pH < 6.0)
- **Application of Agricultural Lime (चूना / CaCO3)** or Dolomite.
- Rate: 2 to 4 tonnes/hectare depending on soil test buffer capacity.
- Apply 3-4 weeks prior to sowing and thoroughly mix into the top 15 cm plough layer. Lime neutralizes exchangeable Aluminum, raises pH to 6.5, and frees up bound Phosphorus.

### 2. Sodic / Alkali Soil Reclamation (pH > 8.5)
- **Application of Mineral Gypsum (जिप्सम / CaSO4·2H2O)**:
  - Calcium in Gypsum displaces exchangeable Sodium (Na+) from the clay lattice. The displaced sodium sulphate is then leached down beyond the root zone by ponding fresh irrigation water for 10-15 days.
  - Dose: 25% to 50% of Gypsum Requirement (GR), typically 3 to 5 tonnes/ha.
- **Green Manuring with Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata)**:
  - Dhaincha is salt-tolerant. Incorporating 45-day-old succulent Dhaincha releases organic acids (carbonic and acetic acids) that dissolve native calcium carbonate, reducing alkalinity.

### 3. Saline Soil Reclamation
- Deep drainage channels, flushing surface salts with fresh canal water, and avoiding saline ground-water irrigation.
