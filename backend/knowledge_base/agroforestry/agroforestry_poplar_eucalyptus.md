# Commercial Agroforestry: Poplar & Clonal Eucalyptus Boundary & Intercropping Guide (व्यावसायिक कृषि वानिकी: पॉपलर एवं यूकेलिप्टस प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Agroforestry—integrating commercial fast-growing timber trees with seasonal agricultural field crops—generates the highest net returns per acre across Northern Uttar Pradesh (Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Bijnor, Bareilly, Rampur, and Lakhimpur Kheri). Because **Poplar (*Populus deltoides*)** is fully **deciduous** (shedding 100% of its leaves in winter from November to February), it permits full winter sunlight penetration to the ground, creating the ideal biological environment for **intercropping high-yield Rabi Wheat (*गेहूं*)**, sugarcane, or shade-loving turmeric without sunlight competition. Over a **5 to 6 year harvesting cycle**, Poplar agroforestry produces 120–150 tonnes of industrial plywood timber per hectare, yielding an exceptional net return of ₹4 Lakh to ₹6 Lakh per acre while simultaneously harvesting regular agricultural crops.

## Technical Specifications: Poplar vs Clonal Eucalyptus

| Agroforestry Parameter | Poplar (*Populus deltoides*) | Clonal Eucalyptus (*Eucalyptus tereticornis*) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Canopy Behavior** | **Deciduous (*पतझड़ी*)**: Leaves drop naturally in November; trees remain leafless until March. Zero shade over winter crops. | **Evergreen (*सदाबहार*)**: Dense continuous leaf canopy year-round; creates persistent shade on northern/southern sides. |
| **Planting Material** | **Bare-root Entire Transplants (ETPs)**: 1-year-old nursery saplings, **3.5 to 5.0 meters tall**, straight unbranched bole. | **Clonal Polybag Saplings**: Genetically identical disease-resistant tissue/cuttings (ITC Clones 7, 10, 413). |
| **Optimal Planting Season** | **1 January to 15 February** (during winter dormancy before spring bud burst). | **July to August** (active monsoon rains). |
| **Spacing Standards** | **Block Plantation**: $5\text{ m} \times 4\text{ m}$ (500 trees/ha) or paired rows at $7\text{ m} \times 3\text{ m}$ (476 trees/ha) for tractor movement.<br>**Boundary Single Row**: 2.5 to 3.0 m tree-to-tree. | **Field Boundary (*मेढ़ पर*)**: 1.5 to 2.0 m single row on farm boundary.<br>**Block System**: $3\text{ m} \times 2\text{ m}$ (1,666 trees/ha) or $3\text{ m} \times 1.5\text{ m}$ for high-density pulpwood. |
| **Intercropping Suitability** | **Exceptional for 5 full years**: Wheat, potato, mustard, sugarcane (years 1–2), turmeric, and ginger (years 3–5). | Suitable for 1st year only in block systems; boundary trees allow normal cropping if root trenching is performed. |
| **Rotation / Felling Cycle** | **5 to 6 Years** (Target tree girth: **90 to 110 cm** at breast height). | **4 to 5 Years** (for paper pulp); **7 to 8 Years** (for construction poles/timber). |

## Poplar Planting & Deep Auger Pit Technique

Poplar has high vegetative transpiration in summer and requires deep root anchorage to prevent lodging during pre-monsoon storm squalls:

1. **Planting Material Quality**:
   - Use certified 1-year-old ETPs of proven clones: **G-48**, **Udai**, **Kranti**, or **WSL-22**.
   - Saplings must be at least **4 meters tall** with clean uninjured bark, intact terminal bud, and free from stem borer pinholes.
2. **Deep Auger Pits (*गहरे गड्ढे*)**:
   - Use a tractor-drawn auger or manual post-hole digger to dig narrow holes **1 meter deep (80–100 cm)** and 15–20 cm wide.
   - Planting in shallow pits (30–40 cm) results in surface root development that tears up during tractor plowing and causes trees to blow down in storms.
3. **ETP Preparation & Fungicide Prick**:
   - Trim bruised root tips with a sharp shears.
   - Dip the bottom 1 meter of the ETP for 15 minutes in **Carbendazim 50% WP (2 g/L) + Chlorpyrifos 20% EC (2 ml/L)** solution to prevent root rot (*Rhizoctonia*) and termite attack.
4. **Planting & Compaction**:
   - Insert ETP into the hole; refill with a mixture of topsoil, **5 kg decomposed FYM, 50 g SSP, and 5 g Phorate/Chlorpyrifos dust**.
   - Compact soil firmly around the bole with a wooden tamper. Provide immediate post-planting irrigation to eliminate subterranean air pockets.

## Intercropping Schedule Over the 5-Year Lifecycle

Agroforestry optimizes land use by transitioning intercrops as the tree canopy matures:

| Year | Tree Stage | Best Companion Intercrops | Management Tips |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **Year 1** | Sapling establishment (slender bole) | Kharif: Sugarcane, Maize, Cowpea, Green gram.<br>Rabi: **Wheat**, Potato, Mustard, Garlic. | Full sunlight available; normal crop yields (100% wheat yield). Keep tractor 50 cm away from tree boles. |
| **Year 2** | Rapid vegetative ascent (tree height 8–10 m) | Kharif: Ratoon Sugarcane, Fodder Sorghum.<br>Rabi: **Wheat**, Lentil, Toria. | Prune lower side branches up to 1/3rd of tree height in January to produce knot-free commercial timber. |
| **Year 3** | Branching canopy expansion | Kharif: Fodder cowpea, green manuring (Dhaincha).<br>Rabi: **Wheat** (switch to shade-tolerant varieties like PBW-550, HD-2967). | Wheat yield maintains 80–85% of open field; tree roots draw subsoil moisture without competition. |
| **Year 4 & 5** | Closed mature summer canopy; leafless in winter | Kharif: **Turmeric (*हल्दी*)**, **Ginger (*अदरक*)**, Elephant foot yam (*जिमीकंद*).<br>Rabi: **Wheat**, Berseem clover, Fodder oats. | Turmeric and ginger thrive under partial summer canopy shade, yielding 180–220 q/ha fresh rhizomes. Winter wheat receives 100% sunlight through bare branches. |

## Eucalyptus Boundary Plantation & The Root Trenching Rule

When farmers plant Eucalyptus along field boundaries, adjacent field crops often show severe stunting and yellowing for 5 to 8 meters away from the tree row. **This is caused by lateral tree root competition, not "soil poisoning"**:

### Mandatory Root Barrier Trenching Protocol (*जड़ अवरोधक नाली*)
- Dig a narrow trench **1.0 meter deep and 40 cm wide** running parallel to the Eucalyptus boundary tree line, positioned **1.5 meters away from the tree trunks**.
- The trench severs all shallow lateral feeding roots of the eucalyptus trees that would otherwise invade the crop root zone.
- Tree roots are forced to grow straight downward into deep subsoil layers, completely eliminating the crop yield penalty in the adjacent field rows while the trees thrive on deep subsoil moisture.

## Timber Measurement & Yamunanagar / Bareilly Mandi Marketing

Poplar wood is the premier raw material for the plywood, matchstick, and packing crate industries:

1. **Volume Measurement (Quarter Girth / Hoppus Formula)**:
   - Industrial log volume in North Indian timber mandis (Yamunanagar, Rampur, Bareilly) is computed as:
     $$\text{Volume } (V) = \left(\frac{\text{Girth in Inches}}{4}\right)^2 \times \frac{\text{Length in Feet}}{144} \quad (\text{Result in Cubic Feet / Cu. Ft.})$$
2. **Timber Grading by Girth (at Breast Height / 1.37 m)**:
   - **Over-Girth (*बड़ा लट्ठा*)**: Girth $> 60\text{ cm}$ ($> 24\text{ inches}$). Commands peak commercial premium for face-veneer plywood (₹1,000 to ₹1,400 per quintal).
   - **Under-Girth (*छोटा लट्ठा*)**: Girth $45\text{ to } 60\text{ cm}$. Used for core-veneer (₹800 to ₹1,000 per quintal).
   - **Sokra / Fuelwood**: Girth $< 45\text{ cm}$. Used for paper pulp and particle board.
3. **Weight Basis**: A 5-year-old Poplar tree in irrigated Western UP weighs **2.5 to 3.5 quintals** of commercial timber, fetching ₹2,500 to ₹3,500 per tree standing.
