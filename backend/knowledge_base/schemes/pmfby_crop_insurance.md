# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) (प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना)

## Overview & Objectives
PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rainfall, hailstorms, pests, and post-harvest cyclone damage).

## Farmer Premium Rates (प्रीमियम दरें)
The premium payable by farmers is heavily subsidized by the Central and State Governments:
- **Kharif Crops (Food grains, Oilseeds, Pulses)**: Maximum **2.0%** of the sum insured.
- **Rabi Crops (Wheat, Barley, Mustard, Gram)**: Maximum **1.5%** of the sum insured.
- **Commercial & Horticultural Crops (Sugarcane, Cotton, Potato, Spices)**: Maximum **5.0%** of the sum insured.
- **Government Subsidy**: The balance premium beyond the farmer's share is shared 50:50 between the Central and State Governments (90:10 for North-Eastern States).

## Risk Coverage Stages
1. Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk: Up to 25% of sum insured if widespread drought or adverse weather prevents sowing.
2. Standing Crop Losses (Sowing to Harvesting): Yield loss determined through Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
3. Localized Calamities: Individual farm assessment for hailstorms, landslides, inundation, and cloudbursts.
4. Post-Harvest Losses: Coverage up to 14 days after harvest for crops kept in the field to dry (cyclones, unseasonal rain).

## Critical Intimation Rule
- For localized disasters (e.g., hailstorm or localized waterlogging), the farmer must report the crop loss within **72 hours** through the National Crop Insurance Portal (`pmfby.gov.in`), the Crop Insurance Mobile App, the insurance company helpline, or the local Agriculture Officer.
