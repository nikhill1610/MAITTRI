# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) (प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना)

## Farmer-friendly summary
The **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY / *प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना*)** provides comprehensive risk insurance coverage for agricultural crops against unavoidable natural perils from pre-sowing to post-harvest stages. Farmers pay only a minimal, flat nominal premium—**2% for Kharif crops**, **1.5% for Rabi crops**, and **5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops**—while the remaining actuarial premium is heavily subsidized by the Central and State Governments. In case of localized disasters like hailstorms, inundation, or post-harvest cyclone damage, **farmers must report the loss within 72 hours** through the PMFBY portal, mobile app, or toll-free helpline to be eligible for insurance assessment and claim payouts.

---

## 1. Premium Structure Payable by Farmers (प्रीमियम दरें)

The premium payable by the cultivator is kept intentionally low to ensure widespread accessibility:

| Crop Category | Season / Crops | Maximum Farmer Share (% of Sum Insured) | Balance Premium Subsidy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kharif Food Grains & Oilseeds** | Paddy, Maize, Bajra, Jowar, Groundnut, Soybean, Arhar/Pigeonpea, Moong, Urad | **2.0%** | Shared 50:50 by Central and State Govts (90:10 in NE States) |
| **Rabi Food Grains & Oilseeds** | Wheat, Barley, Mustard, Gram, Lentil, Field Pea | **1.5%** | Shared 50:50 by Central and State Govts |
| **Commercial & Horticultural Crops** | Potato, Tomato, Sugarcane, Cotton, Onion, Spices, Fruit crops | **5.0%** | Shared 50:50 by Central and State Govts |

> [!NOTE]
> There is no upper capping on the government subsidy. Even if the actuarial premium calculated by the insurance company is 25% or 30%, the farmer pays only 1.5% or 2.0%, and the government absorbs the entire remaining liability.

## 2. Comprehensive Risk Coverage Stages

PMFBY covers crop losses across four distinct phases:

### Phase 1: Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk (बुवाई न हो पाना)
- **Coverage**: If deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions prevent an insured farmer from sowing/planting in the majority of an insured unit area.
- **Compensation**: Up to **25% of the Sum Insured**, and the insurance policy terminates for that season.

### Phase 2: Standing Crop Loss from Sowing to Harvest (खड़ी फसल का नुकसान)
- **Coverage**: Comprehensive yield losses caused by non-preventable natural risks: drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, widespread pests/diseases, natural fire, lightning, storm, cyclone, and hailstorm.
- **Assessment**: Evaluated at the insurance unit level (Gram Panchayat or Block) based on mandatory **Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs)** comparing actual yield against the 7-year historical benchmark (threshold yield).

### Phase 3: Localized Calamities (स्थानीय आपदाएं - व्यक्तिगत खेत स्तर)
- **Coverage**: Loss or damage resulting from localized perils: **Hailstorm (*ओलावृष्टि*)**, **Landslide (*भूस्खलन*)**, **Inundation (*जलभराव*)**, **Cloudburst (*बादल फटना*)**, and natural lightning fire.
- **Assessment**: Conducted on an individual affected farm survey basis.

### Phase 4: Post-Harvest Losses (कटाई उपरांत नुकसान)
- **Coverage**: Available for up to **14 days post-harvest** for crops kept in "cut-and-spread" condition in the field to dry (e.g., harvested paddy, wheat, pulses, oilseeds).
- **Perils Covered**: Unseasonal rains, cyclones, and cyclonic rainfall.

## 3. The Mandatory 72-Hour Localized Intimation Rule (72 घंटे का अनिवार्य नियम)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For **Localized Calamities (Hailstorm, Inundation)** and **Post-Harvest Losses**, individual assessment requires prompt intimation.
> 
> **The crop damage must be formally reported within 72 hours of the calamity occurrence.**
> 
> Claims reported after 72 hours risk being rejected due to lack of verifiable surveyor assessment evidence.

### Channels for Immediate Reporting (Any One):
1. **Crop Insurance Mobile App**: Download *Crop Insurance* App (PMFBY GoI), open "Crop Loss Intimation", enter Aadhaar/Policy number, upload geo-tagged photo with description.
2. **National PMFBY Portal**: Visit `https://pmfby.gov.in` and log in via farmer registration to submit incident details.
3. **National Toll-Free Helpline**: Call **14447** or **1800-180-1551** (operates in Hindi and regional languages).
4. **Local Agriculture Officer**: Submit written intimation with Khasra number and date of disaster to the Block Agriculture Technology Management Agency (ATMA) or Assistant Agriculture Officer (AAO).
5. **Bank Branch / CSC Center**: Intimate the bank branch where the KCC crop loan was disbursed or the Common Service Center (CSC) where the policy was generated.

## 4. Eligibility & Enrollment Procedure

### A. Who is Eligible?
- All farmers including landholders, sharecroppers, and tenant farmers growing notified crops in notified areas.
- **Voluntary Enrollment**: Both loanee farmers (holding Kisan Credit Card) and non-loanee farmers can enroll voluntarily.
- **Opt-out for Loanee Farmers**: Farmers who do not wish to participate must submit an opt-out declaration to their loan-sanctioning bank branch at least **7 days prior** to the season cut-off date.

### B. Required Documents for Non-Loanee Farmers:
1. Aadhaar Card (mandatory identification).
2. Land Records: Copy of updated Record of Rights (RoR / *Khatauni* / *Khasra*).
3. Sowing Certificate (*बुवाई प्रमाण पत्र*): Issued by the Village Accountant (*Lekhpal* / *Patwari*) or Sarpanch/Gram Pradhan.
4. Bank Passbook / Cancelled Cheque: Clear display of Account Number, IFSC code, and Account holder name.
5. Tenancy Agreement or Self-Declaration: For tenant farmers / sharecroppers.

## 6. Claim Settlement Formula & Payout Mechanism

$$\text{Claim Payout} = \frac{\text{Threshold Yield} - \text{Actual Yield}}{\text{Threshold Yield}} \times \text{Sum Insured}$$

Where:
- **Threshold Yield** = Average yield of the best 5 out of the preceding 7 years $\times$ Indemnity Level (70%, 80%, or 90% as notified by the State Government).
- **Actual Yield** = Measured average yield from randomized CCEs in the insurance unit.
- **Direct Payout**: Claims are credited directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS / DBT).
