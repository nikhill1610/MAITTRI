# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme (किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड योजना)

## Farmer-friendly summary
The **Kisan Credit Card (KCC / *किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड*)** is an institutional credit facility introduced by the Government of India, RBI, and NABARD to ensure farmers have timely, flexible, and affordable access to credit for agricultural production, input purchases (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides), machinery hire, and allied activities (dairy, poultry, fisheries). Under the Central Government's Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS), loans up to **₹3.00 Lakh** carry an effective net interest rate of only **4% per annum** for farmers who repay promptly within the stipulated due date. In addition, no collateral security or land mortgage is required for loans up to **₹1.60 Lakh**.

---

## 1. Interest Rate Structure & Subvention Mathematics (ब्याज दर एवं सब्सिडी गणित)

The low interest rate of KCC is made possible through central budgetary interest subsidies:

$$\text{Net Interest Rate} = \text{Base Benchmark Rate (9%)} - \text{Govt Interest Subvention (2%)} - \text{Prompt Repayment Incentive (3%)} = \mathbf{4.0\%}$$

| Component | Standard Rate / Benefit | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Bank Benchmark Lending Rate** | 9.0% per annum | Standard agricultural short-term lending rate. |
| **Government Interest Subvention (IS)** | **- 2.0% per annum** | Provided upfront by Government of India for crop loans up to ₹3.00 Lakh. Net base rate becomes **7.0%**. |
| **Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI)** | **- 3.0% per annum** | Granted if the farmer repays the crop loan within **12 months** or on/before the due date fixed by the bank. |
| **Net Effective Interest Rate** | **4.0% per annum** | For all prompt-repaying farmers. |
| **Default / Overdue Rate** | **7.0% to 11.0%+** | If the borrower fails to repay by the due date, the 3% PRI is forfeited, and overdue compound interest is applied. |

## 2. Credit Limit Sanction & Scale of Finance (ऋण सीमा निर्धारण)

The KCC limit is sanctioned as a 5-year revolving credit facility based on the **Scale of Finance (SoF)** determined annually by the District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) for each crop in each district:

### Limit Calculation Formula for Year 1:
1. **Crop Cultivation Component**: $\text{Scale of Finance (per acre)} \times \text{Cultivated Area (acres)}$.
2. **Post-Harvest & Household Expenses**: Add **10%** of crop component.
3. **Repairs & Maintenance of Farm Assets**: Add **20%** of crop component.
4. **Total Year 1 Limit** = $(\text{Crop Component}) + 10\% + 20\% = \mathbf{130\%}$ of crop cultivation cost.

### 5-Year Progressive Step-Up:
- The sanctioned limit increases automatically by **10% every successive year** to accommodate inflation, yield increases, and input cost increments:
  - **Year 1**: $100\%$ of base sanctioned limit.
  - **Year 2**: $110\%$ of Year 1.
  - **Year 3**: $120\%$ of Year 1.
  - **Year 4**: $130\%$ of Year 1.
  - **Year 5**: $140\%$ of Year 1 (up to $150\%$ of original limit).

## 3. Collateral & Security Guidelines (जमानत व बंधक नियम)

| Loan Amount | Collateral Requirement (RBI Guidelines) |
|---|---|
| **Up to ₹1.60 Lakh** | **Collateral-Free (No Land Mortgage Required)**. Hypothecation of standing crops only. |
| **Up to ₹3.00 Lakh (Tie-Up)** | **Collateral-Free with Tripartite Agreement** (e.g., sugarcane farmers with sugar mill tie-up where sale proceeds are directly routed through the bank). |
| **Above ₹1.60 Lakh (No Tie-up)** | Hypothecation of crops + simple mortgage of agricultural land (*बंधक अंकन / खतौनी में चार्ज दर्ज*). |

---

## 4. Extension to Animal Husbandry, Dairy & Fisheries (पशुपालन एवं मत्स्य पालन केसीसी)

Farmers engaged in allied agricultural activities can obtain KCC working capital loans:
- **Sub-Limit for Allied Activities**: Up to **₹2.00 Lakh** at the concessional **4% net interest rate** (with 2% IS and 3% PRI).
- **Combined Ceiling**: When a farmer avails both crop production and animal husbandry/dairy credit, the aggregate maximum ceiling eligible for interest subvention is **₹3.00 Lakh**.
- **Eligible Expenses**: Green fodder cultivation, concentrate feed purchase, veterinary medicines, artificial insemination fees, electricity/water charges, and labor.

---

## 5. Eligibility Criteria & Who Can Apply

1. **Owner Cultivators**: Individual farmers or joint landholders cultivating their own land.
2. **Tenant Farmers & Oral Lessees**: Farmers who cultivate leased land with simple lease agreements or village local confirmation.
3. **Sharecroppers (*बटाईदार*)**: Cultivators sharing crop produce with landowners.
4. **Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) / Self-Help Groups (SHGs)**: Groups of small/marginal tenant farmers availing credit on mutual guarantee.

---

## 6. Required Documents for Bank Application

1. **Identity & Address Proof**: Aadhaar Card (mandatory) + Voter ID or Driving License.
2. **PAN Card**: PAN card or Form 60 declaration.
3. **Land Records**: Latest copy of Land Revenue Record (*Khatauni / Khasra / Bahi*) certified by the Revenue Authority (*Lekhpal / Patwari*).
4. **Cropping Pattern Declaration**: Showing crops sown or proposed to be sown across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.
5. **No Dues Certificate (NDC)**: Or self-declaration confirming no default in other institutional banks in the service area.
6. **Recent Passport-size Photographs** (2 copies).

---

## 7. Repayment Cycle & Operational Rules

- **Revolving Credit**: Farmers can withdraw and deposit funds multiple times via Rupay Debit Card, ATM, branch withdrawal, or Point of Sale (POS) machines.
- **Single Deposit Mandate**: The entire outstanding principal and interest must be cleared / rotated at least **once within 12 months** from the date of disbursal. 
- **Immediate Re-Drawal**: Once the loan is renewed by depositing the dues, the entire sanctioned limit becomes instantly available for withdrawal on the very same day or following day.
- **Accidental Insurance**: Rupay KCC cardholders are provided with complimentary accidental insurance coverage up to ₹50,000 for permanent disability or death.
