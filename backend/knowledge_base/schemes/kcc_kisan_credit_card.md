# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme (किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड योजना)

## Overview & Benefits
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) provides farmers with timely, flexible, and affordable short-term institutional credit for purchasing seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, diesel, and hiring machinery.
- **Effective Interest Rate**: 
  - Base interest rate is 9%.
  - The Government of India provides an **Interest Subvention of 2%**, bringing the interest to 7%.
  - Farmers who repay loans promptly within 1 year receive an additional **Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) of 3%**.
  - Net effective interest rate for prompt paying farmers is only **4% per annum**.
- **Collateral-Free Limit**: No collateral or mortgage is required for agricultural loans up to **₹1.60 lakh** (extended to ₹3 lakh in case of tie-ups with sugar mills or state agencies).
- **Flexible Tenure**: Credit limit sanctioned for 5 years with an annual review and a 10% annual limit step-up.
- **Coverage**: Includes crop cultivation, allied activities (dairy animals, poultry, fisheries), and post-harvest household expenses.

## Eligibility & Documents
- Individual farmers, joint borrowers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, and Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
- Simple documentation: Land revenue records (Khasra/Khatauni), Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and passport-size photographs.
