# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Scheme Operational Guidelines (पीएम-किसान सम्मान निधि योजना)

## Farmer-friendly summary
The **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN / *पीएम-किसान*)** is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India to provide income support to all landholding farmer families across the country. Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive **₹6,000 per year** transferred directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts in **three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each** (Period 1: April–July; Period 2: August–November; Period 3: December–March). To ensure transparent Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), three mandatory verifications are strictly enforced: **Aadhaar e-KYC**, **Land Record Seeding (*भूलेख अंकन*)**, and an **Aadhaar-Seeded Active NPCI Bank Account**.

---

## 1. Scheme Benefits & Installment Schedule
- **Total Financial Entitlement**: ₹6,000 per financial year per eligible farmer family.
- **Disbursement Schedule**:
  - **Tranche 1**: April to July.
  - **Tranche 2**: August to November.
  - **Tranche 3**: December to March.
- **Mode of Payment**: 100% Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) via National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) directly into the beneficiary's bank account.

---

## 2. Eligibility & Strict Exclusion Criteria

### Who is Eligible?
- All landholding farmer families who possess cultivable agricultural land in their names as per the state land records (Khatauni / *खतौनी*).
- A "Farmer Family" is defined as husband, wife, and minor children.

### Exclusion Categories (Who is NOT Eligible?):
1. **Institutional Landholders**: Land held by trusts, religious institutions, sugar mills, or government departments.
2. **Current or Former Constitutional Office Holders**: Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors of Municipal Corporations, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
3. **Serving or Retired Government Employees**: Regular officers and employees of Central/State Ministries, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), autonomous bodies, local bodies (excluding Class IV / Group D / multi-tasking staff).
4. **Retired Pensioners with Monthly Pension ≥ ₹10,000**: (Excluding Group D / MTS pensioners).
5. **Income Tax Payers**: Any person who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
6. **Registered Professionals**: Practicing Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants (CAs), and Architects.

---

## 3. The 3 Mandatory Verification Pillars (Why Installments Stop)

Over 95% of delayed or rejected PM-KISAN payments are caused by non-compliance with one of these three requirements:

### Pillar 1: Mandatory Aadhaar e-KYC
- All beneficiaries must complete e-KYC authentication.
- **How to Complete**:
  - *Option A (Free Online OTP)*: Visit `pmkisan.gov.in` -> Click **e-KYC** -> Enter Aadhaar number -> Enter OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  - *Option B (PM-KISAN Mobile App Face Auth)*: Download the official PM-KISAN app -> Use facial recognition technology to complete KYC from home without OTP.
  - *Option C (Biometric CSC)*: Visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) for thumbprint authentication.

### Pillar 2: Land Record Seeding (*भूलेख अंकन*)
- The farmer's plot ownership (*खतौनी खाता संख्या एवं खसरा नंबर*) must be verified and mapped onto the PM-KISAN portal by the local **Lekhpal / Revenue Department (Tehsil)**.
- If status shows `"Land Seeding: NO"`: Take a photocopy of your Khatauni (*खतौनी*), Aadhaar Card, and PM-KISAN registration number to your local Tehsil or Deputy Director of Agriculture (DDA) office for verification.

### Pillar 3: Bank Account Aadhaar-NPCI Seeding
- The bank account must be actively linked to Aadhaar through the **NPCI Mapping (National Payments Corporation of India)**.
- If status shows `"FTO Processed: No"` or `"Aadhaar Bank Account Status: Inactive"`: Visit your bank branch and submit the "Aadhaar Seeding and NPCI Consent Form", or open a digital account at India Post Payments Bank (IPPB), which automatically links NPCI within 48 hours.

## 4. Farmer Triage & Self-Correction Workflow

```
Check Beneficiary Status on pmkisan.gov.in (using Mobile or Registration Number)
  │
  ├── Is e-KYC = "YES"?
  │     ├── NO  --> Complete e-KYC via PM-KISAN mobile app Face-Auth or OTP.
  │     └── YES --> Proceed.
  │
  ├── Is Land Seeding = "YES"?
  │     ├── NO  --> Submit Khatauni copy to local Lekhpal / Tehsil for portal verification.
  │     └── YES --> Proceed.
  │
  └── Is Aadhaar Bank Seeding = "YES"?
        ├── NO  --> Visit Bank or IPPB Post Office to link Aadhaar to NPCI.
        └── YES --> Installment will be automatically released in the upcoming DBT cycle.
```

## Farmer Decision Table
| Portal Status Message | Exact Problem | Farmer Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| `e-KYC: NO` | Aadhaar authentication pending | Open PM-KISAN app and complete Face-Auth, or visit CSC for biometric authentication. |
| `Land Seeding: NO` | Land records not verified by Tehsil | Submit land Khatauni and Aadhaar to local Lekhpal or Block Agriculture Officer. |
| `Payment Status: FTO Not Generated` | Account not mapped to NPCI | Visit your bank branch and submit NPCI linking form, or open an IPPB account. |
| `Correction Required in Name as per Aadhaar` | Name mismatch between portal and Aadhaar | Use "Edit Aadhaar Details" tab on `pmkisan.gov.in` to match spelling exactly. |
