# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Scheme (प्रधानमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि योजना)

## What is PM-KISAN? (PM-KISAN क्या है?)
**PM-KISAN** (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a flagship Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India in February 2019 to provide financial income support to all landholding farmer families across the country.
- **Financial Benefit**: Eligible farmer families receive **₹6,000 per year** in income support.
- **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**: The amount is transferred directly into the Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of beneficiary farmers in **three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each** (April-July, August-November, and December-March).
- **100% Central Funding**: Entire funding is borne by the Government of India.

## Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families with cultivable landholding registered in their names are eligible, subject to exclusion criteria.
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  1. **Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account**: Account must be active and linked with Aadhaar NPCI mapper.
  2. **e-KYC Completion**: Mandatory verification via OTP on PM-KISAN portal/app or biometric authentication at CSC centers.
  3. **Land Seeding (भूलेख अंकन)**: State revenue land records must reflect farmer's ownership status.

## Exclusion Categories (Who is NOT Eligible?)
1. Institutional landholders.
2. Farmer families where one or more members hold or held constitutional posts.
3. Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, and District Panchayat Chairpersons.
4. Serving or retired government employees (Central/State/PSUs/Autonomous bodies), excluding Group D / Multi-Tasking Staff.
5. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more.
6. All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
7. Professionals such as Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

## How Farmers Can Apply & Check Status
- Registration: Online via the official portal (`pmkisan.gov.in`) under 'Farmers Corner' or through Common Service Centers (CSC) and State Agriculture Nodal Officers.
- Beneficiary Status: Farmers can check installment status, land seeding, and e-KYC status using their Registration Number or Mobile Number on the PM-KISAN portal or PM-KISAN mobile app with face authentication.
