# Farm Mechanization, Custom Hiring Centers (CHC) & Advanced Farm Implements Guide (कृषि यंत्रीकरण, कस्टम हायरिंग सेंटर एवं उन्नत कृषि यंत्र)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Farm mechanization is the cornerstone of modern precision agriculture, cutting production costs by **25% to 35%**, eliminating labor bottlenecks, and ensuring timely sowing to protect crops from terminal climate shocks. For small and marginal farmers who cannot afford heavy capital machinery, Uttar Pradesh has deployed over **15,000 Custom Hiring Centers (CHC - *कस्टम हायरिंग सेंटर*)** and **Farm Machinery Banks (FMB)** funded under the **Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM / *स्माम योजना*)**. This guide explains the operation and rental economics of transformative machines—**Super Seeder (*सुपर सीडर*)**, **Laser Land Leveler (*लेजर लैंड लेवलर*)**, and **Zero-Till Planter**—and provides the transparent booking protocol via the UP Agriculture DBT portal (*upagriculture.com*).

## Super Seeder Operation & Field Best Practices

The Super Seeder is the most widely adopted machine in the Indo-Gangetic wheat belt for parali management:

1. **Optimal Field Moisture (*तर-वत्तर दशा*)**:
   - Never run a Super Seeder in bone-dry (*सूखा*) or slurry waterlogged (*दलदल*) fields.
   - Ideal soil condition is when the topsoil is friable and moist (*ओट/तर-वत्तर*). If the field is too dry, give a light pre-sowing irrigation (*पलेवा*) 5–7 days before operating.
2. **Tractor Gearing & Forward Speed**:
   - Operate tractor in **Low 1st Gear (L-1)** at an engine speed of **1,800 to 2,000 RPM**.
   - Operating in higher gears (L-2 or L-3) overloads the rotor shaft, results in coarse, uncut stubble clumps, and uneven sowing depth.
3. **Calibration of Seed & Fertilizer Depth**:
   - Calibrate the rear roller depth-adjustment screws to deposit wheat seed strictly at **4 to 5 cm depth**.
   - Adjust fertilizer tines to deposit DAP/NPK 2 cm deeper and to the side of the seed line to avoid direct seed scorch.
4. **Residue Clearance**:
   - Ensure the Combine Harvester that harvested the paddy had a functioning **Super SMS (Straw Management System)** attached to chop and spread straw evenly. Large unchopped heaps cause super seeder tine jamming.

## Government Subsidy Schemes & Custom Hiring Centers (CHC)

### 1. Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM / Krishi Yantrikaran)
- **Individual Farmer Machinery Subsidy**:
  - **40% to 50% subsidy** (up to maximum caps: e.g., ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,25,000 on Super Seeder; ₹1,50,000 on Laser Land Leveler).
  - Special concessions: Up to 50% for SC/ST, Small & Marginal farmers, and women farmers.
- **Custom Hiring Centers (CHC) / Farm Machinery Banks (FMB)**:
  - Rural Youth, Entrepreneur, or Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) groups can establish a CHC with a project cost of **₹10 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh**.
  - **Subsidy: 40% (up to ₹4 Lakh for individuals/entrepreneurs) and up to 80% (up to ₹8 Lakh for registered FPOs/Cooperatives)**.
  - A standard CHC package includes: 1 high-HP tractor (55 HP), 1 Laser Leveler, 1 Super Seeder, 1 MB Plow, 1 Rotavator, and 1 Multi-Crop Thresher.

## Transparent Online Booking via UP Agriculture DBT Portal

To ensure zero middleman corruption, the Uttar Pradesh Department of Agriculture manages machinery token booking online:

1. **Official Portal**: **[upagriculture.com](http://upagriculture.com)** (Section: *कृषि यंत्र हेतु टोकन जनरेट करें*).
2. **Eligibility Pre-Requisite**: Farmer must possess a valid **UP Agriculture Farmer Registration ID (*किसान पंजीकरण संख्या*)** linked to Aadhaar and an active bank account.
3. **Token Booking Sequence**:
   - Department releases quota notifications district-wise in specified time slots (usually June–July for Kharif and September–October for Rabi).
   - Farmer logs in, selects the specific implement, generates an **e-Token**, and deposits security token money (₹2,500 to ₹5,000) online or through designated bank challan within the stipulated deadline (usually 3 to 7 days).
   - Upon verification and random computerised lottery (if applications exceed district quota), an official sanction letter is generated.
   - The farmer purchases the implement from any certified, empaneled manufacturer of choice, uploads serial numbers and geotagged photos with GPS coordinates on the portal, and the subsidy amount is disbursed directly into the farmer's bank account via DBT.

## Solar Agriculture Pumps under PM-KUSUM (Component-B)

- **Scheme Scope**: Replacement of standalone diesel irrigation pumps with high-efficiency solar photovoltaic pump sets (2 HP, 3 HP, 5 HP, 7.5 HP Surface & Submersible).
- **Financial Sharing Structure**:
  - **Central Financial Assistance (CFA)**: 30%.
  - **State Government Subsidy (UP)**: 30% to 40%.
  - **Farmer Share**: Only **30% to 40%** of total system cost.
- **Water Conservation Mandate**: To qualify for PM-KUSUM subsidy in semi-critical and overexploited groundwater blocks, farmers are required to connect the solar pump to micro-irrigation systems (Drip or Sprinkler).
