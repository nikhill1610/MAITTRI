# Sugarcane Red Rot (*Colletotrichum falcatum*) & Sett Rot Eradication Guide (गन्ना लाल सड़न रोग प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
**Red Rot (*लाल सड़न रोग*)**, caused by the fungus *Colletotrichum falcatum*, is historically known as the **"Cancer of Sugarcane"**. In recent seasons, the emergence of a virulent new fungal pathotype (**CF-13**) has broken down the resistance of popular variety **Co-0238** across Western Uttar Pradesh, resulting in complete plot withering, collapse of sugar recovery at the mill, and massive financial loss. Because the fungal pathogen survives internally in infected setts and within soil crop debris, **no standing foliar chemical spray can cure an already infected sugarcane stalk**. Successful eradication depends on immediate varietal replacement, hot-water sett therapy, strict rogueing of infected clumps, and absolute prohibition of ratoon crops in affected plots.

---

## Definitive Diagnostic Symptoms & Field Identification

When inspecting standing sugarcane fields (especially post-monsoon from July to October):

1. **Canopy Signatures**:
   - The first visible above-ground symptom is the yellowing and drooping of the **third or fourth leaf from the top spindle**.
   - Leaf margins wither, roll inward, and the entire crown dries up rapidly into a straw-colored dead bunch while adjacent non-infected canes remain green.
2. **Internal Stalk Diagnostics (Split Test)**:
   - Split the affected cane longitudinally from base to top.
   - **Definitive Red Rot Signature**: The internal pith displays **blood-red tissues interrupted by distinct white transverse patches / horizontal bands** extending across the width of the stalk.
   - The infected tissues emit a pungent, **sour alcoholic / fermented vinegar odour** caused by enzymatic sucrose inversion.
3. **External Stalk & Nodal Lesions**:
   - The rind loses its luster, shrivels, and shows longitudinal depressions.
   - Near the nodes, tiny black velvet-like fungal fruiting bodies (*acervuli*) develop.

## Diagnostic Differential: Red Rot vs Wilt vs Drought

| Diagnostic Feature | Red Rot (*लाल सड़न*) | Sugarcane Wilt (*उकठा*) | Drought Desiccation (*सूखा*) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Causal Factor** | *Colletotrichum falcatum* (Fungus) | *Cephalosporium sacchari* (Fungus) | Soil moisture exhaustion |
| **Internal Discoloration** | **Bright red with clear white transverse chalky bands** | Diffuse dull brownish-purple or muddy red; no white bands | Natural pith color or hollow dry white pith |
| **Odour** | **Strong fermented sour / alcohol smell** | Faint earthy smell, no alcohol odour | Normal cane smell |
| **Stalk Hollows** | Cavities develop lined with grayish-white fungal mycelium | Boat-shaped narrow cavities in internodes | Uniform dry spongy cottony pith |
| **Canopy Pattern** | Scattered individual clumps suddenly dry up | Whole row or patches fade gradually | Entire field shows uniform tip burning and leaf curling |

## Why Standing Foliar Sprays Fail

Farmers in Western UP often spend thousands of rupees spraying systemic fungicides over standing sugarcane canopies after seeing dried tops. **This practice must be completely avoided**:
- Sugarcane stalks possess a thick, waxy cuticle and fibrous rind that prevents fungicide penetration into the internal vascular bundles.
- The pathogen multiplies inside the vascular xylem and intercellular parenchyma. Once 50% of the stalk shows red pith, the sucrose transport system is permanently destroyed.
- **Any advisory suggesting foliar spraying of standing red rot cane is unscientific and causes pure economic loss to the farmer.**

## 5-Pillar Red Rot Eradication Protocol

### Pillar 1: Urgent Varietal Replacement
- Completely phase out planting of seed cane from **Co-0238** plots in villages where Red Rot has been observed.
- Replace with certified disease-resistant early-maturing varieties:
  - **Co-15023**: Resistant, high sucrose (12.8–13.2%), fast tillering.
  - **Co-0118**: Highly tolerant/moderately resistant, solid erect stalks.
  - **CoLk-14201**: High red-rot resistance, developed specifically for UP conditions.
  - **Co-98014**: Resistant mid-late variety.

### Pillar 2: Thermal & Chemical Sett Sanitation
- **Moist Hot Air Treatment (MHAT) / Hot Water Treatment (HWT)**:
  - Prior to planting, seed setts must undergo Hot Water Treatment at **52°C for 30 minutes** in community seed processing plants or sugar mill nursery vats. This kills internal dormant fungal hyphae within the bud scales without injuring germination.
- **Mandatory Chemical Dip**:
  - Submerge cut setts for **15 minutes** in a tank containing **Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2.0 g per liter of water** (0.2% solution) OR **Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP @ 1.5 g per liter of water**.
  - Always add insecticide (**Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L**) to protect the cut ends from soil termites and early borers.

### Pillar 3: Strict Field Rogueing & Sanitation
- Inspect fields weekly from June to September.
- When an infected clump is detected:
  - Dig out the **entire clump including roots and underground stubbles**.
  - Place the infected canes in a gunny bag, carry away from the field, and **burn immediately**. Never dump infected canes into irrigation canals or field borders.
  - Disinfect the dug-up pit by drenching with **Bleaching Powder (Chlorine) @ 10–15 g per hole** or 0.2% Carbendazim solution to kill residual inoculum.

### Pillar 4: Prohibition of Ratooning in Affected Plots
- **Zero Ratoon Rule**: If a plant crop showed more than 2% red rot incidence, **strictly do NOT keep a ratoon crop (*पेड़ी न रखें*)**.
- Stubble shaving of infected fields spreads the fungus through mechanical cutter blades to every subsequent shoot.
- Plow down the field immediately after harvest, collect and burn stubbles, and follow a 1-year non-host rotation with rice, dhaincha (green manure), or wheat.

### Pillar 5: Soil Biocontrol
- At final land preparation, apply **5 kg *Trichoderma harzianum* / *viride* mixed in 200 kg decomposed FYM** per hectare along planting furrows.
- Maintain field drainage: Ensure no standing water or backflow from diseased neighbouring fields enters fresh sugarcane plots during monsoon rains.

## Statutory Chemical Safety Gate

> [!WARNING]
> **Chemical Safety Gate (CIBRC / PPQS Compliance)**:
> Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.
> Chemical intervention against sugarcane red rot is strictly restricted to **pre-sowing sett dip therapy** and **in-furrow trench treatment**. Foliar application on standing mature cane is non-compliant with recommended scientific packages of practices.
