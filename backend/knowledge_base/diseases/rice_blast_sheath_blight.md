# Rice Blast & Sheath Blight Management (धान का ब्लास्ट व शीथ ब्लाइट)

## Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
Rice blast is the most destructive fungal disease affecting foliage, nodes, and panicle neck (Neck Blast / गर्दन तोड़).
- Symptoms: Spindle-shaped (नाव के आकार के) or elliptical eye-shaped spots with grey or ash-colored centers and dark brown/reddish borders on leaves. Neck blast turns the panicle base dark brown, resulting in chaffy, empty grains.
- Favorable Weather: Cool night temperatures (20°C-24°C), relative humidity above 90%, excessive nitrogen fertilizer application, and overcast skies.
- Control:
  - Seed Treatment: Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 2g/kg seed.
  - Foliar Spray: At initial symptom appearance or panicle emergence, spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6g/litre (120g/acre in 200L water) or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/litre.

## Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- Symptoms: Occurs during maximum tillering and booting stage. Oval or irregular greenish-grey water-soaked spots on leaf sheaths near water line. Spots enlarge and coalesce with dark brown margins.
- Cultural Management: Do not apply excess nitrogen. Drain excess field water to reduce microclimate humidity.
- Chemical Management: Spray Validamycin 3% L @ 2 ml/litre or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 2 ml/litre or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1 ml/litre directing the spray to the plant base.
