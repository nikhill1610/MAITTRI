# Rice Blast & Sheath Blight Diagnostic Overview (धान का ब्लास्ट एवं शीथ ब्लाइट)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Rice Blast (*धान का ब्लास्ट*) and Sheath Blight (*शीथ ब्लाइट*) are the two most devastating fungal diseases of paddy in Uttar Pradesh and North India. While both can cause catastrophic yield losses (>40%), they are caused by two completely different fungal pathogens requiring distinct diagnostic identification and management protocols:
- **Rice Blast (*Pyricularia oryzae*)**: Produces classic **spindle-shaped or eye-shaped lesions with ash-grey centers and brownish-red margins** on leaves, nodes, and panicle necks (*neck blast*). For detailed management, consult [Rice Blast Dedicated Guide](file:///c:/Users/HP/Desktop/MAITTRI/backend/knowledge_base/diseases/rice_blast.md).
- **Sheath Blight (*Rhizoctonia solani*)**: Originates right at the waterline, producing **greenish-grey, irregular snake-skin like lesions on the lower leaf sheaths** that climb upwards into the canopy under warm, dense, humid conditions. For detailed management, consult [Rice Sheath Blight Dedicated Guide](file:///c:/Users/HP/Desktop/MAITTRI/backend/knowledge_base/diseases/rice_sheath_blight.md).

## Side-by-Side Diagnostic Discrimination Matrix
| Diagnostic Parameter | Rice Blast (*Pyricularia oryzae*) | Sheath Blight (*Rhizoctonia solani*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Infection Site** | Leaves, leaf collar, stem nodes, panicle neck | Leaf sheath just above water level, climbing upwards |
| **Lesion Shape & Color** | Spindle/diamond-shaped; ash-grey center with dark reddish-brown margin | Large, irregular, oval or oblong with greenish-grey "snake-skin" pattern and dark brown border |
| **Favorable Weather** | Cool nights (20–24°C), high humidity (>90%), dew/drizzle, overcast skies | Warm temperatures (28–32°C), high humidity (>90%), dense canopy |
| **Nutritional Trigger** | Excessive, late Nitrogen/Urea application | High plant density + high nitrogen + stagnant water |
| **Most Destructive Phase**| **Neck Blast**: Panicle neck rots black, grains turn chaffy and break | **Canopy lodging**: Sheaths rot, tillers collapse and lodge into water |
| **Dedicated Guide** | `diseases/rice_blast.md` | `diseases/rice_sheath_blight.md` |

## Integrated Cultural Management (Common to Both Diseases)
1. **Balanced Fertilization**: Never apply excess Urea. Split Nitrogen into 3 equal doses (Basal, Tillering, Panicle Initiation). Always apply adequate Potash (40–50 kg K2O/ha) to enhance thick cell wall resistance.
2. **Plant Density & Aeration**: Maintain 20 x 15 cm spacing. Leave 30 cm alleyways (*alleyways / skip rows*) every 2.5 to 3.0 meters in high-tillering varieties to permit airflow and sunlight into the lower canopy.
3. **Water Management**: Practice Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD). Continuous deep stagnant water elevates microclimate humidity, creating the ideal breeding environment for sheath blight.

## Chemical Triage Notice
- For **Blast**: Tricyclazole 75% WP or Isoprothiolane 40% EC.
- For **Sheath Blight**: Hexaconazole 5% SC, Validamycin 3% L, or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC.

## Farm Safety & Regulatory Notice
> **Regulatory Safety Notice**: *Chemical recommendation requires current label verification with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and local agricultural extension authorities before application.*
