# Mustard White Rust & Foliar Blight (सरसों का सफेद रतुआ व झुलसा रोग)

## White Rust (Albugo candida / सफेद रोली)
- Symptoms: Prominent creamy-white raised pustules or blisters on the lower surface of leaves. 
- Staghead Phase (हिरन सींग विकृति): Systemic infection causes severe floral malformation, swollen distorted inflorescences, sterile flowers, and thick twisted antler-like green heads called "stagheads", causing severe seed yield loss.
- Management:
  - Seed Treatment: Metalaxyl 35% WS @ 6g/kg seed.
  - Foliar Spray: Spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ) @ 2g/litre at first appearance of leaf blisters.

## Alternaria Leaf Blight (Alternaria brassicae)
- Symptoms: Small dark brown circular spots with concentric rings on leaves, stems, and pods. Pod spots cause premature pod shattering and shriveled discolored seeds.
- Management: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/litre or Iprodione 50% WP @ 2g/litre. Ensure weed-free fields and avoid dense sowing to permit sunlight penetration.
