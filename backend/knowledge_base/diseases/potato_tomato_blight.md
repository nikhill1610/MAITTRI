# Potato & Tomato Blight Diseases (आलू व टमाटर का अगेती एवं पिछेती झुलसा)

## Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
Late blight is an aggressive devastating fungal disease triggered by cold cloudy weather, dense morning fog, rain showers, and temperatures between 10°C and 20°C with high relative humidity (>80%).
- Symptoms: Water-soaked irregular greenish-black spots appear on leaf tips and margins. During humid mornings, a delicate white cottony fungal downy growth is visible on the underside of affected leaves. Infected stems collapse and tubers develop irregular brownish dry rot.
- Preventative Management:
  - When cold, cloudy, foggy weather sets in, apply preventative spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/litre.
  - Curative Action (Once symptoms are spotted): Spray systemic fungicides such as Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1 g/litre.

## Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- Symptoms: Concentric dark brown target-board rings (गोल चक्राकार छल्ले) on older lower leaves first. Leaves turn yellow, dry up and drop prematurely.
- Management: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/litre or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/litre. Avoid overhead irrigation that keeps leaves wet for prolonged hours.
