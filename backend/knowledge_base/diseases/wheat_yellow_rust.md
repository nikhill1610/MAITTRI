# Wheat Yellow Rust / Stripe Rust (पीला रतुआ रोग)

## Symptoms & Identification
Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis) is a serious fungal disease prevalent in Northern Plains and Sub-Himalayan tracts during cool, humid weather (10°C to 18°C with morning fog/dew).
- Appearance: Bright yellow, powdery pustules arranged in parallel linear stripes (धारीदार पीले पाउडर के धब्बे) along the leaf veins of older leaves first, gradually moving to younger leaves and ear heads.
- Differentiating from Nitrogen Deficiency: 
  - Yellow Rust: If you rub the yellow stripes with your fingers or a white tissue, a bright yellow powdery dust adheres to your fingers.
  - Nitrogen Deficiency: Whole leaf uniformly turns pale yellow from the leaf tip backward along the midrib; no powdery spore dust comes off on fingers.

## Integrated Disease Management (IPM)
1. Resistant Varieties: Sow resistant cultivars recommended for your zone (e.g., HD 3086, PBW 725, DBW 187, DBW 222).
2. Avoid Late Sowing: Late sown wheat is significantly more vulnerable.
3. Monitoring & Field Sanitation: Inspect shaded field borders and canal-adjacent depressions regularly from late December to February.
4. Chemical Advisory (Upon First Sighting of Focus Pustules):
   - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml per litre of water (200 ml in 200 litres of water per acre) or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC.
   - Repeat spray after 15 days if cloudy humid weather persists and disease spreads.
   - Always verify with local KVK or District Agriculture Office before spraying.
