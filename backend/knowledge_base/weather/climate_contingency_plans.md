# Agricultural Drought & Monsoon Delay Climate Contingency Action Plans (कृषि सूखा एवं मानसून देरी आकस्मिक फसल योजना)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Erratic monsoon behavior—manifesting as delayed onset (15 to 45 days late), prolonged mid-season dry spells (breaks of 2 to 4 weeks during tillering), or premature withdrawal before grain filling—is the single greatest climate threat to Kharif food security in Uttar Pradesh. When rainfall fails, clinging stubbornly to normal long-duration Paddy (*धान*) or Maize (*मक्का*) schedules results in total crop loss and wasted investment. Based on **ICAR-CRIDA District Agriculture Contingency Plans**, farmers must execute disciplined **contingency crop substitutions** (switching to Pearl Millet, Sorghum, Sesame, Short-duration Pulses, or Toria) and implement in-situ soil moisture conservation and foliar anti-transpirant sprays.

---

## Contingency Strategy 2: Mid-Season Dry Spells (Vegetative / Tillering Break)

When rainfall stops for 15 to 25 consecutive days after initial crop germination:

### 1. Inter-Row Soil Mulching & Weed Scraping
- Weeds consume 2 to 3 times more water per kg of biomass than cultivated crops.
- Run a wheel-hoe or animal-drawn blade harrow (*दौड़ा*) between crop rows:
  - Eliminates all competing weeds immediately.
  - Creates a **2–3 cm loose soil dust mulch (*धूल पलवार*)** that breaks soil capillary pores, preventing deep soil moisture evaporation.

### 2. Foliar Anti-Transpirant & Osmoprotectant Sprays
Under acute water stress, plant stomata remain open during midday heat, causing severe turgor collapse. Spray scientific physiological shields:
- **Reflective Anti-Transpirant**:
  - Spray **Kaolin (China Clay) @ 5% (50 g per liter of water)**. Leaves develop a temporary white film that reflects 25–30% of incident solar radiation, lowering leaf canopy temperature by 2–3°C and cutting transpiration loss by 35%.
- **Osmotic Regulator**:
  - Spray **Potassium Nitrate ($KNO_3$) @ 1.0% (10 g/L water)** OR **Urea @ 2.0% (20 g/L water)**. Potassium acts as an osmotic pump, maintaining cellular turgidity and preventing premature leaf drop.

### 3. Life-Saving Micro-Irrigation (*जीवन रक्षक सिंचाई*)
- If canal or tube-well water is severely restricted:
- Never attempt broad surface flood irrigation over the entire plot.
- Provide **protective micro-sprinkler or alternate furrow irrigation** applying only **25 to 30 mm depth** targeted specifically at critical reproductive nodes (flowering or pod/earhead initiation).

## Contingency Strategy 3: Early Withdrawal of Monsoon (Terminal Drought)

When monsoon rains cease abruptly in early September (20–30 days before normal crop maturity):
1. **Selective Thinning (*पौधों की छंटाई*)**:
   - Manually uproot every third plant row or remove weak, non-productive side tillers in maize, pearl millet, and sorghum. Use thinned biomass as green cattle fodder. This reserves 100% of remaining soil moisture for the primary grain earheads.
2. **Supplemental Foliar Potassium**:
   - Spray **Sulphate of Potash (SOP 0:0:50) @ 1.0% (10 g/L)** during early grain filling to accelerate sucrose translocation from stems into the grain, preventing shrivelled seeds.
3. **Harvesting at Physiological Maturity**:
   - Harvest crops as soon as grains reach physiological maturity (formation of black abscission layer at grain base), rather than waiting for field stalks to dry. Stalks can be dried in bundles outside the field.
4. **Conserving Residual Moisture for Early Rabi**:
   - Immediately plow the harvested field using a planked cultivator (*पाटा लगाकर जुताई*) to seal residual moisture.
   - Sow **Toria (*तोरिया*)** or early **Mustard (*सरसों*)** within 48 hours to capitalize on remaining subsoil moisture.

## Zone-Specific Contingency Recommendations (Uttar Pradesh)

### 1. Bundelkhand Zone (Jhansi, Banda, Lalitpur, Hamirpur, Mahoba, Jalaun):
- Highly vulnerable to terminal drought due to shallow red soils (*Rakar*) and cracking black soils (*Mar/Kabar*).
- *Preferred Contingency*: Replace Paddy with **Pearl Millet (HHB-67 Improved)** + **Pigeonpea (UPAS-120)** intercropping (4:2 ratio) on Broad Bed Furrows. In late August, prioritize **Sesame (Pragati)** or **Toria (Type-9)**.

### 2. Eastern Plain Zone (Varanasi, Gorakhpur, Azamgarh, Mirzapur):
- In low-lying flood-prone areas, prepare for flash-flood submergence by planting submergence-tolerant rice cultivars (**Swarna Sub-1, Sambha Sub-1**).
- For delayed monsoon on uplands, replace rice with **Blackgram (Pant U-31)** or **Sesame**.
