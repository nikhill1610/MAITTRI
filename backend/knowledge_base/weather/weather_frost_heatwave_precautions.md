# Weather Precautions: Frost, Cold Wave & Heat Stress (मौसम संबंधी सावधानियां: पाला व लू)

## Frost & Cold Wave Management (पाले से फसलों का बचाव)
Ground frost occurs in Northern & Central India when night temperatures drop below 4°C accompanied by clear skies and zero wind velocity.
- **Vulnerable Crops**: Mustard, Potato, Tomato, Green Pea, Brinjal, and young fruit orchards.
- **Protection Measures**:
  1. **Light Evening Irrigation (हल्की सिंचाई)**: Water has a high specific heat capacity. Irrigating field borders and standing crops in the late evening releases heat overnight, raising the immediate microclimate temperature around plants by 1°C to 2°C, which is often enough to prevent sap freezing.
  2. **Smoke Screens / Smudging (धुआं करना)**: Burn dry leaves, weeds, or damp straw along the upwind borders (usually North/North-West) around midnight. The dense smoke blanket traps outgoing terrestrial longwave radiation.
  3. **Chemical Spray (Dilute Sulphuric Acid)**: Spray 0.1% Sulphuric Acid (1 ml concentrated H2SO4 per litre of water = 200 ml in 200L water/acre) or Soluble Sulphur (80% WDG @ 2g/litre). This lowers freezing point of cell sap and strengthens cell membranes.
  4. **Thatched Covers (टाटी या तिरपाल)**: Protect nursery seedlings and high-value vegetables with polythene or straw thatched sheets oriented towards the South-East.

## Terminal Heat Stress in Wheat (गेहूं में पछुआ हवा व तापमान वृद्धि)
Sudden temperature spikes (>30°C) during February-March during flowering and milking cause premature forced maturity, resulting in shriveled grains and 15-25% yield loss.
- **Preventive Steps**:
  1. **Light & Frequent Irrigation**: Irrigate at heading and milking stages. Avoid irrigating on windy days to prevent lodging.
  2. **Micro-Sprinklers**: Running sprinklers for 30 minutes in the afternoon creates an evaporative cooling effect, reducing canopy temperature by 3°C to 5°C.
  3. **Foliar Anti-Stress Spray**: Spray Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 1% (10g/L) or Salicylic Acid @ 100 ppm or 2% Urea at boot stage and flowering to enhance plant thermo-tolerance.
