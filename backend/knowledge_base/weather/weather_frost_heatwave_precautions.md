# Agro-Meteorological Crop Precautions: Frost, Cold Wave, Terminal Heat & Unseasonal Weather (मौसम संबंधी आपदा प्रबंधन)

## Farmer-friendly summary
Extreme weather events—such as ground frost (*पाला*) and severe cold waves in winter (December–January), followed by sudden terminal heat spikes (*पछुआ हवा व असामयिक गर्मी*) in late Rabi (February–March), and unseasonal hailstorms—pose critical yield threats to northern Indian crops. Ground frost occurs when calm night air drops below 4°C, freezing water inside plant vascular tissues. In contrast, terminal heat forces premature ripening in wheat, shrinking grain size and slashing yields by 15–25%. Implementing proactive, evidence-based agronomic interventions—such as **light evening irrigations**, **foliar anti-stress potassium sprays**, and **rapid surface drainage post-storm**—protects crop investments and stabilizes farm income.

---

## 1. Ground Frost & Cold Wave Management (पाले व शीतलहर से बचाव)

### A. Meteorological Triggers & Vulnerability
- **Trigger**: Occurs primarily between December 20 and January 31 when night temperatures drop below **4.0°C** accompanied by clear, cloudless skies, calm winds, and high relative humidity followed by dry north-westerly Himalayan air masses.
- **Vulnerable Crops**: 
  - **Mustard** (flowering and young pod filling stage—blisters and abortion).
  - **Potato** (foliage blackening, necrosis, and arrested tuberization).
  - **Tomato & Brinjal** (leaf scorching, flower drop, and fruit cracking).
  - **Green Pea & Gram** (pod abortion).

### B. High-Impact Protective Interventions

#### 1. Light Evening Irrigation (*हल्की शाम की सिंचाई*)
- **Scientific Mechanism**: Water has an exceptionally high specific heat capacity. Irrigating field margins or providing a light surface wetting in the late evening (after 4:00 PM) releases latent heat throughout the night.
- **Effect**: Raises canopy microclimate temperature by **1.0°C to 2.0°C**, preventing cellular water from freezing into sharp crystalline ice needles that puncture cell membranes.

#### 2. Smudging / Smoke Screens (*धुआं करना*)
- **Procedure**: Burn piles of damp straw, weed residue, or crop waste mixed with moist cattle dung along the field borders on the upwind side (typically North or North-West) between 12:00 midnight and 4:00 AM.
- **Effect**: Generates a thick, lingering smoke blanket over the crop canopy that reflects and traps outgoing terrestrial longwave infrared radiation, halting further ground cooling.

#### 3. Prophylactic Chemical Anti-Frost Sprays
- **Soluble Sulphur**: Spray Soluble Sulphur (80% WDG) @ **2.0 g/litre** of water (400 g in 200 L water per acre). Sulphur increases cell sap osmotic concentration, lowering its freezing threshold while imparting systemic fungal protection.
- **Dilute Sulphuric Acid**: In severe alert regions, spray **0.1% Sulphuric Acid ($H_2SO_4$)** (1 ml concentrated commercial acid carefully poured into 1 litre of water; i.e., 200 ml in 200 litres of water per acre). Apply 2–3 days prior to forecasted severe cold waves.

#### 4. Physical Nursery Thatching (*टाटी या तिरपाल का आवरण*)
- Cover vegetable nurseries, seedbeds, and young fruit grafts with straw mats (*tati*) or transparent polythene sheets tilted facing South-East. Uncover in the morning once sunshine warms the air.

## 2. Terminal Heat Stress in Wheat (गेहूं में पछुआ हवा व तापमान वृद्धि)

### A. Meteorological Triggers & Agronomic Impact
- **Trigger**: Sudden rise in daytime temperatures above **30°C to 32°C** during late February to mid-March, aggravated by dry, high-velocity westerly winds (*पछुआ हवा*).
- **Vulnerable Stage**: Wheat booting, anthesis, heading, and milky grain-filling (*दूधिया अवस्था*).
- **Damage**: High thermal stress accelerates grain respiration, truncates the grain-filling duration by 7–12 days, and causes forced premature maturity, resulting in shriveled grains (*बारीक व सिकुड़े दाने*), reduced test weight, and **15% to 25% yield loss**.

### B. Field-Level Heat Mitigation Protocols

#### 1. Light & Frequent Irrigation at Milking / Dough Stage
- Provide a light irrigation at heading and milking.
- **CRITICAL WARNING**: **Never apply heavy flood irrigation on windy days.** Saturated soil combined with gusty winds triggers catastrophic wheat lodging (*फसल का गिरना*), causing complete ruin of grain quality. Irrigate during calm evening hours.

#### 2. Afternoon Micro-Sprinkler Evaporative Cooling
- Where portable sprinklers or rain-guns are installed, operate them for **25–35 minutes during peak afternoon hours (12:30 PM to 2:30 PM)**.
- **Impact**: Rapid evaporative cooling lowers canopy temperature by **3.0°C to 5.0°C**, preventing pollen sterility and thermal shock.

#### 3. Foliar Anti-Stress Spraying
- **Potassium Nitrate ($KNO_3$ / 13:0:45)**: Spray @ **1.0%** (10 g per litre of water; i.e., 2 kg in 200 litres of water per acre) at the boot leaf stage and repeat at complete heading. Potassium regulates stomatal conductance, maintains cell turgor, and accelerates carbohydrate translocation into filling grains.
- **Foliar Urea Alternative**: Alternatively, spray **2.0% Urea** (4 kg in 200 L water/acre) to replenish nitrogen reserves and delay flag leaf senescence.

## 3. Unseasonal Rain, Hailstorms & Severe Storms (असामयिक वर्षा, ओलावृष्टि व आंधी)

### A. Immediate Post-Storm Field Rescue Protocol
1. **Rapid Surface Drainage (*अतिशीघ्र जल निकासी*)**: Standing water in wheat, mustard, or vegetable fields must be drained within **12 to 24 hours**. Waterlogged roots suffer rapid asphyxiation and root rots.
2. **Management of Lodged Wheat**:
   - **Do NOT attempt to erect lodged wheat manually.** Manual disturbance tears fragile root zones and breaks stems. Allow the plants to adjust their upper nodes naturally towards sunlight.
   - Once the soil surface dries enough to support foot traffic, apply a foliar spray of **1% Urea + 1% Potassium Nitrate** to revitalize nutrient uptake.
3. **Prophylactic Fungicide Coverage on Hail-Wounded Canopies**:
   - Hailstones inflict physical tearing and bruising on leaves and stems, serving as open entry wounds for secondary pathogens (bacterial soft rots, fungal blights).
   - Spray **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L** or **Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L** within 48 hours of hailstorm cessation to seal tissue wounds.

### B. Mandatory Crop Insurance Action
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Under PMFBY guidelines, localized disaster damage (Hailstorm / Inundation) **must be formally intimated within 72 hours** through the *Crop Insurance* App, portal `pmfby.gov.in`, or toll-free helpline **14447** / **1800-180-1551**.

## 4. Official Agro-Meteorological Advisory Channels

Farmers and field agents should monitor real-time weather advisories to plan irrigation and chemical applications:

- **IMD Agromet Advisory Service (AAS)**: Bi-weekly district-level agromet bulletins issued every Tuesday and Friday ([mausam.imd.gov.in](https://mausam.imd.gov.in)).
- **Meghdoot Mobile App**: Jointly developed by IMD, IITM, and ICAR; provides location-specific weather forecasts and crop advisory in Hindi.
- **Damini Mobile App**: Real-time lightning warnings with 15–30 minute advance alert radius.
- **National Kisan Call Centre**: **1800-180-1551** (Toll-Free, 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM).
